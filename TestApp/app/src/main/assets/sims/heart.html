🩸 Blood Flow & Vessels
Arteries carry blood away from the heart (high pressure).
Capillaries are microscopic vessels where oxygen and nutrients are exchanged with tissues.
Veins carry blood back to the heart (contain valves to prevent backflow).
Pathway: Right heart → pulmonary artery → lungs → pulmonary veins → left heart → aorta → body → vena cava → back.
