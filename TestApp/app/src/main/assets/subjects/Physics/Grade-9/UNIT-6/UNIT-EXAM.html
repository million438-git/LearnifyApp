Instructions

Attempt every question before revealing the answer guide. Show working for calculations and include units.

  1. A source completes 30 oscillations in 15 s. Find frequency and period.

    Show answer guide

    f = 30 ÷ 15 = 2 Hz; T = 1/2 = 0.5 s.

  2. A wave travels at 24 m/s and has frequency 6 Hz. Find wavelength.

    Show answer guide

    λ = v ÷ f = 24 ÷ 6 = 4 m.

  3. Why must the echo travel time be divided by two when finding distance to a wall?

    Show answer guide

    The measured time includes both the journey to the wall and the return journey.
