Exam technique

  • Read the question carefully and identify what is asked.
  • Write the relevant equation before substituting.
  • Use consistent units and show working.
  • Draw and label a diagram when it clarifies the problem.
  • Check whether the answer is reasonable.

Use safe, teacher-approved demonstrations. Never experiment with mains electricity or unsafe equipment.
