Temperature and Thermometers

Unit 7: Temperature and Thermometry

Learning objectives

  • Explain the main concepts in your own words.
  • Use relevant scientific vocabulary correctly.
  • Apply the concepts to a practical or numerical example.

Lesson notes

Temperature describes the thermal state of an object. Heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference. Net spontaneous heat transfer is from hotter to cooler objects.

Thermometers use properties that change with temperature. Read the scale at eye level, wait for a stable reading, and use a thermometer suitable for the temperature range. Celsius and kelvin are common temperature scales.

Key relationship

T(K) = T(°C) + 273.15

Worked example

Convert 25 °C to kelvin: 25 + 273.15 = 298.15 K.

Try it yourself

Convert 30 °C to kelvin.

Reveal suggested answer

30 + 273.15 = 303.15 K.
