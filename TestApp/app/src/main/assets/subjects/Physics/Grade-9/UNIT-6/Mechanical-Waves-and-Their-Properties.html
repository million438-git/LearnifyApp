Mechanical Waves and Their Properties

Unit 6: Mechanical Oscillation and Sound Waves

Learning objectives

Lesson notes

Waves transfer energy without transporting matter overall from source to receiver. In a transverse wave, oscillations are perpendicular to travel; in a longitudinal wave, oscillations are parallel to travel.

Wavelength is the distance between corresponding points on adjacent cycles. Frequency is cycles per second, amplitude is maximum displacement, and wave speed relates frequency to wavelength.

Key relationship

wave speed v = frequency f × wavelength λ

Worked example

A wave has frequency 5 Hz and wavelength 2 m. Speed = 5 × 2 = 10 m/s.

Try it yourself

A wave travels at 24 m/s and has frequency 6 Hz. Find wavelength.

Reveal suggested answer

λ = v ÷ f = 24 ÷ 6 = 4 m.
