Friction, Work and Energy

Unit 4: Force, Work, Energy and Power

Learning objectives

  • Explain the main concepts in your own words.
  • Use relevant scientific vocabulary correctly.
  • Apply the concepts to a practical or numerical example.

Lesson notes

Friction opposes relative motion or attempted sliding between surfaces. It can provide grip for walking and braking, but can also cause wear and heating.

In physics, work is done when a force causes displacement. Kinetic energy is associated with motion; gravitational potential energy is associated with position in a gravitational field. Energy can be transferred between stores while the total energy of a closed system is conserved.

Key relationship

work W = F × s when force is parallel to displacement; KE = ½mv²; GPE = mgh

Worked example

A 15 N force moves a box 3 m in the same direction. Work = 15 × 3 = 45 J.

Try it yourself

Calculate the kinetic energy of a 2 kg object moving at 4 m/s.

Reveal suggested answer

KE = ½ × 2 × 4² = 16 J.
