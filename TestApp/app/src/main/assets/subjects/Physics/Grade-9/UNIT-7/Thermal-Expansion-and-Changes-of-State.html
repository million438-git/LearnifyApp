Thermal Expansion and Changes of State

Unit 7: Temperature and Thermometry

Learning objectives

  • Explain the main concepts in your own words.
  • Use relevant scientific vocabulary correctly.
  • Apply the concepts to a practical or numerical example.

Lesson notes

Many substances expand when heated and contract when cooled. Expansion gaps in bridges and other structures allow materials to change size with temperature. Different materials may expand by different amounts.

Melting changes a solid to a liquid; freezing changes liquid to solid; evaporation and boiling change liquid to gas; condensation changes gas to liquid. During a change of state, energy may change particle arrangement while temperature remains constant for a pure substance at constant pressure.

Key relationship

heating often increases particle motion and can increase average particle separation

Worked example

Expansion joints in a bridge reduce stress when the bridge warms and its materials expand.

Try it yourself

Why are expansion gaps used in bridges?

Reveal suggested answer

They allow thermal expansion and contraction, reducing stress and the risk of damage.
