Branches and Applications of Physics

Unit 1: Physics and Human Society

Learning objectives

  • Explain the main concepts in your own words.
  • Use relevant scientific vocabulary correctly.
  • Apply the concepts to a practical or numerical example.

Lesson notes

Mechanics studies motion and forces. Optics studies light. Acoustics studies sound. Thermodynamics studies heat and energy transfer. Electromagnetism studies electric and magnetic effects. Nuclear physics investigates atomic nuclei, while astrophysics applies physics to stars and other astronomical objects.

Physics connects with chemistry through atoms and energy, with biology through movement and hearing, with geology through earthquakes, and with engineering through the design of safe structures and machines.

Key relationship

Choose the branch according to the phenomenon being investigated.

Worked example

Investigating an echo belongs mainly to acoustics; investigating a lens belongs to optics; analysing a moving vehicle belongs to mechanics.

Try it yourself

Match a refrigerator, a musical instrument, and a telescope to relevant branches of physics.

Reveal suggested answer

Refrigerator: thermodynamics; musical instrument: acoustics; telescope: optics.
