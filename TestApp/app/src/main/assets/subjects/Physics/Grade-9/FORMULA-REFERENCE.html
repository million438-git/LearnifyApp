Physics Formula Reference

Equations, symbols, and revision support

Motion

Average speed = total distance ÷ total time

Average velocity = displacement ÷ time

Acceleration = (final velocity − initial velocity) ÷ time

Force and energy

F = ma

Work = force × displacement (when parallel)

Kinetic energy = ½mv²

Gravitational potential energy = mgh

Power = work ÷ time

Machines

Mechanical advantage = load force ÷ effort force

Efficiency = useful output work ÷ input work × 100%

Oscillations and waves

Frequency = oscillations ÷ time

Period = 1 ÷ frequency

Wave speed = frequency × wavelength

Temperature

T(K) = T(°C) + 273.15

Q = mcΔT

Use only the equations taught in your class. Check the units and assumptions before substituting values.
