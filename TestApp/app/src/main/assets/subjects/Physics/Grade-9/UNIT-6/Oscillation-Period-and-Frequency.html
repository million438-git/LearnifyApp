Oscillation, Period and Frequency

Unit 6: Mechanical Oscillation and Sound Waves

Learning objectives

  • Explain the main concepts in your own words.
  • Use relevant scientific vocabulary correctly.
  • Apply the concepts to a practical or numerical example.

Lesson notes

An oscillation is repeated motion around an equilibrium position. Examples include a swinging pendulum for small angles and a mass attached to a spring. The period is the time for one complete oscillation; frequency is the number of oscillations each second.

Amplitude is the maximum displacement from equilibrium. Frequency is measured in hertz (Hz), and period is measured in seconds.

Key relationship

frequency f = number of oscillations ÷ time; period T = 1 ÷ f

Worked example

A pendulum completes 20 oscillations in 10 s. Frequency = 20 ÷ 10 = 2 Hz; period = 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 s.

Try it yourself

A source completes 30 oscillations in 15 s. Find frequency and period.

Reveal suggested answer

f = 30 ÷ 15 = 2 Hz; T = 1/2 = 0.5 s.
