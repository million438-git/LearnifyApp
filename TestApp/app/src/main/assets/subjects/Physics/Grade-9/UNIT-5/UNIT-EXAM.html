  1. Name three types of simple machine and give one use for each.

    Show answer guide

    Lever: crowbar; pulley: raising a flag; inclined plane: loading a box onto a truck.

  2. A lever raises a 300 N load using a 75 N effort. Find mechanical advantage.

    Show answer guide

    MA = 300 ÷ 75 = 4.

  3. A machine takes in 800 J and delivers 600 J of useful work. Find efficiency.

    Show answer guide

    600 ÷ 800 × 100% = 75%.
