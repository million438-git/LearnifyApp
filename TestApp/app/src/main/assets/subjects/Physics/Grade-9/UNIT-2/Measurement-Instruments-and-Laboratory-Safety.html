Measurement, Instruments and Laboratory Safety

Unit 2: Physical Quantities

Learning objectives

  • Explain the main concepts in your own words.
  • Use relevant scientific vocabulary correctly.
  • Apply the concepts to a practical or numerical example.

Lesson notes

Use a ruler or tape for length, a balance for mass, a stopwatch for time, and a thermometer for temperature. Choose an instrument with a suitable range and scale. Check the zero reading, look at scales at eye level, and repeat measurements when useful.

Accuracy means closeness to an accepted value; precision describes how closely repeated readings agree. Random errors cause variation, while systematic errors shift measurements consistently.

Keep work areas tidy, follow teacher instructions, handle glassware carefully, and never touch electrical mains equipment. Report damaged instruments or unsafe conditions.

Key relationship

mean = sum of measured values ÷ number of measurements

Worked example

Readings of 5.1 cm, 5.2 cm, and 5.0 cm have mean (5.1 + 5.2 + 5.0) ÷ 3 = 5.1 cm.

Try it yourself

Reveal suggested answer
