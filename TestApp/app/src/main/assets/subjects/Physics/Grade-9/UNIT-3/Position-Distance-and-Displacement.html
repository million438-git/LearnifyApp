Position, Distance and Displacement

Unit 3: Motion in a Straight Line

Learning objectives

  • Explain the main concepts in your own words.
  • Use relevant scientific vocabulary correctly.
  • Apply the concepts to a practical or numerical example.

Lesson notes

Position describes an object's location relative to a chosen origin. Distance is the total length of the path travelled. Displacement is the change from initial position to final position and includes direction.

Distance is scalar; displacement is vector. If a person walks away and returns to the starting point, the total distance is positive while the final displacement is zero.

Key relationship

displacement = final position − initial position

Worked example

A student moves from x = 2 m to x = 9 m. Displacement = 9 − 2 = +7 m.

Try it yourself

A student walks 10 m east and then 4 m west. Find distance and displacement.

Reveal suggested answer

Distance = 10 + 4 = 14 m. Displacement = 6 m east.
