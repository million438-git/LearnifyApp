Levers, Pulleys and Mechanical Advantage

Unit 5: Simple Machines

Lesson notes

A lever rotates around a pivot called the fulcrum. The effort is the applied force and the load is the resistance being moved. A pulley changes the direction of a force; a system of pulleys can also reduce the effort needed to lift a load.

Mechanical advantage compares load force with effort force. Actual performance depends on friction and the machine's design.

Key relationship

mechanical advantage MA = load force ÷ effort force

Worked example

If a machine lifts a 200 N load with a 50 N effort, MA = 200 ÷ 50 = 4.

Try it yourself

A lever raises a 300 N load using a 75 N effort. Find mechanical advantage.

Reveal suggested answer

MA = 300 ÷ 75 = 4.
