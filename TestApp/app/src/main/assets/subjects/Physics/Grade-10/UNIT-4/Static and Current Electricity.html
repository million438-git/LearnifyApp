Section 4.1

Charges in Nature

There are two types of electric charge: positive and negative. Protons carry positive charge; electrons carry negative charge. When equal amounts of positive and negative charge are present in a body, the body is neutral. If there is an imbalance (extra electrons or a deficit), the body is charged.

ሁለት ዓይነት የኤሌክትሪክ ቻርጅ አሉ፦ አዎንታዊና አሉታዊ። ፕሮቶን አዎንታዊ ቻርጅ አለው፤ ኤሌክትሮን ደግሞ አሉታዊ። ሁለቱ በእኩል መጠን ሲገኙ አካሉ ገለልተኛ (neutral) ይሆናል። አንደኛው ከሌላው ቢበዛ አካሉ ተሞልቷል (charged) ይባላል።

Key facts:
• Gain electrons → negatively charged. Lose electrons → positively charged.
• SI unit of charge: Coulomb (C). 1 C = 6.25 × 10¹⁸ electrons.
• Charge of one electron: e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
• 1 µC = 10⁻⁶ C; 1 nC = 10⁻⁹ C.
• Conservation of charge: total charge in an isolated system never changes.
• Quantization of charge: q = ne, where n is an integer (positive or negative). ቁልፍ እውነታዎች፦ ኤሌክትሮን ማግኘት → አሉታዊ። ማጣት → አዎንታዊ። የ SI አሃድ ኩሎምብ (C)። e = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C። የቻርጅ ጥበቃ ህግ። ቻርጅ በ e ብዜት ብቻ ይመጣል (q = ne)።

Formulas

Charge quantization: q = n · e   (n = integer)
e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C (elementary charge)
1 C = 6.25 × 10¹⁸ electrons

Diagram: Two Types of Charge

Worked Examples

Example 4.1.1 — Number of electrons in a given charge

A body has a charge of −3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹ C. How many excess electrons does it carry?

አንድ አካል −3.2×10⁻¹⁹ C ቻርጅ አለው። ስንት ተጨማሪ ኤሌክትሮኖች አሉት?

  1. Given: q = −3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹ C; e = −1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C per electron.
  2. Formula: q = ne → n = q/e.
  3. Compute: n = (−3.2×10⁻¹⁹) / (−1.6×10⁻¹⁹) = 2.
  4. Answer: 2 excess electrons (negative charge = excess electrons).
Final: 2 excess electrons. · መልስ፦ 2 ተጨማሪ ኤሌክትሮኖች።

Example 4.1.2 — Charge from a known number of electrons

How much negative charge is carried by 5 × 10¹⁰ electrons?

5×10¹⁰ ኤሌክትሮኖች ስንት አሉታዊ ቻርጅ ይይዛሉ?

  1. Given: n = 5 × 10¹⁰; e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
  2. Formula: q = ne.
  3. Compute: q = (5×10¹⁰)(1.6×10⁻¹⁹) = 8 × 10⁻⁹ C.
  4. Answer: −8 × 10⁻⁹ C = −8 nC.
Final: q = −8 × 10⁻⁹ C = −8 nC. · መልስ፦ −8 nC።

አንድ አካል ኤሌክትሮን ሲያጣ (ወይም ፕሮቶን ሲያገኝ) አዎንታዊ ቻርጅ ይይዛል።

Exercise 4.2 — Conductor with charge +3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹ C. Excess/deficit electrons?

Solution: Charge is positive → deficit of electrons. n = q/e = 3.2×10⁻¹⁹ / 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ = 2 electrons deficit.

መልስ፦ 2 ኤሌክትሮኖች ጎድለዋል (deficit)።

Review 4.1.1 — Types of charges in nature?

Two types: positive (protons) and negative (electrons).

መልስ፦ ሁለት ዓይነት — አዎንታዊና አሉታዊ።

Review 4.1.2 — When is a body negatively charged?

When it has gained electrons — the number of electrons exceeds the number of protons.

መልስ፦ ኤሌክትሮን ሲያገኝ — የኤሌክትሮኖች ቁጥር ከፕሮቶኖች ሲበዛ።

Review 4.1.3 — What does the law of conservation of charge say?

The total electric charge in an isolated system is constant — charge is neither created nor destroyed, only transferred.

መልስ፦ በተናጠል ስርዓት ውስጥ አጠቃላይ ቻርጅ ቋሚ ነው — አይፈጠርም፣ አይጠፋም፤ ይተላለፍ ብቻ።

Review 4.1.4 — What is quantization of charge?

Charge exists only in integral multiples of the elementary charge e: q = ne, where n is an integer.

መልስ፦ ቻርጅ በ e ብዜት ብቻ ይመጣል — q = ne።

Review 4.1.5 — Properties of electric charges

1) Two types (positive, negative). 2) Like charges repel; unlike attract. 3) Quantized (q = ne). 4) Conserved (in isolated systems). 5) Charge resides on the surface of conductors. 6) Charges reside on conductors and are mobile.

1) ሁለት ዓይነት 2) ተመሳሳይ ይጣላሉ፣ ተቃራኒ ይሳባሉ 3) በ e ብዜት 4) ይጠበቃል 5) በኮንዳክተር ላይ ብቻ 6) በኮንዳክተር ላይ ይንቀሳቀሳሉ።

Section 4.2

Methods of Charging a Body

Charging is the process of adding or removing electric charge from a body. There are three methods:

ቻርጅ ማድረግ ማለት ቻርጅ መጨመር ወይም መቀነስ ነው። ሦስት ዘዴዎች አሉ፦

Three methods:
1. Rubbing (friction): Two different neutral materials are rubbed together. Electrons transfer from one to the other, giving opposite charges.
2. Conduction (contact): A charged object touches a neutral object. They end up with the same sign of charge, but weaker.
3. Induction: A charged object is brought near (but does not touch) a neutral conductor that is temporarily grounded. They end up with opposite signs. ሦስት ዘዴዎች፦ 1) በመፋታት፦ ሁለት ገለልተኛ ነገሮች ሲፋቱ — ተቃራኒ ቻርጆች። 2) በመንካት (conduction)፦ የተሞላ ነገር ገለልተኛ ነገርን ሲነካ — ተመሳሳይ ቻርጅ ግን ደካማ። 3) በመግቢያ (induction)፦ የተሞላ ነገር ሳይነካ ሲቃረብ — ተቃራኒ ቻርጅ።

Diagram: Three Methods of Charging

Worked Examples

Example 4.2.1 — Charging by rubbing (comb and hair)

A neutral comb is rubbed with neutral hair. After rubbing, the comb attracts small paper pieces. Explain.

ገለልተኛ ማበጠሪያ (comb) በጸጉር ተፋትቶ ትንንሽ ወረቀቶችን ይስባል። ለምን?

  1. Before rubbing: Both comb and hair are neutral (equal protons and electrons).
  2. Rubbing: Electrons transfer from hair atoms to comb atoms (some materials hold electrons more tightly).
  3. After rubbing: Comb has extra electrons → negatively charged. Hair has lost electrons → positively charged.
  4. Effect: The charged comb attracts neutral paper pieces by induction (polarizing them).
Final: Comb −ve, hair +ve. · መልስ፦ ማበጠሪያው −፣ ጸጉሩ +።

Example 4.2.2 — Charging by conduction (contact)

A +5 µC charged metal sphere touches a neutral metal sphere, then is separated. What happens?

+5 µC የተሞላ የብረት ኳስ ገለልተኛ ኳስን ተነክቶ ሲለያይ ምን ይሆናል?

  1. Contact: Electrons flow from the neutral sphere to the positively charged sphere.
  2. Result: The excess positive charge is shared between both spheres.
  3. After separation: Each sphere has +2.5 µC (assuming identical spheres).
  4. Both are positive — same sign, weaker than original.
Final: Both spheres +2.5 µC. · መልስ፦ ሁለቱም +2.5 µC።

Example 4.2.3 — Charging by induction

A negatively charged ebonite rod is brought near a neutral metal sphere (no contact). Describe the sphere's charges.

አሉታዊ የተሞላ የኢቦናይት ዘንግ ሳይነካ ወደ ገለልተኛ የብረት ኳስ ሲቀርብ ምን ይሆናል?

  1. Bring rod near: Free electrons in the metal sphere are repelled to the far side.
  2. Near side: Becomes positive (electron-deficient). Far side: Becomes negative (excess electrons).
  3. Ground the far side: Electrons flow to Earth.
  4. Remove ground, remove rod: Sphere is left with net positive charge.
Final: Sphere net +ve (opposite to rod). · መልስ፦ ኳሱ +፣ ከዘንጉ ተቃራኒ።

Review 4.2.1 — State the three methods of charging a body

1) Charging by rubbing (friction). 2) Charging by conduction (contact). 3) Charging by induction.

መልስ፦ 1) በመፋታት 2) በመንካት 3) በመግቢያ።

Review 4.2.2 — Describe charging by contact and rubbing

By contact: A charged object touches a neutral one; electrons flow to equalize potential; both end up with the same sign of charge (weaker than the original).

By rubbing: Two different neutral materials are rubbed; electrons transfer from the material with weaker electron hold to the stronger one; the two objects end up with opposite charges.

Section 4.3

The Electroscope

An electroscope is a sensitive instrument used to detect the presence of electric charge, identify its type, compare magnitudes, and determine if a material is a conductor or insulator.

ኤሌክትሮስኮፕ ቻርጅ መኖሩን፣ ዓይነቱንና መጠኑን ለመለየት የሚያገለግል መሳሪያ ነው።

Structure: Glass jar + metal rod + metal plate (top) + two thin gold leaves (bottom).
How it works: When a charged object is brought near the metal plate, charges are redistributed. The gold leaves repel each other (like charges) and diverge. More charge → more divergence. አወቃቀር፦ የመስታወት ማሰሮ + የብረት ዘንግ + የብረት ሰሌዳ + ሁለት ቀጭን የወርቅ ቅጠሎች። ቻርጅ ሲቃረብ ቅጠሎቹ ይለያያሉ።

Diagram: Electroscope

Worked Examples

Example 4.3.1 — Detecting charge with an electroscope

A positively charged rod is brought near (not touching) the neutral electroscope's plate. Describe what happens.

አዎንታዊ የተሞላ ዘንግ ሳይነካ ወደ ገለልተኛ ኤሌክትሮስኮፕ ሲቀርብ ምን ይሆናል?

  1. Neutral electroscope: Leaves hang down (closed).
  2. Bring +ve rod near: Electrons attracted from leaves & rod up to the plate.
  3. Leaves become +ve (electron-deficient). Plate becomes −ve (excess electrons).
  4. Like charges repel: The two +ve leaves repel each other → they diverge.
  5. Remove rod: Charges return to even distribution; leaves fall back together.
Final: Leaves diverge while rod is near; collapse when removed. · መልስ፦ ዘንጉ ሲቃረብ ቅጠሎቹ ይለያያሉ፤ ሲወገድ ይመለሳሉ።

Example 4.3.2 — Identifying the charge sign

A negatively charged rod is brought near an electroscope that is already positively charged. Describe what happens.

አሉታዊ የተሞላ ዘንግ አስቀድሞ አዎንታዊ ወደ ሆነ ኤሌክትሮስኮፕ ሲቀርብ ምን ይሆናል?

  1. Electroscope is +ve: Leaves are diverged (both +ve).
  2. Bring −ve rod near: Electrons attracted to plate; more +ve charge pushed to leaves.
  3. Result: Leaves diverge more (more +ve charges on them).
  4. If rod were +ve instead: Leaves would collapse slightly (positive charge pulled up to plate).
Final: More divergence → rod is −ve (opposite to electroscope). · መልስ፦ ቅጠሎቹ በበለጠ ሲለያዩ ዘንጉ − ነው።

መልስ፦ ቻርጅ መኖሩን፣ ዓይነቱንና መጠኑን ለመለየት፤ እንዲሁም ኮንዳክተር/ኢንሱሌተር መሆኑን ለመፈተሽ።

If the leaves are diverged (spread apart), the electroscope carries net charge. If leaves hang together (collapsed), it is neutral.

መልስ፦ ቅጠሎቹ ከተለያዩ ቻርጅ አለው፤ ካልተለያዩ ገለልተኛ ነው።

Section 4.5

Coulomb's Law of Electrostatics

Coulomb's law gives the magnitude of the electrostatic force between two point charges. The force is directly proportional to the product of the charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.

የኩሎምብ ህግ በሁለት ነጥብ ቻርጆች መካከል ያለውን የኤሌክትሮስታቲክ ኃይል ይሰጣል። ኃይሉ ከቻርጆቹ ብዜት ጋር ቀጥተኛ፣ ከርቀታቸው ካሬ ጋር ተቃራኒ ነው።

Formulas

F = k · |q₁ · q₂| / r²
k = 1/(4πε₀) ≈ 9 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²
ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²) (permittivity of free space)
Direction: like charges repel; unlike attract (along the line joining them)

Diagram: Coulomb Forces

Worked Examples

Example 4.5.1 — Force between two charges (matric exam)

q₁ = 5.0 µC and q₂ = −12.0 µC, separated by 30 cm. Find the magnitude of the force.

q₁ = 5.0 µC እና q₂ = −12.0 µC በ30 ሴ.ሜ ርቀት። የኃይሉን መጠን ፈልግ።

  1. Convert: q₁ = 5 × 10⁻⁶ C; q₂ = −12 × 10⁻⁶ C; r = 0.30 m.
  2. Formula: F = k |q₁ q₂| / r².
  3. Substitute: F = (9×10⁹)(5×10⁻⁶)(12×10⁻⁶) / (0.30)².
  4. Compute numerator: 9 × 5 × 12 × 10⁻³ = 540 × 10⁻³ = 0.54.
  5. Divide: 0.54 / 0.09 = 6.0 N.
  6. Direction: Opposite signs → attractive force.
Final: F = 6.0 N (attractive). · መልስ፦ 6.0 N፣ የመሳሳብ ኃይል።

Example 4.5.2 — Force with microcoulomb charges

q₁ = 2 µC and q₂ = −4 µC, 20 cm apart. Find magnitude and direction of the force.

q₁ = 2 µC፣ q₂ = −4 µC፣ 20 ሴ.ሜ ርቀት። መጠንና አቅጣጫ ፈልግ።

  1. Convert: q₁ = 2×10⁻⁶ C; q₂ = −4×10⁻⁶ C; r = 0.20 m.
  2. Formula: F = k |q₁ q₂| / r².
  3. Substitute: F = (9×10⁹)(2×10⁻⁶)(4×10⁻⁶) / (0.04).
  4. Numerator: 72 × 10⁻³ = 0.072.
  5. Divide: 0.072 / 0.04 = 1.8 N.
  6. Direction: Opposite signs → attractive.
Final: F = 1.8 N attractive. · መልስ፦ 1.8 N፣ የመሳሳብ።

Example 4.5.3 — Finding distance for a given force

Two equal charges of 1.1 × 10⁻⁷ C experience a force of 4.2 × 10⁴ N. How far apart are they?

ሁለት እኩል 1.1×10⁻⁷ C ቻርጆች 4.2×10⁴ N ኃይል ይሰማቸዋል። ርቀታቸው ስንት ነው?

  1. Given: q₁ = q₂ = 1.1×10⁻⁷ C; F = 4.2×10⁴ N.
  2. Rearrange: r = √(k |q₁ q₂| / F).
  3. Numerator: (9×10⁹)(1.1×10⁻⁷)² = 9×10⁹ × 1.21×10⁻¹⁴ = 1.089×10⁻⁴.
  4. Divide: 1.089×10⁻⁴ / 4.2×10⁴ = 2.59×10⁻⁹.
  5. Square root: r ≈ 5.09×10⁻⁵ m = 0.051 mm.
Final: r ≈ 5.1 × 10⁻⁵ m. · መልስ፦ 5.1×10⁻⁵ ሜትር።

Textbook Exercises

Review 4.5.1 — Two charges 1 C and −4 C. Direction of force?

Opposite signs → attractive force (toward each other along the joining line).

መልስ፦ የመሳሳብ (attractive)።

Review 4.5.2 — q₁ = 2×10⁻⁶ C and q₂ = −4×10⁻³ C, 30 cm apart. Force?

Step 1: r = 0.30 m. Step 2: F = (9×10⁹)(2×10⁻⁶)(4×10⁻³) / (0.30)².

Step 3: Numerator = 9×2×4×10⁰ = 72. Denominator = 0.09.

Step 4: F = 72 / 0.09 = 800 N attractive.

መልስ፦ 800 N፣ የመሳሳብ።

End-of-unit Q8 — q₁=2µC, q₂=−4µC, 20 cm apart. Force?

Solution: F = (9×10⁹)(2×10⁻⁶)(4×10⁻⁶) / (0.20)² = 0.072/0.04 = 1.8 N attractive.

መልስ፦ 1.8 N፣ የመሳሳብ።

End-of-unit Q9 — Two spheres 4.0 cm apart, F = 1.2×10⁹ N, one has twice the charge (opposite sign). Find charges.

Step 1: Let charges be q and −2q. |q₁q₂| = 2q².

Step 2: F = k(2q²)/r² → 1.2×10⁹ = (9×10⁹)(2q²)/(0.04)².

Step 3: 1.2×10⁹ × 0.0016 = 18×10⁹ q² → 1.92×10⁶ = 18×10⁹ q².

Step 4: q² = 1.067×10⁻⁴ → q ≈ 1.03×10⁻² C. So charges are ±1.03×10⁻² C and ∓2.06×10⁻² C.

መልስ፦ ±1.03×10⁻² C እና ∓2.06×10⁻² C።

End-of-unit Q10 — Two equal charges 1.1×10⁻⁷ C, F = 4.2×10⁴ N. Distance?

Solution: r = √(kq²/F) = √[(9×10⁹)(1.1×10⁻⁷)²/(4.2×10⁴)] = √(1.089×10⁻⁴/4.2×10⁴) = √(2.59×10⁻⁹) ≈ 5.09×10⁻⁵ m.

መልስ፦ 5.09×10⁻⁵ ሜትር።

Section 4.6

The Electric Field

An electric field is a region around a charge where another charge would experience a force. The field strength E is defined as force per unit charge on a positive test charge.

የኤሌክትሪክ መስክ በቻርጅ ዙሪያ ያለ አካባቢ ሲሆን ሌላ ቻርጅ ኃይል የሚሰማበት ነው። ጥንካሬው E በአንድ አሃድ ቻርጅ ላይ የሚሠራ ኃይል ነው።

Formulas

Electric field: E = F / q   [N/C]
Force from field: F = qE
Field of a point charge: E = kq / r²
Direction: radially outward from +ve charge; radially inward toward −ve charge

Diagram: Electric Field Lines

Properties of field lines

  • Never cross each other.
  • Perpendicular to the surface of a conductor.
  • Originate at +ve charges; terminate at −ve charges.
  • Number of lines ∝ magnitude of charge.
  • Close together where field is strong; far apart where weak.

Worked Examples

Example 4.6.1 — Electric field of a point charge (matric exam)

Calculate the field due to a 2.0 nC point charge at 5.0 mm away.

2.0 nC ቻርጅ በ5.0 ሚሜ ርቀት ላይ የሚፈጥረውን መስክ አስላ።

  1. Given: q = 2.0×10⁻⁹ C; r = 5.0×10⁻³ m.
  2. Formula: E = kq/r².
  3. Substitute: E = (9×10⁹)(2×10⁻⁹) / (5×10⁻³)².
  4. Numerator: 18. Denominator: 25×10⁻⁶.
  5. Compute: 18 / (25×10⁻⁶) = 7.2 × 10⁵ N/C.
  6. Direction: Away from the +ve charge.
Final: E = 7.2 × 10⁵ N/C, away from charge. · መልስ፦ 7.2×10⁵ N/C፣ ከቻርጁ ወደ ውጭ።

Example 4.6.2 — Force on a charge in a field

A 3.50 µC charge is placed in a 250 N/C field pointing east. Find magnitude and direction of the force.

3.50 µC ቻርጅ ወደ ምስራቅ 250 N/C መስክ ውስጥ ሲቀመጥ ኃይሉን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: q = 3.50×10⁻⁶ C; E = 250 N/C, east.
  2. Formula: F = qE.
  3. Compute: F = (3.50×10⁻⁶)(250) = 8.75×10⁻⁴ N.
  4. Direction: Positive charge → force in same direction as E → east.
Final: F = 8.75 × 10⁻⁴ N east. · መልስ፦ 8.75×10⁻⁴ N ወደ ምስራቅ።

Example 4.6.3 — Field at a distance

Find the field at 2.00 m from a 5.00 mC point charge.

ከ5.00 mC ቻርጅ 2.00 ሜትር ርቀት ላይ ያለውን መስክ ፈልግ።

  1. Given: q = 5.00×10⁻³ C; r = 2.00 m.
  2. Formula: E = kq/r².
  3. Substitute: E = (9×10⁹)(5×10⁻³) / 4.
  4. Compute: E = 45×10⁶ / 4 = 1.125×10⁷ N/C.
Final: E ≈ 1.13 × 10⁷ N/C outward. · መልስ፦ 1.13×10⁷ N/C፣ ወደ ውጭ።

Section 4.8

Current, Voltage, and Ohm's Law

Current (I) = rate of flow of charge. Voltage (V) = work per unit charge (potential difference). Resistance (R) = opposition to current flow. Ohm's law links them: V = IR.

አሁንታ (I) = የቻርጅ ፍሰት መጠን። ቮልቴጅ (V) = በአንድ አሃድ ቻርጅ የሚሠራ ሥራ። መቋቋሚያ (R) = የቻርጅ ፍሰትን የመቃወም ባህሪ። የኦህም ህግ፦ V = IR።

Formulas

Current: I = Q / t   [A = C/s]
Voltage: V = W / Q   [V = J/C]
Ohm's Law: V = I R   [Ω = V/A]
Resistance from material: R = ρ L / A   [Ω]

Worked Examples

Example 4.8.1 — Charge from current and time

A current of 0.5 A flows for 10 minutes. Find the total charge.

0.5 A ለ10 ደቂቃ ሲፈስ ጠቅላላ ቻርጁን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: I = 0.5 A; t = 10 min = 600 s.
  2. Formula: Q = I·t.
  3. Compute: Q = 0.5 × 600 = 300 C.
Final: Q = 300 C. · መልስ፦ 300 C።

Example 4.8.2 — Work to move a charge across a voltage

How much work moves 2 C across a 12 V potential difference?

2 C በ12 V ልዩነት ለማንቀሳቀስ ስንት ሥራ ያስፈልጋል?

  1. Given: Q = 2 C; V = 12 V.
  2. Formula: W = V·Q.
  3. Compute: W = 12 × 2 = 24 J.
Final: W = 24 J. · መልስ፦ 24 J።

Example 4.8.3 — Ohm's law (bulb example)

A bulb with resistance 1200 Ω is connected to a 220 V source. Find the current.

1200 Ω ቡልብ ከ220 V ምንጭ ጋር ሲገናኝ አሁንታውን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: V = 220 V; R = 1200 Ω.
  2. Formula: I = V/R.
  3. Compute: I = 220/1200 = 0.183 A.
Final: I ≈ 0.18 A. · መልስ፦ 0.18 A።

Example 4.8.4 — Heater at increased voltage (matric exam)

A heater draws 4 A at 60 V. What current does it draw at 120 V?

ማሞቂያ በ60 V 4 A ይስባል። በ120 V ስንት A ይስባል?

  1. Step 1 (find R): R = V/I = 60/4 = 15 Ω.
  2. Step 2 (new current): I = 120/15 = 8 A.
  3. Conclusion: Doubling voltage doubles current (for constant R).
Final: I = 8 A at 120 V. · መልስ፦ 8 A።

Example 4.8.5 — Resistivity calculation (matric exam)

A wire 1 m long, 0.3 mm diameter, has resistance 26 Ω at 20 °C. Find resistivity.

1 ሜ ርዝመት፣ 0.3 ሚሜ ዲያሜትር፣ 26 Ω መቋቋሚያ ያለው ሽቦ ጥግግት ፈልግ።

  1. Radius: r = 0.15 mm = 1.5×10⁻⁴ m.
  2. Area: A = πr² = 3.14 × 2.25×10⁻⁸ = 7.07×10⁻⁸ m².
  3. Formula: ρ = R·A/L.
  4. Compute: ρ = (26)(7.07×10⁻⁸) / 1 = 1.84×10⁻⁶ Ω·m.
Final: ρ ≈ 1.84 × 10⁻⁶ Ω·m (manganese). · መልስ፦ 1.84×10⁻⁶ Ω·m።

Section 4.9

Combination of Resistors

Series: One path — same current through all. Parallel: Multiple paths — same voltage across all.

ተከታታይ (Series)፦ አንድ መንገድ — ተመሳሳይ አሁንታ። ትይዩ (Parallel)፦ በርካታ መንገዶች — ተመሳሳይ ቮልቴጅ።

Formulas

Series: R_eq = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + … ; I same; V = V₁ + V₂ + …
Parallel: 1/R_eq = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ + … ; V same; I = I₁ + I₂ + …

Diagram: Series vs Parallel

Worked Examples

Example 4.9.1 — Series resistors (matric exam)

R₁=12 Ω, R₂=3 Ω, R₃=4 Ω, R₄=5 Ω in series with 12 V battery. Find R_eq and current.

R₁=12 Ω፣ R₂=3 Ω፣ R₃=4 Ω፣ R₄=5 Ω ተከታታይ በ12 V ባትሪ። R_eq እና አሁንታ ፈልግ።

  1. R_eq: 12 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 24 Ω.
  2. Current: I = V/R_eq = 12/24 = 0.50 A.
  3. Same current in all resistors: 0.50 A each.
Final: R_eq = 24 Ω; I = 0.50 A. · መልስ፦ R_eq = 24 Ω፤ I = 0.50 A።

Example 4.9.2 — Parallel resistors (matric exam)

R₁=12 Ω, R₂=12 Ω, R₃=6 Ω in parallel with 12 V source. Find R_eq and each branch current.

R₁=12 Ω፣ R₂=12 Ω፣ R₃=6 Ω ትይዩ በ12 V። R_eq እና የእያንዳንዱን አሁንታ ፈልግ።

  1. R_eq: 1/R_eq = 1/12 + 1/12 + 1/6 = 1/12 + 1/12 + 2/12 = 4/12.
  2. R_eq = 3 Ω.
  3. Total current: I = 12/3 = 4 A.
  4. Branch currents: I₁ = 12/12 = 1 A; I₂ = 1 A; I₃ = 12/6 = 2 A.
  5. Check: 1+1+2 = 4 A ✓
Final: R_eq = 3 Ω; I = 4 A; branches 1A, 1A, 2A. · መልስ፦ R_eq = 3 Ω፤ I = 4 A።

Example 4.9.3 — Series-parallel combination (matric exam)

R₁=4 Ω in series with a parallel combination of R₂=5 Ω and R₃=9 Ω. Voltage = 6 V. Find R_eq and current through R₁.

R₁=4 Ω ከ R₂=5 Ω እና R₃=9 Ω ትይዩ ጋር ተከታታይ። 6 V። R_eq እና አሁንታ ፈልግ።

  1. Parallel part: 1/R_p = 1/5 + 1/9 = 9/45 + 5/45 = 14/45.
  2. R_p = 45/14 ≈ 3.21 Ω.
  3. Total: R_eq = 4 + 3.21 = 7.21 Ω.
  4. Current: I = 6/7.21 = 0.83 A.
  5. Current through R₁ = total current (it's in series) = 0.83 A.
Final: R_eq = 7.21 Ω; I = 0.83 A. · መልስ፦ R_eq = 7.21 Ω፤ I = 0.83 A።

Section 4.11

Electrical Safety, Voltmeter & Ammeter

Voltmeter: measures voltage, connected in parallel with the component, high resistance. Ammeter: measures current, connected in series, low resistance.

ቮልትሜትር፦ ቮልቴጅ ይለካል፤ በትይዩ (parallel) ይገናኛል። አሚትር፦ አሁንታ ይለካል፤ በተከታታይ (series) ይገናኛል።

Worked Examples

Example 4.11.1 — Fuse and safety (matric exam)

Why is a fuse used in a circuit?

ለምን ፊውዝ (fuse) በወረዳ ውስጥ ያገለግላል?

  1. Fuse = thin metal wire with low melting point, inserted in series.
  2. Normal current: Fuse stays intact — no damage.
  3. Fault (short circuit): Current surges, fuse wire heats up and melts.
  4. Circuit breaks — protects appliances from damage.
Final: Fuse = sacrificial element that breaks the circuit at excessive current. · መልስ፦ ፊውዝ ከመጠን በላይ አሁንታ ሲፈስ ይቀልጦ ወረዳውን ያቋርጣል።

Example 4.11.2 — Grounding safety

How does a three-prong plug protect the user?

ሶስት እግር (three-prong) መሰኪያ ተጠቃሚውን እንዴት ይጠብቃል?

  1. Two wires: Live (220 V) + neutral (0 V) — power the device.
  2. Third wire: Case ground — connected to the metal case and to Earth.
  3. Fault scenario: If live touches case, current flows through ground wire (low resistance), not user.
  4. Result: Fuse/breaker trips; user safe.
Final: Third wire → safest path for fault current. · መልስ፦ ሶስተኛው ሽቦ ለተጠቃሚው ደህንነት አስተማማኝ መንገድ ይሰጣል።

Textbook Exercises

Review 4.11.1 — Factors determining damage from electric shock

1) Magnitude of current. 2) Path through the body (e.g., hand-to-hand crosses the heart). 3) Duration of exposure. 4) Frequency of the current. 5) Wet vs dry skin.

መልስ፦ የአሁንታ መጠን፣ በሰውነት ውስጥ የሚያልፈው መንገድ፣ የቆይታ ጊዜ፣ ድግግሞሽ፣ እርጥብ/ደረቅ ቆዳ።

Review 4.11.2 — Why dry hands before touching switches?

Water (especially with dissolved ions) is a good conductor. Wet skin has much lower resistance → dangerous current can flow through the body. Dry skin provides insulation.

መልስ፦ ውሃ አምጪ ነው፤ እርጥብ ቆዳ መቋቋሚያው ዝቅተኛ ስለሆነ አደገኛ አሁንታ ያስፈልጋል።

End-of-unit Q26 — Don't flick switches with wet hands; don't throw water on electric fire

Same reason: water conducts electricity. Wet hands lower body resistance → shock. Water on electric fire conducts current back to you → shock. Use sand or CO₂ extinguisher instead.

መልስ፦ ውሃ ኤሌክትሪክ ያስተላልፋል፤ አደጋ ያስከትላል።

End-of-unit Q27 — Why is wet skin's resistance smaller?

Water + salts (from sweat) provide mobile ions, turning the skin from a dry insulator into a wet conductor. Lower resistance → more current for the same voltage → more danger.

መልስ፦ ጨውና ውሃ አዮኖችን ስለሚያመጡ ቆዳው ኮንዳክተር ይሆናል።

End-of-unit Q28 — What determines severity of a shock?

Current magnitude (most critical), duration, path through body, and skin resistance. Voltage alone is not enough — 100 V through dry skin may be safe, 30 V through wet skin can be lethal.

መልስ፦ በዋናነት የአሁንታው መጠን፣ የቆይታ ጊዜ፣ የመንገድ አቅጣጫ፣ የቆዳ መቋቋሚያ።

End-of-unit Q29 — Fuses prevent shock hazards?

Fuses interrupt when current exceeds a set threshold (e.g., 15 A). They protect wiring from overheating and prevent fire. They do NOT directly prevent small currents (5-100 mA) that cause shock — for that, GFCIs are needed.

መልስ፦ ፊውዞች ከመጠን በላይ አሁንታን ይገድባሉ፤ ግን ትንሽ ሾክ አያግዱም — ለዚያ GFCI ያስፈልጋል።

End-of-unit Q30 — Heater plugged in, lights go out

The heater draws a large current that exceeds the circuit's rated capacity → the fuse blows or circuit breaker trips. This is overloading protection. Fix: use lower-power devices or rewire with higher capacity.

መልስ፦ ማሞቂያው ከመጠን በላይ አሁንታ ስለሚስብ ፊውዙ ተቀልጦ ወይም ብሬከሩ ተጠልፎ መብራቶቹ ጠፉ።

End-of-unit Q31 — Why is 30 A fuse dangerous in a 15 A circuit?

The 15 A circuit's wiring is rated for only 15 A. A 30 A fuse will allow 30 A to flow before melting → wires overheat and can start a fire BEFORE the fuse protects. Always match fuse rating to circuit rating.

መልስ፦ የ15 A ወረዳ 30 A ፊውዝ ካለው ሽቦው ከመጠን በላይ ሞቆ እሳት ሊያስነሳ ይችላል።
