Section 5.1

Magnets

A magnet is a material that produces a magnetic field and can attract or repel other magnetic materials. The earliest magnets were natural stones called lodestones (magnetized magnetite, Fe₃O₄) discovered by the ancient Greeks. Today, magnets come in many shapes and are used in countless devices.

ማግኔት የራሱን መግነጢሳዊ መስክ የሚፈጥር እና ሌሎች መግነጢሳዊ ቁሶችን የሚስብ ወይም የሚጠላ ቁስ ነው። የመጀመሪያዎቹ ማግኔቶች ሎድስቶን (lodestone) ይባሉ ነበር — የተመግነጠዘ የብረት ኦክሳይድ ዓለት።

Types of Magnets

1. Permanent magnets — made of magnetized steel, retain their magnetism permanently. Used in speakers, phones, cars, generators, TVs, sensors.

2. Temporary magnets — become magnetized in the presence of a magnetic field; lose magnetism when the field is removed. Examples: iron nails, paper clips.

3. Electromagnets — a coil of wire wrapped around an iron core; only magnetic when current flows. Strength controlled by current. Used in generators, motors, transformers, MRI machines.

ሶስት ዓይነት ማግኔቶች፦ 1) ቋሚ (permanent) — ለዘላለም መግነጢሳዊነቱን ይይዛል። 2) ጊዜያዊ (temporary) — በመግነጢሳዊ መስክ ውስጥ ብቻ ይሠራል። 3) ኤሌክትሮማግኔት — በኤሌክትሪክ አሁንታ ብቻ ይሠራል።

Properties of Magnets

  • Two poles: Every magnet has a North pole and a South pole. Poles are the regions of greatest magnetic attraction.
  • Poles come in pairs: Cutting a magnet in half produces two smaller magnets, each with its own N and S pole. You cannot isolate a single pole (no magnetic monopoles).
  • Free-hanging magnet: Suspended freely, a magnet aligns with the Earth's N-S direction. The end pointing north is the North pole.
  • Like poles repel; unlike poles attract.
  • Distance matters: Force between two magnets is stronger when they are closer together.

የማግኔት ባህሪያት፦ ሁለት ዋልታዎች (ሰሜን፣ ደቡብ)፤ ዋልታዎች ሁልጊዜ ጥንድ ሆነው ይገኛሉ (ማግኔትን ቢቀራፈፉም ሁለቱም ዋልታዎች ይኖራሉ)፤ ተመሳሳይ ዋልታዎች ይጣላሉ፣ ተቃራኒ ዋልታዎች ይሳባሉ፤ በነፃ ሲሰቀል ወደ ሰሜን-ደቡብ ይመራል።

Demagnetization — losing magnetism:
• Exposing to extreme temperatures (heat).
• Hammering or dropping (breaks atomic alignment).
• Striking one magnet against another inappropriately.
• Applying a strong alternating field. ማግኔትነትን ማጣት፦ ሙቀት መጋለጥ፣ መምታት/መውደቅ፣ በሌላ ማግኔት ላይ መምታት።

Worked Examples

Example 5.1.1 — Distinguish the three types

Classify: a bar magnet, an iron nail that has been near a magnet, a coil of wire with current flowing through it.

ሶስቱን ዓይነት ለይ፦ ባር ማግኔት፣ በማግኔት አጠገብ የኖረ ብረት ምስማር፣ አሁንታ ያለው ጠመጠም (coil)።

  1. Bar magnet → Permanent magnet. It retains magnetism permanently, independent of surroundings.
  2. Iron nail near magnet → Temporary magnet. Magnetized only while near the magnet; loses magnetism when separated.
  3. Coil with current → Electromagnet. Magnetic only when current flows; strength depends on current.
Final: Permanent / Temporary / Electromagnet. · መልስ፦ ቋሚ / ጊዜያዊ / ኤሌክትሮማግኔት።

Example 5.1.2 — Breaking a magnet

A bar magnet is broken into three pieces. What poles does each piece have?

ባር ማግኔት ወደ ሶስት ተቀርፎ ቢከፋፈል እያንዳንዱ ቁራጭ ስንት ዋልታዎች ይኖሩታል?

  1. Magnetic poles cannot be isolated — they always come in pairs.
  2. When you cut a magnet, the atoms at the cut reorganize so each piece becomes a complete magnet.
  3. Therefore, each of the three pieces has its own North pole and South pole.
  4. This continues down to atomic level — no matter how finely you cut, you get pairs.
Final: Each piece has N and S poles. · መልስ፦ እያንዳንዱ ቁራጭ N እና S አለው።

Properties: 1) Two poles (N and S). 2) Poles always in pairs. 3) Like poles repel, unlike attract. 4) Free magnet aligns N-S. 5) Attraction strongest at poles. 6) Force decreases with distance.

ትርጓሜ፦ ማግኔት መግነጢሳዊ መስክ የሚፈጥርና ብረቶችን የሚስብ ቁስ ነው። ባህሪያት፦ ሁለት ዋልታዎች፣ ጥንድ ሆነው ይገኛሉ፣ ተመሳሳይ ይጣላሉ ተቃራኒ ይሳባሉ፣ በነፃ ሲሰቀል ወደ N-S ይመራል።

Review 5.1.2 — How can a magnet lose its magnetic properties?

By: 1) heating to extreme temperatures, 2) hammering/dropping, 3) striking against another magnet, 4) strong alternating current.

ማግኔትነቱን ያጣል፦ በሙቀት፣ በመምታት፣ በሌላ ማግኔት ላይ በመምታት።

Review 5.1.3 — Rule for magnetic attraction and repulsion

Like poles (N-N, S-S) repel. Unlike poles (N-S) attract.

ተመሳሳይ ዋልታዎች ይጣላሉ፣ ተቃራኒ ዋልታዎች ይሳባሉ።

Review 5.1.4 — Temporary vs permanent magnet

Permanent: retains magnetism permanently (e.g., steel magnet).

Temporary: magnetic only while near a magnetic field (e.g., iron nail near a magnet).

ቋሚ፦ ሁልጊዜ መግነጢሳዊ ነው። ጊዜያዊ፦ በመግነጢሳዊ መስክ ውስጥ ብቻ ነው።

Review 5.1.5 — What are magnetic poles? Does a magnetic mono-pole exist?

Magnetic poles are the two ends of a magnet where the magnetic force is strongest (N and S). No magnetic mono-pole has ever been observed — poles always come in pairs.

ዋልታዎች የማግኔቱ ሁለት ጫፎች ናቸው። አንድ ብቻ ዋልታ (monopole) የለም — ሁልጊዜ ጥንድ ናቸው።

Review 5.1.6 — If you break a magnet into two, do you get isolated N and S poles?

No. Each broken piece becomes a complete magnet with its own N and S pole. Poles cannot be separated.

አይደለም። እያንዳንዱ ቁራጭ የራሱ N እና S ይኖረዋል።

Section 5.2

Magnetic Field

A magnetic field is the region around a magnet (or moving charge) where a magnetic force acts on other magnetic materials or moving charges. It is visualized using magnetic field lines.

መግነጢሳዊ መስክ በማግኔት (ወይም በሚንቀሳቀስ ቻርጅ) ዙሪያ ያለ እና ሌሎች መግነጢሳዊ ቁሶች ወይም ቻርጆች ላይ ኃይል የሚሠራበት አካባቢ ነው።

Properties of Magnetic Field Lines

  • Never intersect. (If they did, there would be two field directions at one point.)
  • Form closed loops. Outside a magnet, N → S. Inside, S → N.
  • Origin at N, terminate at S (outside the magnet).
  • Density ∝ field strength. Closer lines = stronger field.
  • Strongest at the poles (lines are densest there).
  • Unlike poles: lines converge (come together). Like poles: lines diverge (bend apart).

የመስክ መስመሮች ባህሪያት፦ አይገናኙም፤ የተዘጉ ዑደቶች ናቸው፤ ከሰሜን ወደ ደቡብ ይሄዳሉ (ከማግኔቱ ውጭ)፤ ጥግግታቸው ከመስኩ ጥንካሬ ጋር ተመጣጣኝ ነው፤ በዋልታዎች ላይ ጥንካሬው ይበልጣል።

Worked Examples

Example 5.2.1 — Where is the field strongest?

A bar magnet is placed on a table with iron filings scattered around it. Where do the filings clump most densely?

ባር ማግኔት በጠረጴዛ ላይ ተቀምጦ የብረት ቅንጣቶች ተበትነውበታል። ቅንጣቶቹ የሚሰበሰቡት የት ነው?

  1. Iron filings align with the magnetic field lines.
  2. Where field lines are dense → strong field → more filings clump.
  3. Field lines are densest at the poles (N and S ends).
  4. So the filings clump most at the two poles.
Final: Densest at the poles. · መልስ፦ በዋልታዎቹ ላይ።

Example 5.2.2 — Do field lines cross?

Two magnetic field lines appear to be heading toward the same point. What must happen?

ሁለት የመስክ መስመሮች ወደ አንድ ነጥብ ይመሩ እንደሆነ ምን ይሆናል?

  1. If they crossed, that point would have two field directions.
  2. By definition, a field has exactly ONE direction at each point.
  3. Therefore, they must bend away from each other — field lines NEVER cross.
Final: They never cross. · መልስ፦ አይገናኙም።

Review 5.2.1 — Draw a bar magnet and its field lines

Field lines emerge from the N pole, curve through space, and enter the S pole. Inside the magnet, they continue S → N forming closed loops. Density is highest near the poles.

መስመሮቹ ከ N ወጥተው ወደ S ይገባሉ (ከማግኔቱ ውጭ)፤ በውስጥ ከ S ወደ N ይሄዳሉ።

Like poles (N-N or S-S): Field lines bend away from each other — they diverge in the middle.

Unlike poles (N-S): Field lines flow from N to S — they converge/connect in the middle.

ተመሳሳይ ዋልታዎች፦ መስመሮቹ ይለያያሉ። ተቃራኒ ዋልታዎች፦ መስመሮቹ ይገናኛሉ።

Review 5.2.3 — Where is field strongest / weakest?

Strongest: at the poles (lines dense). Weakest: far from the magnet (lines spread out).

በዋልታዎች ላይ ጠንካራ፤ ከማግኔቱ ርቆ ደካማ።

Review 5.2.4 — Explain magnetic field using field lines

A magnetic field is a region where a magnetic force acts, visualized by field lines whose direction shows the field direction and whose density shows the field's strength.

መስክ ማለት ኃይል የሚሠራበት አካባቢ ነው፤ መስመሮቹ አቅጣጫውንና ጥንካሬውን ያሳያሉ።

Review 5.2.5 — Properties of magnetic field lines

1) Never intersect. 2) Closed loops. 3) Outside: N → S; inside: S → N. 4) Density ∝ field strength. 5) Strongest at poles. 6) Lines from unlike poles converge, from like poles diverge.

1) አይገናኙም 2) የተዘጉ ናቸው 3) ከ N ወደ S 4) ጥግግታ = ጥንካሬ 5) በዋልታዎች ጠንካራ 6) ተቃራኒ ይሳባሉ ተመሳሳይ ይጣላሉ።

Section 5.3

Earth's Magnetic Field and the Compass

Earth has its own magnetic field (the geomagnetic field), extending from deep inside the Earth's outer core out into space. It is generated by the motion of molten iron in the outer core. The field is like that of a giant bar magnet tilted ~11° from the geographic axis.

ምድር የራሱ መግነጢሳዊ መስክ አላት። ይህ የሚፈጠረው በውጫዊው ኮር (outer core) ውስጥ በሚንቀሳቀሰው ብረት ነው። የምድር መስክ ከጂዮግራፊያዊው ዘንግ በ~11° ያዘነበለ ግዙፍ ባር ማግኔት ይመስላል።

Crucial consequence: The magnetic pole near Earth's geographic North is actually a magnetic SOUTH pole. That is why the North pole of a compass needle (which is a magnetic North pole) is attracted to it.
Therefore: Compass needle's N pole points toward Earth's geographic North — but that location is the Earth's magnetic S pole. ወሳኝ ጉዳይ፦ የምድር ጂዮግራፊያዊ ሰሜን አጠገብ ያለው መግነጢሳዊ ዋልታ በእውነቱ መግነጢሳዊ ደቡብ ዋልታ ነው! የኮምፓስ ሰሜን ዋልታ ወደዚያ ይሳባል።

Worked Examples

Example 5.3.1 — Compass direction reasoning

A compass is placed on a table. Why does its needle point toward geographic North?

ኮምፓስ በጠረጴዛ ላይ ተቀምጦ ለምን ወደ ጂዮግራፊያዊ ሰሜን ይመራል?

  1. The compass needle is itself a small magnet with an N pole and an S pole.
  2. Earth's magnetic field is like a giant bar magnet with its south magnetic pole near the geographic North.
  3. Like a magnetic compass, the needle's N pole is attracted to that magnetic S pole — which is under geographic North.
  4. Therefore, the needle's N pole points toward geographic North.
Final: N pole of needle → Earth's magnetic S pole (which is near geographic North). · መልስ፦ የኮምፓሱ N ዋልታ ወደ ምድር መግነጢሳዊ S ዋልታ (በጂዮግራፊያዊ ሰሜን አጠገብ) ይሳባል።

Example 5.3.2 — Why Earth's field matters

What would happen to Earth's compasses if the outer core stopped moving?

የውጫዊው ኮር መንቀሳቀስ ቢያቆም ኮምፓሶች ምን ይሆናሉ?

  1. Earth's magnetic field is generated by the motion of molten iron in the outer core.
  2. If that motion stopped, the field would collapse.
  3. Compasses would no longer align with a planetary field — they would point in random directions.
  4. Navigation would lose this natural reference.
Final: Earth's field would collapse; compasses would fail. · መልስ፦ መስኩ ይጠፋል፤ ኮምፓሶች አይሠሩም።

Textbook Exercises

Review 5.3.1 — What is the evidence for the existence of Earth's magnetic field?

A free compass needle always aligns N-S. Migratory animals (pigeons, sea turtles) navigate by sensing the field. Auroras are caused by charged particles from the Sun spiraling along field lines toward the poles.

ነፃ የሆነ የኮምፓስ መርፌ ሁልጊዜ ወደ N-S ይመራል፤ እንስሳት ለመንገድ ፍለጋ ይጠቀሙበታል፤ አውሮራስ በመስኩ ምክንያት ይከሰታል።

Review 5.3.2 — Why does a compass show magnetic North?

The compass needle is a tiny magnet. It aligns with the Earth's magnetic field. The needle's N pole is attracted toward Earth's magnetic S pole, which is located near the geographic North Pole. Thus, the N end of the needle points north.

የኮምፓስ መርፌ ትንሽ ማግኔት ነው፤ ከምድር መስክ ጋር ይስማማል። የ N ዋልታው ወደ ምድር መግነጢሳዊ S ዋልታ ይሳባል — ይህም በጂዮግራፊያዊ ሰሜን አጠገብ ነው።

Section 5.4

Magnetic Field of a Current-Carrying Conductor

A current-carrying wire produces a magnetic field around it. The field lines form closed concentric circles perpendicular to the wire.

አሁንታ የሚሸከም ሽቦ በዙሪያው መግነጢሳዊ መስክ ይፈጥራል። የመስክ መስመሮቹ ከሽቦው ጋር ቀጥተኛ የሆኑ የተዘጉ ክብ ቅርጽ ያላቸው ናቸው።

Formulas

B = μ₀ I / (2π d)
μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A (permeability of free space)
1 G = 10⁻⁴ T (gauss)
Earth's field at surface ≈ 5 × 10⁻⁵ T = 0.5 G
Right-hand grip rule: Point your right thumb in the direction of the current. Your curled fingers show the direction of the magnetic field lines (which form concentric circles around the wire). የቀኝ እጅ ደንብ፦ በቀኝ አውራ ጣትህ የአሁንታውን አቅጣጫ አሳይ። የታጠፉት ጣቶች የመስኩን አቅጣጫ ያሳያሉ።

Diagram: Field Around a Wire

Worked Examples

Example 5.4.1 — Field of a long straight wire (matric exam)

A long straight wire carries a 3 A current. Find B at 2 cm from the wire (μ₀ = 4π×10⁻⁷ T·m/A).

ረዥም ቀጥተኛ ሽቦ 3 A አሁንታ ይሸከማል። ከሽቦው 2 ሴ.ሜ ርቀት ላይ B ፈልግ።

  1. Given: I = 3 A; d = 2 cm = 0.02 m; μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A.
  2. Formula: B = μ₀ I / (2π d).
  3. Substitute: B = (4π×10⁻⁷)(3) / (2π × 0.02).
  4. Simplify π: = (4×3×10⁻⁷) / (2×0.02) = (12×10⁻⁷) / 0.04 = 300 × 10⁻⁷.
  5. Compute: B = 3.0 × 10⁻⁵ T.
Final: B = 3.0 × 10⁻⁵ T. · መልስ፦ 3.0×10⁻⁵ T።

Example 5.4.2 — Distance for a given B

What distance from a 10 A wire gives B = 1 × 10⁻⁵ T?

ከ10 A ሽቦ B = 1×10⁻⁵ T ለማግኘት ምን ያህል ርቀት ያስፈልጋል?

  1. Given: I = 10 A; B = 1×10⁻⁵ T; μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷.
  2. Rearrange: d = μ₀ I / (2π B).
  3. Substitute: d = (4π×10⁻⁷)(10) / (2π × 1×10⁻⁵).
  4. Simplify: = (4×10⁻⁶) / (2×10⁻⁵) = 0.2 m.
  5. Answer: d = 0.20 m = 20 cm.
Final: d = 0.20 m. · መልስ፦ 0.20 ሜትር።

Example 5.4.3 — Direction using right-hand rule

A wire carries current straight toward you (out of the page). Which way do the field lines wrap?

ሽቦ ወደ አንተ አቅጣጫ (ከገጹ ወደ ውጭ) አሁንታ ይሸከማል። የመስክ መስመሮች ወዴት ይታጠፋሉ?

  1. Right-hand rule: thumb points toward you (out of page).
  2. Fingers curl counter-clockwise (when viewed from your side).
  3. So field lines form counter-clockwise circles when looking along the current direction.
Final: Counter-clockwise circles. · መልስ፦ በተቃራኒ ሰዓት አቅጣጫ ክብ።

Textbook Exercises

Review 5.4.1 — Strength of B if distance is very large

From B = μ₀I/(2πd), as d → ∞, B → 0. Field becomes negligible far from the wire.

d ሲጨምር B ይቀንሳል — ወደ ዜሮ ይጠጋል።

Review 5.4.2 — Factors affecting B

B depends on: 1) current I (B ∝ I), 2) distance d (B ∝ 1/d), 3) permeability of medium (μ₀).

B ይወሰናል፦ በ I (ቀጥተኛ)፣ በ d (ተቃራኒ)፣ በ μ₀።

Section 5.5

Magnetic Force on a Moving Charge

A moving charge in a magnetic field experiences a magnetic force perpendicular to both its velocity and the field.

በመግነጢሳዊ መስክ ውስጥ የሚንቀሳቀስ ቻርጅ በሁለቱም ፍጥነቱና መስኩ ላይ ቀጥተኛ የሆነ መግነጢሳዊ ኃይል ይሰማዋል።

Formulas

F = q v B sin θ
θ = angle between v and B
F = 0 if θ = 0° or 180° (v ∥ B)
F is maximum if θ = 90° (v ⊥ B): F = q v B
Right-hand rule for F on a positive charge:
1) Point right thumb in the direction of velocity v.
2) Point fingers in the direction of field B.
3) The palm pushes in the direction of the force F.
For a negative charge (electron), the force is in the opposite direction. የቀኝ እጅ ደንብ (አዎንታዊ ቻርጅ)፦ አውራ ጣት ወደ v፣ ጣቶች ወደ B፣ መዳፍ ወደ F። አሉታዊ ቻርጅ — ኃይሉ ተቃራኒ አቅጣጫ ነው።

Diagram: Force on a Moving Charge

Worked Examples

Example 5.5.1 — Force when v ∥ B

A 50 C charged particle moves at 3 m/s parallel to a 1 T magnetic field. Find the force.

50 C ቻርጅ በ3 m/s ፍጥነት ከ1 T መስክ ጋር ትይዩ ሲንቀሳቀስ ኃይሉን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: q = 50 C; v = 3 m/s; B = 1 T; θ = 0° (parallel).
  2. Formula: F = q v B sin θ.
  3. Compute: F = 50 × 3 × 1 × sin 0° = 50 × 3 × 1 × 0 = 0 N.
  4. Interpretation: When velocity is parallel to the field, no magnetic force acts.
Final: F = 0 N. · መልስ፦ 0 N።

Example 5.5.2 — Force on an electron (matric exam)

An electron moves at 7.5 × 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to Earth's field of 1.0 × 10⁻⁵ T. Find the force. (e = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C.)

ኤሌክትሮን 7.5×10⁶ m/s በቀጥታ ወደ ምድር መስክ (1.0×10⁻⁵ T) ሲንቀሳቀስ ኃይሉን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: q = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C; v = 7.5 × 10⁶ m/s; B = 1.0 × 10⁻⁵ T; θ = 90° (perpendicular).
  2. Formula: F = q v B sin 90° = q v B (since sin 90° = 1).
  3. Compute: F = (1.6×10⁻¹⁹)(7.5×10⁶)(1.0×10⁻⁵).
  4. Numerator: 1.6 × 7.5 × 1.0 = 12; exponents: −19 + 6 + (−5) = −18.
  5. Answer: F = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁸ N.
Final: F = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁸ N. · መልስ፦ 1.2×10⁻¹⁸ N።

Example 5.5.3 — Particle perpendicular to field

A proton (q = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C) moves at 2 × 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to a 0.5 T field. Find the force.

ፕሮቶን (1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C) በ2×10⁶ m/s ወደ 0.5 T መስክ ቀጥታ ሲንቀሳቀስ ኃይሉን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: q = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C; v = 2×10⁶ m/s; B = 0.5 T; θ = 90°.
  2. Formula: F = q v B.
  3. Compute: F = (1.6×10⁻¹⁹)(2×10⁶)(0.5) = (1.6×2×0.5)×10⁻¹³.
  4. Answer: F = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ N.
Final: F = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ N. · መልስ፦ 1.6×10⁻¹³ N።

Textbook Exercises

Review 5.5.1 — Can the magnetic force on a moving charge be zero?

Yes. If v is parallel or anti-parallel to B (θ = 0° or 180°), sin θ = 0 → F = 0. Also zero if v = 0 (charge at rest).

አዎ። v ከ B ጋር ትይዩ ከሆነ F = 0።

Review 5.5.2 — Electron in Earth's field (7.5×10⁶ m/s perpendicular to 1.0×10⁻⁵ T)

Solution: F = q v B = (1.6×10⁻¹⁹)(7.5×10⁶)(1.0×10⁻⁵) = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁸ N.

መልስ፦ 1.2×10⁻¹⁸ N።

Section 5.6

Magnetic Force on a Current-Carrying Wire

A current-carrying wire in a magnetic field experiences a force proportional to the current, the field strength, the length of the wire, and sin θ (angle between current and field).

በመግነጢሳዊ መስክ ውስጥ ያለ አሁንታ የሚሸከም ሽቦ ኃይል ይሰማዋል።

Formulas

F = I L B sin θ
I = current, L = length, B = magnetic field, θ = angle between wire and field
F is maximum when θ = 90° (wire ⊥ field): F = I L B
Right-hand rule for wire: Point right fingers in the direction of B. Point right thumb in the direction of current I. The palm pushes in the direction of the force F. የቀኝ እጅ ደንብ (ሽቦ)፦ ጣቶች ወደ B፣ አውራ ጣት ወደ I፣ መዳፍ ወደ F።

Worked Examples

Example 5.6.1 — Wire perpendicular to field (matric exam)

A 25 cm wire carries 6 A at right angles to a 0.30 T magnetic field. Find the force.

25 ሴ.ሜ ሽቦ 6 A በ0.30 T መስክ ላይ ቀጥታ ሲያልፍ ኃይሉን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: L = 0.25 m; I = 6 A; B = 0.30 T; θ = 90°.
  2. Formula: F = I L B sin θ = I L B (since sin 90° = 1).
  3. Compute: F = 6 × 0.25 × 0.30.
  4. Answer: F = 0.45 N.
Final: F = 0.45 N. · መልስ፦ 0.45 N።

Example 5.6.2 — Find B from force (matric exam)

A 0.50 m wire carrying 8.0 A experiences a force of 0.40 N when perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field. Find B.

0.50 ሜ ሽቦ 8.0 A አሁንታ ያለው፤ 0.40 N ኃይል ሲሰማው B ፈልግ።

  1. Given: L = 0.50 m; I = 8.0 A; F = 0.40 N; θ = 90°.
  2. Rearrange: B = F / (I L).
  3. Compute: B = 0.40 / (8.0 × 0.50) = 0.40 / 4.0.
  4. Answer: B = 0.10 T.
Final: B = 0.10 T. · መልስ፦ 0.10 T።

Example 5.6.3 — Wire at angle θ

A 0.20 m wire carrying 5 A makes a 30° angle with a 0.40 T field. Find the force.

0.20 ሜ ሽቦ 5 A በ30° አንግል ከ0.40 T መስክ ጋር ሲያልፍ ኃይሉን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: L = 0.20 m; I = 5 A; B = 0.40 T; θ = 30°.
  2. Formula: F = I L B sin 30°.
  3. Compute: F = 5 × 0.20 × 0.40 × 0.5.
  4. Answer: F = 0.20 N.
Final: F = 0.20 N. · መልስ፦ 0.20 N።

Textbook Exercises

Review 5.6.1 — Explain force on a current-carrying wire

When a wire carries current in a magnetic field, the moving charges in the wire experience the Lorentz force q v × B. These forces transfer to the wire material, producing a net force on the wire (F = ILB sin θ).

አሁንታው በሽቦው ውስጥ ያሉ ቻርጆች በመንቀሳቀስ መግነጢሳዊ ኃይል ይሰማቸዋል። ይህም ወደ ሽቦው ይተላለፋል።

Review 5.6.2 — Factors affecting the force

1) Current I, 2) field strength B, 3) length L of wire in field, 4) angle θ between I and B.

በI፣ በB፣ በL፣ በθ ይወሰናል።

Review 5.6.3 — Right-hand rule for wire

Fingers → B; thumb → current I; palm → force F.

ጣቶች → B፣ አውራ ጣት → I፣ መዳፍ → F።

Review 5.6.4 — Wire 400 m, B = 0.20 T, F = 2.5 N. Current?

Solution: I = F / (L B) = 2.5 / (400 × 0.20) = 2.5 / 80 = 0.031 A = 31 mA.

መልስ፦ 0.031 A።

Section 5.7

Magnetic Force Between Two Parallel Wires

Parallel wires carrying currents exert magnetic forces on each other because each wire produces a field that acts on the other's moving charges.

ትይዩ ሽቦዎች አሁንታ ሲያስተላልፉ እያንዳንዱ የሚፈጥረው መስክ በሌላው ላይ ኃይል ያሳድራል።

The rule:
• Currents in the same direction → wires attract.
• Currents in opposite directions → wires repel.
• Magnitudes of force are equal and opposite (Newton's third law), even if currents differ. ደንቡ፦ ተመሳሳይ አቅጣጫ → ይሳባሉ። ተቃራኒ አቅጣጫ → ይጣላሉ። መጠኖቹ እኩል ናቸው።

Diagram: Parallel Wires

Worked Examples

Example 5.7.1 — Same direction reasoning

Wire A carries current upward; wire B (to the right) also carries current upward. Do they attract or repel?

ሽቦ A ወደ ላይ አሁንታ፣ ሽቦ B በቀኝ በኩል ወደ ላይ አሁንታ። ይሳባሉ ወይስ ይጣላሉ?

  1. Wire A produces field at B's location. Using right-hand rule: thumb up (A), fingers curl counter-clockwise viewed from above → at B's location (to the right of A), field points OUT of the page.
  2. Wire B carries current upward. Force on B: F = I L × B. Using right-hand rule: I up, B out → F toward A (left).
  3. So wire B is pushed toward A → attraction.
  4. Conclusion: same direction → attract.
Final: Attract. · መልስ፦ ይሳባሉ።

Example 5.7.2 — Opposite direction reasoning

Wire A carries current upward; wire B (to the right) carries current downward. Do they attract or repel?

ሽቦ A ወደ ላይ፣ ሽቦ B (በቀኝ) ወደ ታች። ይሳባሉ ወይስ ይጣላሉ?

  1. Wire A's field at B's location points out of page (same as before).
  2. Wire B current is now downward. Force on B: I down, B out → F points right (away from A).
  3. So B is pushed away from A → repulsion.
  4. Conclusion: opposite directions → repel.
Final: Repel. · መልስ፦ ይጣላሉ።

Textbook Exercises

Review 5.7.1 — Two parallel wires with currents in same direction attract. Why?

Each wire produces a field at the other's location. Applying the right-hand rule twice (once for each wire's field on the other) shows that the force on each wire points toward the other. The magnitudes are equal by Newton's third law.

እያንዳንዱ ሽቦ የሚፈጥረው መስክ በሌላው ላይ ኃይል ያሳድራል። የቀኝ እጅ ደንብ ሲተገበር ኃይሉ ወደ ሌላው ይመራል።

Review 5.7.2 — Phase and neutral lines from power poles — attract or repel?

Phase and neutral carry AC current. In a single-phase AC system, phase and neutral carry currents in opposite directions at every instant (current flows out on phase, returns on neutral). So the wires repel each other. However, in practice they are often bundled together to minimize this effect.

Phase እና neutral በተቃራኒ አቅጣጫ አሁንታ ስለሚያስተላልፉ ይጣላሉ።

ማግኔትነት በሕክምና፣ በቤት ውስጥ፣ በኤሌክትሮኒክስ፣ በኢንዱስትሪና በአሰሳ ሁሉም ቦታ ያገለግላል።

Worked Examples

Example 5.8.1 — Why an MRI magnet is very strong

An MRI uses a field of 3 T. How does this compare to Earth's field?

MRI 3 T መስክ ይጠቀማል። ከምድር መስክ ጋር ማወዳደር?

  1. Earth's surface field ≈ 5 × 10⁻⁵ T = 0.00005 T.
  2. MRI field = 3 T.
  3. Ratio = 3 / (5×10⁻⁵) = 6 × 10⁴ = 60,000 times stronger.
  4. This strong field is needed to make hydrogen nuclei (protons) in the body align.
Final: About 60,000 × Earth's field. · መልስ፦ ~60,000 እጥፍ ጠንካራ።

Example 5.8.2 — Magnetic data storage

How does a hard disk drive use magnetism to store data?

ሃርድ ዲስክ ዳታ ለማከማቸት ማግኔትነትን እንዴት ይጠቀማል?

  1. The disk surface is coated with a magnetic material.
  2. A tiny write head magnetizes each region of the surface in one of two directions (N → left or N → right).
  3. These two directions represent binary 0 and 1.
  4. A read head later detects the field direction → recovers the data.
Final: Two magnetization directions = bits 0 and 1. · መልስ፦ ሁለት አቅጣጫዎች = 0 እና 1።
