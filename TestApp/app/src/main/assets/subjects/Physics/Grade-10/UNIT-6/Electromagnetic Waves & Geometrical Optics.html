Section 6.1

Electromagnetic (EM) Waves

An electromagnetic wave is a wave made of oscillating electric and magnetic fields that travel together through space at the speed of light. Unlike mechanical waves (sound, water), EM waves need no medium — they can travel through a vacuum.

ኤሌክትሮማግኔቲክ ሞገድ በተቀናጁ የኤሌክትሪክና መግነጢሳዊ መስኮች የተሰራ እና በብርሃን ፍጥነት በጠፈር የሚጓዝ ሞገድ ነው። ሜካኒካል ሞገዶች ከሚለው በተቃራኒ ኤሌክትሮማግኔቲክ ሞገዶች መካከለኛ አያስፈልጋቸውም — በባዶ ጠፈር ውስጥ ማለፍ ይችላሉ።

Key facts:
• EM waves are transverse — E and B oscillate perpendicular to the direction of propagation.
• E and B fields are perpendicular to each other and to the direction of travel.
• Speed in vacuum: c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s.
• Produced by accelerating charges (vibrating electrons).
• Carry radiant energy from the Sun — 92% reaches us as IR + visible. ቁልፍ እውነታዎች፦ ተጨማሪ ሞገዶች ናቸው። የ E እና B መስኮች እርስ በርስ ቀጥታ ናቸው። በባዶ ጠፈር ውስጥ ፍጥነቱ c = 3×10⁸ m/s። በተጣደፉ ቻርጆች ይፈጠራሉ።

Formulas

c = λ × f   (speed = wavelength × frequency)
c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s (vacuum)
Higher frequency → shorter wavelength

Diagram: EM Wave

Worked Examples

Example 6.1.1 — Frequency from wavelength (red light)

Red light has wavelength 700 nm. Find its frequency.

ቀይ ብርሃን የሞገድ ርዝመት 700 nm ነው። ድግግሞሹን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: λ = 700 nm = 700 × 10⁻⁹ m; c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s.
  2. Formula: c = λf → f = c/λ.
  3. Substitute: f = (3.00 × 10⁸) / (700 × 10⁻⁹).
  4. Compute: f = 4.29 × 10¹⁴ Hz.
Final: f = 4.29 × 10¹⁴ Hz. · መልስ፦ 4.29×10¹⁴ Hz።

Example 6.1.2 — Wavelength from frequency (FM radio)

An FM radio station broadcasts at 103.4 MHz. Find its wavelength.

ኤፍ ኤም ሬዲዮ 103.4 MHz ያስተላልፋል። የሞገድ ርዝመቱን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: f = 103.4 MHz = 103.4 × 10⁶ Hz; c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s.
  2. Formula: λ = c/f.
  3. Substitute: λ = (3.00 × 10⁸) / (103.4 × 10⁶).
  4. Compute: λ = 2.9 m.
Final: λ = 2.9 m. · መልስ፦ 2.9 ሜትር።

Review 6.1.1 — Explain how an EM wave propagates

An accelerating electric charge produces a changing electric field, which induces a changing magnetic field, which in turn induces a changing electric field. These coupled oscillations propagate outward as an electromagnetic wave.

ተጣድፎ የሚንቀሳቀስ ቻርጅ ተለዋዋጭ የኤሌክትሪክ መስክ ይፈጥራል፤ ይህም ተለዋዋጭ መግነጢሳዊ መስክ ይፈጥራል፤ በዚህም ሞገዱ ወደ ውጭ ይጓዛል።

Review 6.1.2 — Do EM waves need a medium to travel?

No. EM waves can travel through a vacuum — this is how sunlight reaches Earth through empty space.

አይፈልጉም። ኤሌክትሮማግኔቲክ ሞገዶች በባዶ ጠፈር ውስጥ ማለፍ ይችላሉ።

Review 6.1.3 — Describe the properties of EM waves

1) Transverse — E and B perpendicular to direction of travel. 2) E ⊥ B. 3) Travel at c = 3×10⁸ m/s in vacuum. 4) Carry energy (radiant energy). 5) Can travel through vacuum. 6) Produced by accelerating charges. 7) Obey c = λf.

1) ተጨማሪ 2) E ⊥ B 3) በባዶ ጠፈር c = 3×10⁸ m/s 4) ጉልበት ያጓጉዛሉ 5) መካከለኛ አያስፈልጋቸውም 6) በተጣደፉ ቻርጆች ይፈጠራሉ 7) c = λf።

Section 6.2

The EM Spectrum

The electromagnetic spectrum is the complete range of all EM wave frequencies and wavelengths. All EM waves are the same type of wave — they differ only in frequency, wavelength, and energy.

ኤሌክትሮማግኔቲክ ስፔክትረም ሁሉንም የኤሌክትሮማግኔቲክ ሞገዶች ድግግሞሾችና ሞገድ ርዝመቶች የሚያካትት ነው። ሁሉም ተመሳሳይ ዓይነት ሞገዶች ናቸው — የሚለያዩት በድግግሞሽ፣ በሞገድ ርዝመትና በጉልበት ብቻ ነው።

በድግግሞሽ እየጨመረ አዘጋጅ፦ X-ray, infrared, visible, gamma, radio።

Example 6.2.2 — Uses and dangers

Why are X-rays used in hospitals but with strict precautions?

X-ray በሆስፒታል ለምን ጥቅም ላይ ይውላል ግን በጥንቃቄ?

Section 6.3

Light as a Wave

Light is an EM wave that our eyes can detect. Visible light occupies a tiny slice of the EM spectrum. It travels at c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s in a vacuum, slower in matter, and always in straight lines (in a uniform medium).

ብርሃን ዓይኖቻችን ሊያዩት የሚችሉት የኤሌክትሮማግኔቲክ ሞገድ ነው። በባዶ ጠፈር ውስጥ በ c = 3×10⁸ m/s ይጓዛል፤ በቁስ ውስጥ ይቀንሳል፤ በተመሳሳይ ሁኔታ ውስጥ በቀጥታ መስመር ይጓዛል።

Speed of light:
• Vacuum: c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s (nothing travels faster).
• Water: ≈ 2.25 × 10⁸ m/s
• Glass: ≈ 1.97 × 10⁸ m/s
• Slower in denser media (due to interactions with atoms).
• Light from the Sun reaches Earth in ~8.5 minutes (150 million km). የብርሃን ፍጥነት፦ በባዶ ጠፈር 3×10⁸ m/s፤ በውሃ ~2.25×10⁸፤ በመስታወት ~1.97×10⁸። በጨለማ ቁስ ውስጥ ይቀንሳል።

Formulas

c = λ × f   (all EM waves, any medium)
v = c / n   (speed in a medium; n = refractive index)

Worked Examples

Example 6.3.1 — Frequency of 400 nm light

Violet light has λ = 400 nm. Find its frequency.

ቫዮሌት ብርሃን λ = 400 nm። ድግግሞሹን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: λ = 400 × 10⁻⁹ m; c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s.
  2. Formula: f = c/λ.
  3. Substitute: f = (3.00 × 10⁸) / (400 × 10⁻⁹).
  4. Compute: f = 7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz.
Final: f = 7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz. · መልስ፦ 7.5×10¹⁴ Hz።

Example 6.3.2 — Speed in glass

Light enters glass with refractive index n = 1.52. Find its speed in the glass.

ብርሃን ወደ n = 1.52 ያለው መስታወት ሲገባ ፍጥነቱን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: n = 1.52; c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s.
  2. Formula: n = c/v → v = c/n.
  3. Substitute: v = (3.00 × 10⁸) / 1.52.
  4. Compute: v = 1.97 × 10⁸ m/s.
Final: v = 1.97 × 10⁸ m/s. · መልስ፦ 1.97×10⁸ m/s።

Textbook Exercises

Review 6.3.1 — Are light rays real?

No. Light rays are imaginary straight lines used to show the path light travels. Only the light itself is real.

አይደሉም። ብርሃን ጨረሮች ምናባዊ መስመሮች ናቸው።

Review 6.3.2 — Evidence that light travels in straight lines

Dust particles illuminated by a light beam (e.g., through a window) show light going straight. Shadows have sharp edges (if the source is small). A laser beam is straight.

በብርሃን ጨረር ውስጥ የሚታዩ የአቧራ ቅንጣቶች ቀጥተኛ መሆኑን ያሳያሉ።

Review 6.3.3 — How does light wave propagate?

Light travels in straight lines (rays) in a uniform medium. In a new medium, its speed changes, causing bending (refraction). Direction is indicated by arrows on rays.

በተመሳሳይ ሁኔታ ውስጥ በቀጥታ ይጓዛል። አዲስ ሁኔታ ሲገባ ፍጥነቱ ይቀየራል — refraction።

Review 6.3.4 — Do EM waves need a medium?

No — they travel through vacuum. This is why we can see the Sun, stars, and galaxies.

አይፈልጉም። ስለዚህ ፀሐይን፣ ከዋክብትን ማየት እንችላለን።

Review 6.3.5 — Speed of light and symbol

c ≈ 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s in vacuum. It changes (decreases) in matter, but c itself is the vacuum value.

c ≈ 3×10⁸ m/s በባዶ ጠፈር። በቁስ ውስጥ ይቀንሳል።

Review 6.3.6 — Frequency of 400 nm wave

f = c/λ = (3×10⁸)/(400×10⁻⁹) = 7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz.

መልስ፦ 7.5×10¹⁴ Hz።

Section 6.4

Laws of Reflection & Refraction

Reflection: Light bounces off a surface. Refraction: Light bends when moving between media of different optical densities.

ማንጸባረቅ ብርሃን ከወለል ላይ መመለስ ነው። ሪፍራክሽን ብርሃን ከአንድ ሁኔታ ወደ ሌላ ሲገባ መታጠፍ ነው።

Formulas

Law of reflection: θᵢ = θᵣ (angles from normal)
Snell's law: n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂
Refractive index: n = c / v
Critical angle: sin θ_c = n₂ / n₁   (n₁ > n₂)
n₁ = 1.00 (air), 1.33 (water), 1.52 (glass), 2.42 (diamond)
Bending rules:
• Slower medium (higher n) → ray bends toward the normal.
• Faster medium (lower n) → ray bends away from the normal.
• Total internal reflection occurs when going from high-n to low-n medium with θᵢ > θ_c. የመታጠፍ ደንብ፦ ወደ ጨለማ ሁኔታ → ወደ ኖርማል ይታጠፋል። ወደ ብርሃን ሁኔታ → ከኖርማል ይርቃል። ሙሉ ውስጣዊ ማንጸባረቅ — ከ n ከፍተኛ ወደ n ዝቅተኛ እና θ > θ_c።

Worked Examples

Example 6.4.1 — Snell's law (water → air)

Light in water (n=1.33) hits the surface at 35° from normal. Find the angle in air (n=1.00).

በውሃ ውስጥ ያለ ብርሃን (n=1.33) 35° አንግል ሲነካ በአየር ውስጥ ያለው አንግል ስንት ነው?

  1. Given: n₁ = 1.33 (water), θ₁ = 35°, n₂ = 1.00 (air).
  2. Snell: n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂.
  3. Substitute: 1.33 × sin 35° = 1.00 × sin θ₂.
  4. Compute: 1.33 × 0.574 = 0.763 → θ₂ = sin⁻¹(0.763) = 49.7°.
  5. Check: θ₂ > θ₁ → ray bent away from normal (into less dense medium) ✓
Final: θ₂ ≈ 49.7°. · መልስ፦ 49.7°።

Example 6.4.2 — Critical angle of glass

Find the critical angle for glass (n=1.52) → air.

የመስታወት (n=1.52) → አየር ወሳኝ አንግል ፈልግ።

  1. Given: n₁ = 1.52, n₂ = 1.00.
  2. Formula: sin θ_c = n₂/n₁.
  3. Substitute: sin θ_c = 1/1.52 = 0.658.
  4. Compute: θ_c = sin⁻¹(0.658) = 41.1°.
Final: θ_c ≈ 41°. · መልስ፦ 41°።

Example 6.4.3 — Refractive index from speeds

Light travels at 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s in a medium. Find the refractive index.

ብርሃን በ2.0×10⁸ m/s በሚጓዝበት ሁኔታ ውስጥ የማጣቀሻ ኢንዴክስ ፈልግ።

  1. Given: v = 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s; c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s.
  2. Formula: n = c/v.
  3. Compute: n = 3.00/2.0 = 1.5.
Final: n = 1.5. · መልስ፦ n = 1.5።

Textbook Exercises

Review 6.4.1 — Ray strikes a surface at 25° to surface, find angle of reflection

Angles are from the normal. If angle with surface = 25°, then angle with normal = 90° − 25° = 65°. So θᵢ = θᵣ = 65°.

ከወለል 25° → ከኖርማል 65°። θᵣ = 65°።

Review 6.4.2 — State the law of reflection

1) θᵢ = θᵣ (both measured from the normal). 2) Incident ray, reflected ray, and normal all lie in the same plane.

1) θᵢ = θᵣ 2) ሁሉም በአንድ አውሮፕላን ውስጥ ናቸው።

Review 6.4.3 — Reflection from rough vs smooth surfaces

Smooth (specular): parallel rays stay parallel after reflection → clear image. Rough (diffuse): parallel rays scatter in different directions → no clear image (how we see non-shiny objects).

ለስላሳ (specular) — ትይዩ ጨረሮች ትይዩ ሆነው ይመለሳሉ። ሸካራ (diffuse) — ጨረሮች ይበትናሉ።

Review 6.4.4 — State Snell's Law

n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂ (ratio of sin θ₁ to sin θ₂ equals the ratio of refractive indices).

n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂.

Review 6.4.5 — Refractive index meaning

n = c/v — the ratio of light's speed in vacuum to its speed in the medium. Higher n → slower light → more bending.

n = c/v — በባዶ ጠፈር ፍጥነት በሁኔታው ፍጥነት ሲካፈል።

Review 6.4.6 — Air → diamond at 30° from interface

θᵢ (from normal) = 90° − 30° = 60°. n₁ = 1.00, n₂ = 2.42.

sin θ₂ = (1.00/2.42) sin 60° = 0.413 × 0.866 = 0.358.

θ₂ = sin⁻¹(0.358) ≈ 21°. From interface: 90° − 21° = 69°.

መልስ፦ ከወለል ~69°።

Review 6.4.7 — Air → water, bend toward or away from normal?

Toward the normal. Water is denser (n=1.33 > 1.00), so light slows and bends toward the normal.

ወደ ኖርማል ይታጠፋል።

Review 6.4.8 — Where is light fastest: kerosene, turpentine, water?

Speed is highest in the lowest-n material. From Table 6.1: water (n=1.33) < kerosene (1.44) < turpentine (1.47). Light is fastest in water.

በውሃ ውስጥ በጣም ፈጣን ነው (n=1.33)።

Review 6.4.9 — Silicon → water, ray at 69° in water

n_water ≈ 1.33, n_silicon ≈ 3.5 (typical). From Snell: n_si sin θ_si = n_w sin θ_w. sin θ_si = (1.33/3.5) sin 69° = 0.38 × 0.934 = 0.355. θ_si ≈ 20.8°.

መልስ፦ θ ≈ 21°።

Review 6.4.10 — Conditions for total internal reflection

1) Light travels from denser to rarer medium (n₁ > n₂). 2) Angle of incidence > critical angle.

1) ከጨለማ ወደ ቀላል ሁኔታ 2) θᵢ > θ_c።

Review 6.4.11 — Critical angle explanation

The angle of incidence in the denser medium for which the refracted ray travels along the interface (θ₂ = 90°). Beyond it, total internal reflection occurs.

የመግቢያው አንግል ለእሱ θ₂ = 90° የሚሆንበት ነጥብ ነው።

Review 6.4.12 — Why does a diamond sparkle?

Diamond has a very high refractive index (n = 2.42) and small critical angle (~24°). Light entering a diamond undergoes multiple total internal reflections before exiting, spreading in different directions — creating the sparkle.

አልማዝ n=2.42 ስላለው ብዙ ጊዜ ሙሉ ውስጣዊ ማንጸባረቅ ያደርጋል — ብልጭታ ይፈጥራል።

Review 6.4.13 — Diamond → silicon TIR?

Diamond n=2.42, silicon n≈3.5. Since silicon has higher n, light travels from lower to higher — no TIR (TIR requires going from higher to lower n).

አይደለም። TIR ከፍተኛ n ወደ ዝቅተኛ n ብቻ ነው።

Review 6.4.14 — Which color refracts most/least in prism?

Violet refracts the most (shortest wavelength, highest n in glass). Red refracts the least (longest wavelength, lowest n).

ቫዮሌት በጣም ይታጠፋል፤ ቀይ በትንሹ።

Section 6.5

Mirrors and Lenses

Mirrors reflect light to form images. Lenses refract light to form images. Both can create real (on-screen) or virtual (apparent) images depending on the geometry.

መስተዋቶች ብርሃንን በማንጸባረቅ ምስል ይፈጥራሉ። ሌንሶች በማጣመም ምስል ይፈጥራሉ።

Formulas

Mirror equation: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f
Lens equation: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f   (v, u, f all signed)
Magnification: m = h'/h = −v/u
f = R/2 (spherical mirror)

Sign conventions

Worked Examples

Example 6.5.1 — Concave mirror (matric exam)

Object 6 cm tall at 25 cm from a concave mirror with f = 15 cm. Find image distance, size, and nature.

ነገር 6 ሴ.ሜ ርዝመት፣ 25 ሴ.ሜ ከተዘበራረቀ መስተዋት (f=15 ሴ.ሜ)። ምስሉን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: h = +6 cm; u = +25 cm; f = +15 cm (concave).
  2. Mirror equation: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f → 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/15 − 1/25.
  3. Compute: = (5 − 3)/75 = 2/75 → v = +37.5 cm. Real image in front.
  4. Magnification: m = −v/u = −37.5/25 = −1.5.
  5. Image height: h' = m·h = −1.5 × 6 = −9 cm. Inverted, enlarged.
Final: Real, inverted, magnified (1.5×), at 37.5 cm in front. · መልስ፦ እውነተኛ፣ ተገልብጦ፣ የተስፋፋ (1.5×)፣ 37.5 ሴ.ሜ ፊት።

Example 6.5.2 — Convex mirror (matric exam)

Convex mirror R = 3 m; object at 5 m. Find image distance and nature.

Convex መስተዋት R=3 ሜትር፤ ነገር 5 ሜትር። ምስሉን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: R = −3 m → f = −1.5 m; u = +5 m.
  2. Mirror: 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−1.5) − 1/5.
  3. Compute: = −0.667 − 0.2 = −0.867 → v = −1.15 m. Behind mirror (virtual).
  4. Magnification: m = −v/u = −(−1.15)/5 = +0.23. Upright, smaller.
Final: Virtual, upright, 0.23× size, at 1.15 m behind. · መልስ፦ ምናባዊ፣ ቀጥታ፣ 0.23×፣ 1.15 ሜትር ጀርባ።

Example 6.5.3 — Concave lens (matric exam)

Concave lens f = −15 cm; image at −10 cm. Find object distance and magnification.

Concave ሌንስ f=−15 ሴ.ሜ; ምስል −10 ሴ.ሜ። የነገር ርቀትንና ማጉላትን ፈልግ።

  1. Given: v = −10 cm; f = −15 cm.
  2. Lens: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f → 1/u = 1/v − 1/f.
  3. Compute: 1/u = 1/(−10) − 1/(−15) = −0.1 + 0.067 = −0.033.
  4. u = −30 cm (virtual object — or by absolute value, object at 30 cm in front if interpreting conventionally: u = +30 cm).
  5. Magnification: m = −v/u = −(−10)/(30) = +0.33.
Final: u = 30 cm; m = +0.33 (virtual, erect, smaller). · መልስ፦ u = 30 ሴ.ሜ፤ m = +0.33።

Example 6.5.4 — Convex lens (matric exam)

Object 2 cm tall at 15 cm from convex lens f=10 cm. Find v, h', m.

ነገር 2 ሴ.ሜ፣ 15 ሴ.ሜ ከ convex ሌንስ (f=10)። ፈልግ።

  1. Given: h = +2 cm; u = +15 cm; f = +10 cm.
  2. Lens: 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/10 − 1/15 = (3−2)/30 = 1/30.
  3. v = +30 cm — real image behind lens.
  4. Magnification: m = −v/u = −30/15 = −2.
  5. Image height: h' = m·h = −2 × 2 = −4 cm. Inverted, enlarged 2×.
Final: Real, inverted, 2× enlarged, at 30 cm behind lens. · መልስ፦ እውነተኛ፣ ተገልብጦ፣ 2×፣ 30 ሴ.ሜ።

Section 6.6

Human Eye & Optical Instruments

The human eye is like a camera: it uses a lens system to focus images onto a light-sensitive screen called the retina. Optical instruments extend the eye's capabilities.

የሰው ዓይን እንደ ካሜራ ነው፦ ሌንስ ተጠቅሞ ምስልን በሬቲና ላይ ያተኩራል። የኦፕቲካል መሣሪያዎች የዓይንን አቅም ያራዝማሉ።

Eye structure and function

  • Cornea: transparent front bulge; provides most refraction.
  • Iris: colored muscular diaphragm; controls pupil size.
  • Pupil: opening that lets light in.
  • Lens: fine-tunes focus by changing shape (accommodation).
  • Retina: contains rods (light intensity) and cones (color).
  • Optic nerve: carries signals to the brain.

ኮርኒያ — የፊት ግልጽ ክፍል። አይሪስ — የቀለም ጡንቻ። ተማሪ (pupil) — ብርሃን የሚገባበት ክፍተት። ሌንስ — ትኩረትን የሚያስተካክል። ሬቲና — rods እና cones ያሉበት። ኦፕቲክ ነርቭ — ወደ አንጎል የሚወስድ።

Power of accommodation

The eye's lens can change its focal length by the action of ciliary muscles. When relaxed → lens thin → long f (sees far). When contracted → lens thick → short f (sees near). Near point = 25 cm; far point = infinity.

ሲሊየሪ ጡንቻዎች በመለዋወጥ ሌንሱ ትኩረቱን ያስተካክላል። ሲዝናና → ቀጭን → � USር ርቀት ያያል። ሲወጠር → ወፍራም → ቅርብ ያያል። Near point = 25 ሴ.ሜ፤ Far point = ወሰን የለውም።

  • Simple microscope (magnifier): single convex lens; virtual, erect, enlarged image.

Section 6.7

Primary Colors of Light & Human Vision

The retina contains two kinds of photoreceptor cells: rods (sensitive to light intensity, not color) and cones (sensitive to color). There are three types of cones, each most sensitive to red, green, or blue light.

ሬቲና ሁለት ዓይነት ፎቶሪሴፕተሮች አሉት፦ rods (ለብርሃን መጠን) እና cones (ለቀለም)። ሶስት ዓይነት cones አሉ — ለቀይ፣ ለአረንጓዴ፣ ለሰማያዊ ስሜታዊ።

Additive primary colors: Red (R), Green (G), Blue (B).
• Mixing all three equally → white light.
• Mixing two primaries gives a secondary color:
  R + G = Yellow
  R + B = Magenta
  G + B = Cyan ዋና ተጨማሪ ቀለሞች፦ ቀይ (R)፣ አረንጓዴ (G)፣ ሰማያዊ (B)። ሶስቱም ሲደመሩ ነጭ። R+G=ቢጫ፣ R+B=ማጄንታ፣ G+B=ሳያን።

Example 6.7.1 — Predicting secondary colors

What color does red + green light produce? What about all three?

  1. Red + green → yellow (a secondary color).
  2. Red + blue → magenta.
  3. Green + blue → cyan.
  4. Red + green + blue (equal intensities) → white.
Final: R+G = Yellow; R+G+B = White. · መልስ፦ R+G=ቢጫ፤ R+G+B=ነጭ።

Example 6.7.2 — Color of an object

Why does a red apple look red in white light?

ቀይ ፖም በነጭ ብርሃን ለምን ቀይ ይመስላል?

  1. White light contains all visible colors.
  2. The apple's pigment absorbs all colors except red.
  3. Red light is reflected into our eyes.
  4. Our brain interprets this as "red".
Final: Reflects red; absorbs other colors. · መልስ፦ ቀዩን ያንጸባርቃል፤ ሌሎቹን ይስባል።

Section 6.9

Color Subtraction Using Filters

Subtractive color works the opposite way of additive: pigments/filters absorb some wavelengths and reflect/transmit others. The subtractive primary colors are cyan, magenta, yellow.

Subtractive color ማጣመር በተቃራኒ አቅጣጫ ይሠራል፦ ቀለሞች/ፊልተሮች አንዳንድ ሞገዶችን ይስባሉ፣ ሌሎችን ያንጸባርቃሉ። ዋና የመቀነሻ ቀለሞች፦ ሳያን፣ ማጄንታ፣ ቢጫ።

Subtractive primaries: Cyan (C), Magenta (M), Yellow (Y).
• Cyan filter → absorbs red; transmits blue + green (= cyan).
• Magenta filter → absorbs green; transmits red + blue (= magenta).
• Yellow filter → absorbs blue; transmits red + green (= yellow).
Complements: Cyan↔Red, Magenta↔Green, Yellow↔Blue. ዋና የመቀነሻ ቀለሞች፦ ሳያን፣ ማጄንታ፣ ቢጫ። ሳያን → ቀዩን ይስባል። ማጄንታ → አረንጓዴውን ይስባል። ቢጫ → ሰማያዊውን ይስባል።

Worked Examples

Example 6.9.1 — Cyan filter

White light shines on a cyan filter. What color emerges?

ነጭ ብርሃን በሳያን ፊልተር ላይ ሲወድቅ ምን ቀለም ይወጣል?

  1. Cyan = Blue + Green (in additive terms).
  2. Cyan filter absorbs red (the complementary color).
  3. It transmits blue and green.
  4. Our eyes see blue + green = cyan.
Final: Cyan. · መልስ፦ ሳያን።

Example 6.9.2 — Yellow light on blue pigment

Yellow light falls on a blue pigment. What color appears?

ቢጫ ብርሃን በሰማያዊ ቀለም ላይ ሲወድቅ ምን ይታያል?

  1. Yellow light = Red + Green.
  2. Blue pigment absorbs R and G (and reflects only B).
  3. But the incoming light has no blue (only R+G).
  4. So all incoming light is absorbed → the object appears black.
Final: Black (all absorbed). · መልስ፦ ጥቁር።

Example 6.9.3 — Cyan + Yellow filters

White light passes through a cyan filter, then through a yellow filter. What emerges?

ነጭ ብርሃን በሳያን ፊልተር ከዚያም በቢጫ ፊልተር ላይ ሲያልፍ ምን ይወጣል?

  1. Cyan filter transmits B + G (absorbs R).
  2. Yellow filter transmits R + G (absorbs B).
  3. Only the overlap passes both: G (green).
Final: Green. · መልስ፦ አረንጓዴ።
