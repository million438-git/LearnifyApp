🔭 Introduction

Everything moves. Motion is relative – a body may be at rest relative to one reference and in motion relative to another. This unit covers uniformly accelerated motion, equations of motion, graphs, vertical motion under gravity, and uniform circular motion (2D).

📐 3.1 Uniformly Accelerated Motion in 1D

Acceleration

Vector: rate of change of velocity. \( \vec{a} = \frac{\Delta \vec{v}}{\Delta t} \) (m/s²).
Uniform acceleration: velocity changes by equal amounts in equal time intervals.

Average acceleration: \(a_{av} = \frac{v_f - v_i}{\Delta t}\)
Instantaneous acceleration: \(a = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}\)

Example 3.1: Car from rest to 20 m/s in 6 s → a = 3.33 m/s².
Example 3.2: v(t)=t²-2t → a(t)=2t-2 → at t=2 s, a=2 m/s².
[Cheetah acceleration – 96.6 km/h in 3 s → a≈8.94 m/s²]

📈 3.2 Equations of Motion (1D constant a)

  • \(v_f = v_i + at\)
  • \(s = v_i t + \frac12 a t^2\)
  • \(s = v_f t - \frac12 a t^2\)
  • \(s = \frac{v_i + v_f}{2} t\)
  • \(v_f^2 = v_i^2 + 2 a s\)

All are vector equations; direction matters.

Example 3.3: Car 0→20 m/s in 8 s → a=2.5 m/s², s=80 m.
Example 3.4: Plane lands 40 m/s, a=-1.5 m/s² → stop distance 533 m, time 26.7 s.

Stopping distance

\(s_{braking} = \frac{v^2}{2a} = \frac{v^2}{2\mu g}\) ; total stopping = reaction distance + braking = \(v t_r + \frac{v^2}{2\mu g}\)

⬇️ 3.4 Vertical Motion / Free Fall

Acceleration \(g = 9.8\,\text{m/s}^2\) downward. Equations same with \(a = \pm g\).

  • Upward: \(v = u - gt\), \(y = ut - \frac12 gt^2\), \(v^2 = u^2 - 2gy\)
  • Downward: \(v = u + gt\), \(y = ut + \frac12 gt^2\)
Example 3.8: Particle thrown up at 20 m/s from 15 m building. Times to reach various levels – quadratic solutions.
Terminal velocity: When drag balances weight → constant velocity.

🔄 3.5 Uniform Circular Motion

Angular quantities

\(\theta = \frac{s}{r}\) (rad), \(\omega = \frac{\Delta \theta}{\Delta t}\) (rad/s), \(v_t = \omega r\)

Centripetal acceleration

\(a_c = \frac{v^2}{r} = \omega^2 r\) (always toward center)

Centripetal force

\(F_c = m a_c = m\frac{v^2}{r}\) – provided by tension, friction, banking, etc.

Example 3.9: ω=40 rad/s, r=0.3 m → v_t=12 m/s.
Example 3.10: Car 14 m/s, r=20 m → ω=0.7 rad/s, T=8.97 s.
Example 3.14: Curve μ=0.7, r=40 m → v_max=√(μ g r)=16.7 m/s.

📌 Key Equations Summary

1D motion: \(v=u+at,\; s=ut+\frac12 at^2,\; v^2=u^2+2as\)
Free fall: \(g=9.8\) m/s²
Circular: \(a_c=v^2/r\), \(v=\omega r\), \(F_c=mv^2/r\)

🎯 40 Advanced Motion Problems

Q1
A car accelerates from rest at 2 m/s² for 5 s, then continues at constant speed for 8 s, then decelerates at 1.5 m/s² until stop. Find total distance travelled.
✅ solution: Stage1: s1 = 0·5 + ½·2·25 = 25 m, v1=10 m/s. Stage2: s2 = 10·8 = 80 m. Stage3: t3 = 10/1.5 = 6.667 s, s3 = 10·6.667 - ½·1.5·(6.667)² = 33.33 m. Total = 25+80+33.33 = 138.33 m.
Q2
A stone is thrown vertically upward with 15 m/s from a cliff 50 m high. How long does it take to hit the ground below? (g=9.8 m/s²)
✅ solution: Use s = ut - ½gt² with s = -50 m, u=+15. -50 = 15t -4.9t² → 4.9t² -15t -50 =0 → t = [15±√(225+980)]/9.8 = [15±√1205]/9.8; √1205≈34.7; positive t = (15+34.7)/9.8 = 5.07 s.
Q3
A body is projected vertically up with velocity 20 m/s. At what time will its kinetic energy be half of the initial kinetic energy? (g=10 m/s²)
✅ solution: Initial KE = ½m(20)². Half KE means v² = 200 → v = ±√200 ≈ ±14.14 m/s. v = 20 - gt → 14.14 = 20 -10t → t=0.586 s (up) ; or -14.14 = 20-10t → t=3.414 s (down). Both possible.
Q4
A car moving at 30 m/s applies brakes and stops after 5 s. Find (a) deceleration, (b) stopping distance.
✅ solution: (a) a = (0-30)/5 = -6 m/s². (b) s = 30·5 + ½(-6)·25 = 150 -75 = 75 m.
Q5
A ball is dropped from a height of 80 m. At the same instant, another ball is thrown upward from ground with 40 m/s. When and where do they meet? (g=10 m/s²)
✅ solution: Ball A: y = 80 - 5t². Ball B: y = 40t -5t². Set equal: 80 -5t² = 40t -5t² → 80 = 40t → t=2 s. Height: y = 80-20 = 60 m above ground.
Q6
A particle moves along x‑axis with acceleration a = 2t (m/s²). At t=0, x=0, v=4 m/s. Find velocity and position at t=3 s.
✅ solution: a = dv/dt = 2t → v = ∫2t dt = t² + C; v(0)=4 → C=4 → v = t²+4. At t=3, v=9+4=13 m/s. x = ∫v dt = ∫(t²+4)dt = t³/3 +4t + D; x(0)=0 → D=0 → x = t³/3+4t; at t=3, x=27/3+12=9+12=21 m.
Q7
A stone is thrown horizontally at 15 m/s from a 100 m high cliff. How far from the base does it land? (g=10 m/s²)
✅ solution: Time to fall: 100 = ½·10·t² → t = √20 ≈ 4.472 s. Horizontal distance = 15·4.472 = 67.08 m.
Q8
A ball is kicked at 20 m/s, 30° above horizontal. Find (a) max height, (b) time of flight, (c) range. (g=10)
✅ solution: ux=20cos30=17.32 m/s, uy=20sin30=10 m/s. (a) H = uy²/2g = 100/20 = 5 m. (b) T = 2uy/g = 20/10 = 2 s. (c) R = ux·T = 17.32·2 = 34.64 m.
Q9
A train starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 0.5 m/s². What distance does it cover in the 10th second of motion?
✅ solution: Distance in nth second: s_n = u + a/2 (2n-1). Here u=0, a=0.5, n=10 → s_10 = 0 + 0.25(19) = 4.75 m.
Q10
A stone is thrown vertically upward and returns to the thrower in 6 s. Find (a) initial velocity, (b) max height. (g=10)
✅ solution: Total time T = 2u/g → 6 = 2u/10 → u = 30 m/s. H = u²/2g = 900/20 = 45 m.
Q11
A car moving at 20 m/s overtakes another car moving at 15 m/s. The faster car brakes at 2 m/s². How far must it travel to be 50 m ahead of the slower car? (slower maintains speed)
✅ solution: Relative initial speed = 5 m/s. Deceleration relative = -2 m/s². Required relative displacement = 50 m. Using s_rel = u_rel t + ½ a_rel t² → 50 = 5t - t² → t² -5t +50=0 → discriminant negative → impossible with constant braking. Max relative displacement occurs when relative velocity zero: 5 -2t=0 → t=2.5 s, s_rel =5·2.5 -1·(2.5)² =12.5 -6.25=6.25 m max. So never 50 m.
Q12
A stone is thrown horizontally from the top of a building 45 m high. If it strikes the ground at an angle of 45°, find the initial speed. (g=10)
✅ solution: Time to fall: t = √(2h/g)=√9=3 s. Final vertical velocity vy = gt = 30 m/s. Strike angle 45° means tan45 = vy/vx =1 → vx = vy = 30 m/s. So initial horizontal speed = 30 m/s.
Q13
A particle moves in a circle of radius 2 m with constant speed 4 m/s. Find centripetal acceleration and period.
✅ solution: a_c = v²/r = 16/2 = 8 m/s². Period T = 2πr/v = 4π/4 = π ≈ 3.14 s.
Q14
A 0.5 kg ball is whirled in a horizontal circle at the end of a 1 m string. If the string can withstand a maximum tension of 50 N, what is the maximum speed possible?
✅ solution: T_max = m v²/r → v² = T_max·r/m = 50·1/0.5 = 100 → v = 10 m/s.
Q15
A car travels on a banked curve of radius 80 m at 30 m/s. What should be the banking angle so that no friction is needed? (g=10)
✅ solution: tanθ = v²/(rg) = 900/(80·10)=900/800=1.125 → θ = 48.4°.
Q16
A stone is dropped from a height h. It covers the last 30 m in 1 s. Find h. (g=10)
✅ solution: Let t be total time. Then ½gt² - ½g(t-1)² = 30 → 5[t² - (t² -2t+1)] =30 → 5(2t-1)=30 → 10t -5 =30 → 10t=35 → t=3.5 s. h = ½·10·(3.5)² =5·12.25=61.25 m.
Q17
Two trains, each 100 m long, are running on parallel tracks. One overtakes the other in 10 s when they go in same direction, and crosses in 5 s when opposite. Find speeds.
✅ solution: Let v1>v2. Same direction: relative speed = v1-v2, distance = 200 m → v1-v2 = 200/10 =20. Opposite: relative speed = v1+v2, distance = 200 m → v1+v2 = 200/5 =40. Solve: v1=30 m/s, v2=10 m/s.
Q18
A car accelerates from rest at constant a for 5 s, then constant speed for 10 s, then decelerates at 2 m/s² to stop. Total distance = 500 m. Find a.
✅ solution: Stage1: s1=½a·25=12.5a, v1=5a. Stage2: s2=5a·10=50a. Stage3: t3 = (5a)/2 =2.5a, s3= v1·t3 -½·2·t3² =5a·2.5a - (2.5a)² =12.5a² -6.25a²=6.25a². Total: 12.5a+50a+6.25a² = 62.5a +6.25a² =500 → divide 6.25: 10a + a² =80 → a²+10a-80=0 → a = [-10 ± √(100+320)]/2 = (-10+20.49)/2 =5.245 m/s².
Q19
A ball is thrown horizontally at 10 m/s from a height of 20 m. What is the magnitude of velocity just before striking the ground? (g=10)
✅ solution: Time t = √(2h/g)=√4=2 s. vy = gt =20 m/s. vx constant =10. Final speed = √(10²+20²)=√500=22.36 m/s.
Q20
A particle moves in a circle of radius 5 m with angular velocity ω = 4t rad/s. Find centripetal acceleration at t=2 s.
✅ solution: ω=4·2=8 rad/s. v=ωr=40 m/s. a_c = v²/r =1600/5=320 m/s². (or a_c = ω²r =64·5=320).
Q21
A stone is thrown upward at 30 m/s. Find the distance travelled in the 4th second. (g=10)
✅ solution: For nth second upward: s_n = u - g/2 (2n-1). n=4 → s_4 = 30 -5·7 =30-35 = -5 m. Negative means it has come back down past the start? Actually formula works for upward motion until apex; at t=3.06 s apex, so 4th second includes downward. Better use displacement: position at t=3: 30·3 -5·9=90-45=45 m; at t=4: 120-80=40 m. Distance travelled in 4th second = |40-45| =5 m downward. So distance =5 m.
Q22
A body is projected at angle θ such that its horizontal range equals maximum height. Find tanθ.
✅ solution: Range R = u²sin2θ/g, max height H = u²sin²θ/(2g). Set R = H → (u²·2sinθcosθ)/g = u² sin²θ/(2g) → 4cosθ = sinθ → tanθ = 4.
Q23
A motorcyclist goes around a vertical circle of radius 5 m inside a well of death. What minimum speed at the top to avoid falling? (g=10)
✅ solution: At top, mg + N = mv²/r. Minimum speed when N=0 → v² = rg =5·10=50 → v≈7.07 m/s.
Q24
A stone is dropped into a well and the splash is heard after 3.91 s. If speed of sound is 340 m/s, find depth of well. (g=10)
✅ solution: t1 = fall time = √(2h/g), t2 = h/340. t1+t2 = 3.91. Let h = 5t1². Then t1 + 5t1²/340 = 3.91 → t1 + (5/340)t1² = 3.91 → multiply 340: 340t1 +5t1² = 1329.4 → 5t1²+340t1-1329.4=0 → t1≈3.0 s (positive root). Then h = 5·9=45 m.
Q25
A particle is projected with 25 m/s at 53° (sin53=0.8, cos53=0.6). Find radius of curvature at the highest point.
✅ solution: At top, v = ucosθ =15 m/s. Normal acceleration = g. Radius ρ = v²/g =225/10=22.5 m.
Q26
A car accelerates from rest at 3 m/s² for 6 s, then at 2 m/s² for 4 s. Find final velocity and total distance.
✅ solution: Stage1: v1=3·6=18 m/s, s1=½·3·36=54 m. Stage2: v2=18+2·4=26 m/s, s2=18·4+½·2·16=72+16=88 m. Total s=142 m, v=26 m/s.
Q27
A stone is thrown vertically up with 20 m/s. At what height will its speed be half of its initial?
✅ solution: v² = u² -2gh. v=10 → 100=400-20h → 20h=300 → h=15 m.
Q28
A wheel of radius 0.5 m accelerates from rest to 10 rad/s in 4 s. Find angular acceleration and tangential acceleration at rim.
✅ solution: α = Δω/Δt =10/4=2.5 rad/s². a_t = αr = 2.5·0.5 =1.25 m/s².
Q29
A ball is thrown at 40° to horizontal. It reaches maximum height in 2 s. Find initial speed and range. (g=10)
✅ solution: uy = u sin40. Time to top = u sin40/g = 2 → u sin40 = 20 → u =20/0.643=31.1 m/s. Range R = u² sin80 /g = (967·0.985)/10 ≈95.3 m.
Q30
A particle is projected so that its horizontal range is 4 times the maximum height. Find projection angle.
✅ solution: R = 4H → (2u²sinθcosθ)/g = 4(u²sin²θ)/(2g) → cancel u²/g: 2sinθcosθ = 2 sin²θ → cosθ = sinθ → tanθ=1 → θ=45°.
Q31
A 0.2 kg ball is whirled in a vertical circle radius 0.5 m with constant speed 4 m/s. Find tension at the lowest point. (g=10)
✅ solution: At bottom, T - mg = mv²/r → T = m(g + v²/r) = 0.2(10 + 16/0.5)=0.2(10+32)=0.2·42=8.4 N.
Q32
A car moving at 20 m/s sees an obstacle 100 m ahead and decelerates at 2 m/s². Will it stop in time?
✅ solution: stopping distance = v²/(2a) = 400/4 =100 m. Exactly at the obstacle. It stops just at it.
Q33
A stone is thrown horizontally at 12 m/s from a height 20 m. Find the speed after 1 s. (g=10)
✅ solution: vy = gt =10 m/s. vx=12. speed = √(144+100)=√244=15.62 m/s.
Q34
A car travels around a circular track of radius 100 m at constant speed 30 m/s. Find centripetal acceleration and the minimum coefficient of friction needed to avoid skidding. (g=10)
✅ solution: a_c = v²/r = 900/100 =9 m/s². μ_min = a_c/g = 0.9.
Q35
A stone is thrown at angle 30° with speed 20 m/s. Find the time when velocity becomes perpendicular to initial velocity.
✅ solution: Initial velocity vector: (u cos30, u sin30). At time t, v = (u cos30, u sin30 - gt). Perpendicular condition: dot =0 → u²cos²30 + u sin30(u sin30 - gt)=0 → u²(cos²30+ sin²30) - u sin30 g t =0 → u² - u g t sin30 =0 → t = u/(g sin30) = 20/(10·0.5)=20/5=4 s.
Q36
A particle moves in x-y plane with x=3t, y=4t-5t² (SI). Find velocity and acceleration at t=2 s.
✅ solution: vx=3, vy=4-10t → at t=2, vy=4-20=-16, so v=(3,-16) m/s. ax=0, ay=-10, so a=(0,-10) m/s².
Q37
A stone is thrown upward with speed u. Another stone is thrown downward from same height with same speed u simultaneously. Find time when they meet.
✅ solution: They start at same point? If both from same point, they never meet (one goes up, one down). If one from ground up, other from height H down: up: y1=ut-½gt², down: y2=H-ut-½gt². Set equal: ut = H-ut → t=H/(2u). Requires H. Without H, cannot determine. Usually such problem provides meeting height or time.
Q38
A ball is dropped from a height of 5 m onto a concrete floor and rebounds to 3.2 m. Find coefficient of restitution.
✅ solution: Speed just before impact: v = √(2gh) = √(100)=10 m/s. Speed just after: u = √(2g·3.2)=√64=8 m/s. e = u/v = 0.8.
Q39
A projectile covers range R when fired at 30°. For same initial speed, find other angle that gives same range.
✅ solution: Range same for complementary angles → 60°.
Q40
A particle moves in a circle radius 2 m with angular acceleration α=3 rad/s². At t=0, ω=2 rad/s. Find tangential and centripetal acceleration at t=2 s.
✅ solution: ω = 2 + 3·2 = 8 rad/s. a_t = α r = 3·2 =6 m/s². a_c = ω²r =64·2=128 m/s².
Q41
A 5 kg block compresses a spring 0.3 m (k=1000 N/m) and is released on a frictionless surface. Find speed when spring is uncompressed.
✅ solution: ½kA² = ½mv² → v = A√(k/m)=0.3√(1000/5)=0.3√200=0.3·14.14=4.24 m/s.
Q42
A 50 kg person jumps from 1.5 m and lands with knees bent, stopping in 0.3 s. Find average force on legs. (g=10)
✅ solution: v before = √(2·10·1.5)=√30≈5.477 m/s. Δp = mΔv = 50·5.477 =273.85 kg·m/s. F_avg = Δp/Δt = 273.85/0.3 =912.8 N upward. Add weight during stop? Actually net force = F_avg - mg = Δp/Δt, so F_avg = mg + Δp/Δt = 500 + 912.8 =1412.8 N.
Q43
A 4 kg block on frictionless table is connected to a 2 kg hanging mass. Find acceleration.
✅ solution: For hanging: 20 - T =2a; for 4 kg: T=4a. Substitute: 20 -4a=2a → 20=6a → a=3.33 m/s².
Q44
A 2000 kg truck accelerates from 10 m/s to 20 m/s over 100 m. Find average net force.
✅ solution: v²=u²+2as → 400=100+2a·100 → 200a=300 → a=1.5 m/s². F=ma=2000·1.5=3000 N.
Q45
A 0.2 kg puck slides on frictionless ice at 4 m/s and hits a stationary 0.3 kg puck elastically. Find final velocities.
✅ solution: v1 = (m1-m2)/(m1+m2) u1 = (-0.1/0.5)·4 = -0.8 m/s; v2 = 2m1/(m1+m2) u1 = (0.4/0.5)·4 =3.2 m/s.
Q46
A 3 kg mass moves in a circle radius 2 m with constant speed 6 m/s. Find centripetal force.
✅ solution: F = mv²/r = 3·36/2 = 54 N.
Q47
A 50 N force pulls a 10 kg block at 30° for 5 m. Find work done.
✅ solution: W = F s cosθ = 50·5·0.866=216.5 J.
Q48
A 0.5 kg ball is swung in a vertical circle radius 1 m with speed 4 m/s at bottom. Find tension at bottom.
✅ solution: T - mg = mv²/r → T = 0.5·10 + 0.5·16/1 =5+8=13 N.
Q49
A 2 kg mass at (1,4) and a 3 kg mass at (5,2). Find center of mass.
✅ solution: x_cm = (2·1+3·5)/5 = (2+15)/5=17/5=3.4; y_cm = (2·4+3·2)/5 = (8+6)/5=14/5=2.8.
Q50
A force F= (4i+5j) N acts on a 2 kg mass. Find acceleration vector.
✅ solution: a = F/m = (2i+2.5j) m/s².
Q51
A 10 kg box is pushed with 60 N at 20° below horizontal. μk=0.2. Find acceleration. (g=10, cos20≈0.94, sin20≈0.342)
✅ solution: Fx=60·0.94=56.4; Fy downward =60·0.342=20.52. F_N = mg + Fy =100+20.52=120.52; f_k=0.2·120.52=24.104. Net Fx =56.4-24.104=32.296; a=3.23 m/s².
Q52
A 0.3 kg ball is thrown at 20 m/s at 60°. Find potential energy at top.
✅ solution: H = (u² sin²θ)/(2g)= (400·0.75)/20 =300/20=15 m. PE = mgh = 0.3·10·15=45 J.
Q53
A 2 kg mass compresses a spring 0.1 m (k=800 N/m). Find speed when spring is half compressed (0.05 m).
✅ solution: Energy: ½kA² = ½kx² + ½mv² → ½·800·0.01 = ½·800·0.0025 + ½·2·v² → 4 = 1 + v² → v²=3 → v=1.732 m/s.
Q54
A 0.5 kg ball moving at 10 m/s strikes a wall at 30° and rebounds with same speed at same angle. Find impulse magnitude. (Component perpendicular to wall changes)
✅ solution: Perpendicular component v_perp = 10 sin30=5 m/s. Change = 5 - (-5)=10 m/s. Δp = 0.5·10=5 kg·m/s (impulse perpendicular to wall).
Q55
A 4 kg block on frictionless table is tied to a string passing over pulley to a 1 kg hanging mass. Find acceleration.
✅ solution: 1·10 - T =1a; T=4a → 10 =5a → a=2 m/s².
Q56
A 1500 kg car climbs a 5° slope at constant speed 25 m/s. Find power if μk=0.05. (g=10, sin5≈0.087, cos5≈0.996)
✅ solution: F = mg sinθ + μ mg cosθ =15000·0.087 + 0.05·15000·0.996 =1305 + 747 =2052 N. P = Fv = 2052·25 =51300 W.
Q57
A 2 kg object has KE 36 J. Find momentum.
✅ solution: ½mv²=36 → v²=36 → v=6 m/s. p = mv =12 kg·m/s.
Q58
A 0.2 kg ball is thrown at 12 m/s and caught with a glove that recoils 0.05 m. Find average stopping force.
✅ solution: v²=u²+2as → 0=144+2a·0.05 → a = -1440 m/s². F = ma = 0.2·1440=288 N.
Q59
A 0.5 kg object moving at 4 m/s collides inelastically with a 1 kg object at rest and they stick. Find final speed and energy loss.
✅ solution: p_i = 0.5·4=2; final mass 1.5 kg; v=2/1.5=1.333 m/s. KE_i = 0.5·0.5·16=4 J; KE_f = 0.5·1.5·(1.333)²=0.75·1.777=1.333 J; loss = 2.667 J.
Q60
A force \(F = 5t\) N acts on a 2 kg mass initially at rest. Find power at t=3 s.
✅ solution: a = F/m = 2.5t; dv/dt=2.5t → v = 1.25t². At t=3, v=1.25·9=11.25 m/s; F=15 N. P = F·v = 15·11.25 =168.75 W.
