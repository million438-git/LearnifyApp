⚡ Introduction

This unit covers electrostatics (charges, Coulomb's law, electric field, potential) and electric circuits (current, resistance, Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's rules, capacitors). Fundamental for entrance exams.

🔋 6.1 Coulomb's Law

Properties of charges

  • Like charges repel, unlike attract.
  • Charge is conserved: \(q_{total} = constant\).
  • Charge is quantized: \(q = ne\) where \(e = 1.6\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}\).

Coulomb's law

\(F = k\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}\) with \(k = 8.99\times10^9\ \mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}\)

Vector form: \(\vec{F}_{12} = k\frac{q_1q_2}{r^2}\hat{r}_{12}\)

Example 6.2: Two charges \(-1.0\times10^{-10}\) C each, 1 cm apart → repulsive force \(9.0\times10^{-7}\) N.

Multiple charges

Superposition principle: net force = vector sum of individual forces.

🧲 6.2 Electric Fields

Electric field definition

\(\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q}\) (test charge). For point charge: \(E = k\frac{|q|}{r^2}\) direction away from +q, toward -q.

Electric field lines

Start on +, end on -; density indicates strength; never cross.

Electric flux

\(\Phi = \vec{E}\cdot\vec{A} = EA\cos\theta\)

Gauss's law: \(\Phi = \frac{Q_{inside}}{\epsilon_0}\)

Example 6.4: E due to 2.0 nC at 5.0 mm = \(7.2\times10^5\) N/C away from charge.

🔌 6.3 Electric Potential

Electric potential energy & potential

\(U = k\frac{qQ}{r}\) , \(V = \frac{U}{q} = k\frac{Q}{r}\)

Potential difference: \(\Delta V = V_B - V_A = -\int_A^B \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l}\)

For uniform field: \(\Delta V = E d\)

Equipotential surfaces

Surfaces of constant V; perpendicular to E; no work moving along them.

Example 6.7: V from 1 μC at 1 mm = \(9\times10^6\) V = 9 MV.

⚡ 6.4 Current, Resistance, Ohm's Law

Electric current

\(I = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}\) (A). Direction = flow of positive charge.

Current density

\(J = \frac{I}{A} = \sigma E = n e v_d\)

Resistance & resistivity

\(R = \rho \frac{L}{A}\) ; Ohm's law: \(V = IR\) (for ohmic materials).

Example 6.10: I=6 mA, A=12 mm² → J = 500 A/m².

🔗 6.5 Resistors in series & parallel

Series

\(R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2 + \dots\) ; same current, voltage divides.

Parallel

\(\frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \dots\) ; same voltage, current divides.

Example 6.12: 5Ω, 4Ω||12Ω, 8Ω → R_eq = 5+3+8=16Ω; currents: 1.5 A through 5Ω, 8Ω; 1.125 A through 4Ω, 0.375 A through 12Ω.

📦 6.6 Capacitors

Capacitance

\(C = \frac{Q}{V}\) (F). Parallel plate: \(C = \epsilon_0 \frac{A}{d}\) with dielectric \(C = \kappa C_0\).

Series/parallel capacitors

Parallel: \(C_{eq} = C_1 + C_2 + \dots\) ; Series: \(\frac{1}{C_{eq}} = \frac{1}{C_1} + \frac{1}{C_2} + \dots\)

Energy stored

\(U = \frac{1}{2}CV^2 = \frac{Q^2}{2C}\)

Example 6.19: A=200 cm², d=2 mm, κ=6, V=12V → C=5.31×10⁻¹⁰ F, Q=6.37 nC.

🔄 6.7 Kirchhoff's Rules

Junction rule

Sum of currents entering = sum leaving (charge conservation).

Loop rule

Sum of potential changes around any closed loop = 0 (energy conservation).

Example 6.17: Multi‑loop circuit → solve simultaneous equations for I₁, I₂, I₃.

📌 Key Equations

\(F = k\frac{q_1q_2}{r^2}\) ; \(E = \frac{F}{q} = k\frac{Q}{r^2}\)
\(V = k\frac{Q}{r}\) ; \(\Delta V = E d\)
\(I = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}\) ; \(V = IR\) ; \(R = \rho \frac{L}{A}\)
\(C = \frac{Q}{V} = \epsilon_0\frac{A}{d}\) ; \(C_{series}\): \(\frac{1}{C}=\sum\frac{1}{C_i}\) ; \(C_{parallel}=\sum C_i\)
Kirchhoff: \(\sum I_{in}= \sum I_{out}\) ; \(\sum \Delta V = 0\)

🎯 70 Electrostatics & Circuits Problems

Q1
Two charges \(q_1 = +2\mu C\) and \(q_2 = -3\mu C\) are 10 cm apart. Find the force on each (magnitude and direction).
✅ solution: \(F = k\frac{|q_1q_2|}{r^2} = 8.99\times10^9 \frac{2\times10^{-6}\cdot3\times10^{-6}}{0.1^2} = 8.99\times10^9 \frac{6\times10^{-12}}{0.01} = 8.99\times10^9 \times 6\times10^{-10} = 5.394\) N. Attractive, so force on q₁ is toward q₂, on q₂ toward q₁.
Q2
Three charges \(q_1 = +4\mu C\) at (0,0), \(q_2 = -2\mu C\) at (0.3 m, 0), \(q_3 = +5\mu C\) at (0,0.4 m). Find net force on q₁.
✅ solution: F₁₂ (repulsive on q₁? q₁+ q₂- → attractive, so toward q₂): magnitude \(k·4e-6·2e-6/0.3² = 8.99e9·8e-12/0.09 = 8.99e9·8.888e-11 = 0.799\) N in +x. F₁₃ (repulsive because both +): \(k·4e-6·5e-6/0.4² = 8.99e9·20e-12/0.16 = 8.99e9·1.25e-10 = 1.124\) N in +y. Net force: \(\sqrt{0.799²+1.124²} = 1.38\) N at θ = arctan(1.124/0.799) = 54.6° above +x.
Q3
What is the electric field at (0,0) due to a charge \(Q = +6\mu C\) placed at (0.2 m, 0)?
✅ solution: \(E = kQ/r² = 8.99e9·6e-6/0.04 = 8.99e9·1.5e-4 = 1.3485\times10^6\) N/C directed away from Q, so along -x direction (since Q is at +x).
Q4
Two point charges \(q_1 = +5nC\) at x=0, \(q_2 = -5nC\) at x=10 cm. Find E at x=5 cm.
✅ solution: At midpoint, fields from both are in same direction (toward negative charge). E₁ = k·5e-9/(0.05)² = 8.99e9·5e-9/0.0025 = 8.99e9·2e-6 = 17980 N/C (away from q₁, so right). E₂ = same magnitude but toward q₂ (also right). Total = 35960 N/C right.
Q5
A proton is placed in a uniform electric field of 500 N/C. Find its acceleration. (\(m_p = 1.67\times10^{-27}\) kg, \(q_p = 1.6\times10^{-19}\) C)
✅ solution: \(F = qE = 1.6e-19·500 = 8e-17\) N. \(a = F/m = 8e-17 / 1.67e-27 = 4.79\times10^{10}\) m/s².
Q6
A 2 μC charge is placed in an electric field \(\vec{E} = (3\hat{i} + 4\hat{j})\times10^3\) N/C. Find the force vector on it.
✅ solution: \(\vec{F} = q\vec{E} = 2\times10^{-6}(3000\hat{i} + 4000\hat{j}) = (6\times10^{-3}\hat{i} + 8\times10^{-3}\hat{j})\) N.
Q7
Find the electric potential at the midpoint of the two charges in Q4.
✅ solution: \(V = kq_1/r_1 + kq_2/r_2 = 8.99e9·5e-9/0.05 + 8.99e9·(-5e-9)/0.05 = 0\).
Q8
How much work is required to bring a +2 μC charge from infinity to a point 20 cm from a +5 μC charge?
✅ solution: \(W = qV = 2e-6 · k·5e-6/0.2 = 2e-6·8.99e9·5e-6/0.2 = 2e-6·(44.95e3/0.2?) \) Actually \(kqQ/r = 8.99e9·5e-6·2e-6/0.2 = 8.99e9·1e-11/0.2 = 8.99e9·5e-11 = 0.4495\) J. So W = 0.45 J.
Q9

✅ solution: \(I = V/R = 10/5 = 2\) A. \(P = I^2R = 4·5 = 20\) W, also \(P=VI=10·2=20\) W.
Q10

Q26

Q33

Q38

Q41

Q42

✅ solution: Parallel: 1/Rp = 1/20+1/30 = 0.05+0.03333=0.08333 → Rp=12 Ω. Total = 12+60=72 Ω.
Q60
A 2 μF and 3 μF in parallel are in series with a 5 μF. Find total C.
✅ solution: Parallel first: 2+3=5 μF. Then series with 5: 1/C = 1/5+1/5 = 0.2+0.2=0.4 → C = 2.5 μF.
Q61
A 10 V battery with internal resistance 1 Ω is connected to a parallel combination of 2 Ω and 3 Ω. Find current through 2 Ω.
✅ solution: Parallel: 1/Rp = 1/2+1/3 = 0.5+0.3333=0.83333 → Rp=1.2 Ω. Total R = 1.2+1=2.2 Ω. I_total = 10/2.2 = 4.545 A. V_parallel = I_total·1.2 = 5.4545 V. I₂ = 5.4545/2 = 2.727 A.

✅ solution: Distance from center to corner = half diagonal = 0.2·√2/2 = 0.1414 m. V = 4·k·2e-6/0.1414 = 4·8.99e9·2e-6/0.1414 = 4·(17980/0.1414?) Actually 8.99e9·2e-6 = 17980, /0.1414 = 127140, times 4 = 508560 V = 508.6 kV.
Q63
A 5 μF capacitor is charged to 100 V and then connected across a 3 μF uncharged capacitor. Find final voltage and energy lost.
✅ solution: Q_initial = 5·100 = 500 μC. C_total = 8 μF. V = 500/8 = 62.5 V. Initial energy = 0.5·5e-6·10000 = 0.025 J. Final = 0.5·8e-6·3906.25 = 0.015625 J. Loss = 0.009375 J.
Q64
A wire of length L, radius r has resistance R. If length is doubled and radius halved, find new resistance.
✅ solution: R = ρL/A. New L' = 2L, new A' = π(r/2)² = A/4. So R' = ρ·2L/(A/4) = 8·ρL/A = 8R.
Q65

Q66
A 100 μF capacitor is charged to 50 V and then discharged through a 10 kΩ resistor. Find time constant and initial current.
✅ solution: τ = RC = 10000·100e-6 = 1 s. I_initial = V/R = 50/10000 = 5 mA.
Q67
A 2 μC charge is at (0,0), a -3 μC at (0.3 m, 0). Find point on x-axis where V=0.
✅ solution: Let x be coordinate. For x between 0 and 0.3, potentials opposite sign cancel. k·2/x + k·(-3)/(0.3-x) = 0 → 2/x = 3/(0.3-x) → 2(0.3-x) = 3x → 0.6 - 2x = 3x → 0.6 = 5x → x=0.12 m. For x>0.3, both positive? Actually -3 gives negative, but distance (x-0.3) so equation 2/x = 3/(x-0.3) → 2(x-0.3)=3x → 2x-0.6=3x → -0.6=x impossible. So only x=0.12 m.
Q68
A 10 Ω and 20 Ω are in parallel with a 30 Ω in series with this combination. All connected to 24 V. Find power in 20 Ω.
✅ solution: Parallel: 1/Rp = 1/10+1/20 = 0.1+0.05=0.15 → Rp=6.667 Ω. Total R = 6.667+30=36.667 Ω. I_total = 24/36.667 = 0.6545 A. V_parallel = 0.6545·6.667 = 4.3636 V. I₂₀ = 4.3636/20 = 0.21818 A. P₂₀ = I²·20 = 0.04762·20 = 0.9524 W.
Q69
A 4 μF capacitor is charged to 60 V and then connected across an uncharged 6 μF capacitor. Find final charge on 4 μF.
✅ solution: Initial Q = 4·60 = 240 μC. Final V = 240/10 = 24 V. Q₄ = 4·24 = 96 μC.
Q70
A 1.5 V cell with internal resistance 0.5 Ω is short‑circuited. Find short‑circuit current and power dissipated internally.
✅ solution: I_sc = 1.5/0.5 = 3 A. Power dissipated = I²r = 9·0.5 = 4.5 W.
