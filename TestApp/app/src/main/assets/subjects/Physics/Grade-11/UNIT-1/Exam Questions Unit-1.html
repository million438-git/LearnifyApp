📘 Unit overview: Physics in human society

Physics is the fundamental science that explains how the universe behaves. Its laws govern motion, energy, electricity, and waves. These principles are not just academic – they directly shape modern society through technology, medicine, communication, and industry. Understanding the connection between physics and everyday life is essential for responsible innovation and global development.

📌 REMEMBER: Every technological advance – from smartphones to MRI machines – is built on physics concepts.
🌟 WOW: Without physics, we would have no electricity, no internet, and no space exploration!
Q1 (conceptual)
Physics is best described as:
  • A) A collection of formulas
  • B) A study of machines only
  • C) A science that explains natural laws and their applications
  • D) A subject for engineers only
Correct answer: C
Physics explains how nature works using laws such as motion, energy, and force. These laws are then applied to technology, medicine, transport, and industry. It is not limited to formulas or one profession.

Technology is built using physical laws such as electricity, mechanics, and waves. Without physics, engineering and modern devices cannot exist.

Q16 (calculation)
A machine uses energy at a rate of 500 joule per second. How much energy does it use in 10 seconds?
Step-by-step:
Energy used = power × time = 500 × 10 = 5000 joule.
✅ Answer: 5000 joule
Q17 (calculation)
An electric bulb uses 100 joule each second. How much energy is used in 1 minute?
Step-by-step:
1 minute = 60 seconds
Energy = 100 × 60 = 6000 joule.
✅ Answer: 6000 joule
Q18 (calculation)
A worker does 2000 joule of work in 20 seconds. Find the power.
Step-by-step:
Power = work / time = 2000 / 20 = 100 watt.
✅ Answer: 100 watt
Q19 (application)
A motor lifts a load using 3000 joule of energy. If lifting time is reduced, what happens to power?
Answer: Power increases.
Explanation: Power depends on how fast energy is used. Less time means greater power.

Q24 (calculation)
Which uses more energy: 100 watt for 10 seconds OR 50 watt for 20 seconds?
Calculation:
First: 100 × 10 = 1000 joule
Second: 50 × 20 = 1000 joule
✅ Both use the same energy.
