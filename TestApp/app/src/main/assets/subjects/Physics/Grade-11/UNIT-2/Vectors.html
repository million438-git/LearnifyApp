🔬 introduction

Many physical quantities have both magnitude and direction – force, velocity, displacement. This unit develops ways to describe and operate with vectors in two dimensions.

  • familiarize with basic principles of vector operations
  • interpret phenomena using the vector concept
  • develop problem‑solving skills with vectors
📌 2.1 vectors & types

scalar vs vector

  • Scalar: mass, speed, distance – only magnitude.
  • Vector: force, velocity, acceleration, displacement – magnitude + direction.

representation

Arrow: length = magnitude (scale), tip = direction.

[diagram: vector arrow with scale]

types of vectors

  • Parallel vectors: same direction.
  • Antiparallel vectors: opposite directions.
  • Equal vectors: same magnitude and direction.
  • Collinear vectors: lie along same line or parallel lines.
  • Co‑planar vectors: lie in the same plane.
  • Zero vector: magnitude zero, starting point coincides with terminal point.
  • Orthogonal vectors: perpendicular.
  • Unit vector: magnitude = 1.
  • Negative of a vector: same magnitude, opposite direction.
Example 2.1: Find the negative of a displacement 60 km toward North.
Answer: 60 km toward South.
✏️ 2.2 graphical addition

triangle law

Place vectors head to tail; resultant from tail of first to head of last.

parallelogram law

Place vectors tail to tail; diagonal from common tail to opposite corner = resultant.

polygon law

For more than two vectors: chain head‑to‑tail; resultant from start to end.

subtraction

\(\vec{A} - \vec{B} = \vec{A} + (-\vec{B})\)

🧮 2.3 algebraic (component) method

collinear vectors

Same direction → add magnitudes; opposite → subtract, direction of larger.

perpendicular vectors

\(R = \sqrt{A^2 + B^2}\) ; direction \(\theta = \tan^{-1}(B/A)\)

components

\(A_x = A\cos\theta\) , \(A_y = A\sin\theta\) (if \(\theta\) from +x axis).

\(\vec{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j}\)

resultant by components

\(R_x = \sum A_x\) , \(R_y = \sum A_y\)
\(R = \sqrt{R_x^2 + R_y^2}\) , \(\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{R_y}{R_x}\right)\) (watch quadrant).

unit vectors

\(\hat{i}\) , \(\hat{j}\) along +x, +y.
\(\vec{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j}\)
Unit vector in direction of \(\vec{A}\): \(\hat{u}_A = \frac{\vec{A}}{A}\)

Example 2.5: Force 35 N at 40° above negative x‑axis → components: F_x = 35cos40° = 26.8 N (negative direction? careful: angle measured from negative x, so F_x = -35cos40° = -26.8 N, F_y = 35sin40° = +22.5 N.)
Example 2.6: A = 100 m at 50°, B = 200 m at 150° (from +x). Find R.
A_x = 64.3, A_y = 76.6 ; B_x = -173.2, B_y = 100 → R_x = -108.9, R_y = 176.6 → R = 207.5 m, θ = 58.3° above negative x.
⚫ 2.4 dot (scalar) product

definition

\(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B} = AB\cos\theta\) (scalar)

Also \(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B} = A_xB_x + A_yB_y\)

Angle between vectors: \(\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}}{AB}\)

applications

Work \(W = \vec{F}\cdot\vec{s}\) ; Power \(P = \vec{F}\cdot\vec{v}\)

Example 2.13: \(\vec{A}=\hat{i}+\hat{j}\), \(\vec{B}=-2\hat{i}+3\hat{j}\) → dot = 1, A=√2, B=√13 → cosθ = 1/(√2·√13) ≈ 0.196 → θ ≈ 78.7°.

Q1
Classify as scalar or vector: (a) 35 kg, (b) 20 m/s north, (c) 9.8 m/s² downward, (d) 100 J, (e) 15 N at 30°.
✅ answer: (a) scalar, (b) vector, (c) vector, (d) scalar, (e) vector.
Q2
Define parallel, antiparallel, and equal vectors. Give an example of each.
✅ answer: Parallel: same direction (e.g., two forces east). Antiparallel: opposite directions (e.g., 10 N east and 10 N west). Equal: same magnitude and direction (e.g., two 5 m displacements north).
Q3
What is the negative of a vector? If \(\vec{A}=30\) m/s at 60° north of east, find \(-\vec{A}\).
✅ answer: Same magnitude, opposite direction. \(-\vec{A}=30\) m/s at 60° south of west (or 240°).
Q4
State the triangle law of vector addition. Illustrate with a sketch.
✅ answer: If two vectors are represented by two sides of a triangle in sequence (head‑to‑tail), then the third side closing the triangle (from tail of first to head of second) gives the resultant vector.
Q5
Two forces 10 N and 15 N act at a point. (a) maximum possible resultant? (b) minimum? (c) if perpendicular, find magnitude and direction of resultant.
✅ answer: (a) 25 N (same direction). (b) 5 N (opposite). (c) R = √(10²+15²)=18.03 N, θ = tan⁻¹(15/10)=56.3° from the 10 N force.
Q6
A car travels 20 km due east, then 25 km due north. Find displacement (magnitude and direction).
✅ answer: R = √(20²+25²)=32.0 km, θ = tan⁻¹(25/20)=51.3° north of east.
Q7
A vector \(\vec{A}\) has components A_x = -5, A_y = 12. Find magnitude and direction (angle from +x axis).
✅ answer: A = √(25+144)=13. θ = tan⁻¹(12/|-5|) = 67.4°; since x negative, y positive → quadrant II, so θ = 180-67.4 = 112.6°.
Q8
Resolve a force of 50 N acting at 30° below the positive x‑axis into components.
✅ answer: F_x = 50 cos30° = 43.3 N, F_y = -50 sin30° = -25 N.
Q9
Given \(\vec{A}=3\hat{i}+4\hat{j}\) and \(\vec{B}=5\hat{i}-2\hat{j}\), find \(\vec{A}+\vec{B}\) and its magnitude.
✅ answer: \(\vec{A}+\vec{B}=8\hat{i}+2\hat{j}\); |R| = √(64+4)=√68=8.25 units.
Q10
Find the unit vector in the direction of \(\vec{D}=6\hat{i}-8\hat{j}\).
✅ answer: |D|=10, so \(\hat{u}=0.6\hat{i}-0.8\hat{j}\).
Q11
A vector has magnitude 12 and direction 150° from +x. Write it in unit‑vector notation.
✅ answer: 150° → cos150 = -√3/2 ≈ -0.866, sin150 = 0.5. So \(\vec{A}=12(-0.866\hat{i}+0.5\hat{j})=-10.392\hat{i}+6\hat{j}\).
Q12
If \(\vec{P}=2\hat{i}+3\hat{j}\) and \(\vec{Q}=4\hat{i}-5\hat{j}\), find \(\vec{P}-\vec{Q}\) and its magnitude.
✅ answer: \(\vec{P}-\vec{Q}=(2-4)\hat{i}+(3+5)\hat{j}=-2\hat{i}+8\hat{j}\); |R|=√(4+64)=√68=8.25.
Q13
Find the dot product of \(\vec{A}=5\hat{i}-3\hat{j}\) and \(\vec{B}=2\hat{i}+4\hat{j}\).
✅ answer: \(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}=5·2 + (-3)·4 = 10 -12 = -2\).
Q14
Two vectors have magnitudes 6 and 8, angle between them 60°. Find scalar product.
✅ answer: \(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}=6·8·\cos60° = 48·0.5 = 24\).
Q15
Find angle between \(\vec{A}=2\hat{i}+2\hat{j}\) and \(\vec{B}=4\hat{i}-3\hat{j}\).
✅ answer: dot = 8-6=2; |A|=√8=2.828, |B|=5; cosθ = 2/(2.828·5)=0.1414 → θ ≈ 81.9°.
Q16
A force \(\vec{F}=10\hat{i}+5\hat{j}\) N acts on a body that undergoes displacement \(\vec{s}=3\hat{i}+2\hat{j}\) m. Find work done.
✅ answer: W = \(\vec{F}\cdot\vec{s}=10·3+5·2=30+10=40\) J.
Q17
Find angle between \(\vec{P}=3\hat{i}+4\hat{j}\) and the positive x‑axis.
✅ answer: tanθ = 4/3 → θ = 53.13°.
Q18
Boat heads east at 4 m/s across a river flowing south at 3 m/s. Find resultant velocity (magnitude and direction).
✅ answer: R = √(4²+3²)=5 m/s, θ = tan⁻¹(3/4)=36.9° south of east.
Q19
Find a unit vector perpendicular to both \(\vec{A}=2\hat{i}+3\hat{j}\) and \(\vec{B}=4\hat{i}-5\hat{j}\). (Hint: in 2D, perpendicular = swap and change sign)
✅ answer: A vector perpendicular in 2D can be obtained by (A_y, -A_x) e.g. \(\vec{n}=3\hat{i}-2\hat{j}\) (not unit). Normalize: |n|=√13, unit = (3/√13)i - (2/√13)j. But there are two directions.
Q20
Three forces act: 10 N east, 15 N 30° north of east, and 20 N west. Find net force (components and magnitude).
✅ answer: F1=(10,0); F2=(15cos30=12.99, 15sin30=7.5); F3=(-20,0). Sum: Rx=10+12.99-20=2.99 N; Ry=0+7.5+0=7.5 N; R=√(2.99²+7.5²)=8.09 N, θ=tan⁻¹(7.5/2.99)=68.2° north of east.
Q21
If \(\vec{A}=5\hat{i}-12\hat{j}\) and \(\vec{B}=4\hat{i}+3\hat{j}\), find component of \(\vec{A}\) along \(\vec{B}\).
✅ answer: comp = \(\vec{A}\cdot\hat{B}\). \(\hat{B}=(4/5,3/5)\). Dot = 5·0.8 + (-12)·0.6 = 4 - 7.2 = -3.2.
Q22
A particle moves from (2,3) to (5,7) meters. Find displacement vector and magnitude.
✅ answer: \(\vec{d}= (5-2)\hat{i}+(7-3)\hat{j}=3\hat{i}+4\hat{j}\); magnitude 5 m.
Q23
Given \(\vec{C}=2\hat{i}+6\hat{j}\), find a vector of magnitude 10 in the direction of \(\vec{C}\).
✅ answer: |C|=√40=6.3249; unit = (0.316i+0.949j); vector = 10×unit = 3.16i+9.49j.
Q24
Find angle between \(\vec{P}=2\hat{i}-5\hat{j}\) and \(\vec{Q}=3\hat{i}+4\hat{j}\).
✅ answer: dot = 6-20 = -14; |P|=√29≈5.385; |Q|=5; cosθ = -14/(5.385·5)= -0.52 → θ ≈ 121.3°.
Q25
Under what condition is \(\vec{A}+\vec{B}=\vec{A}-\vec{B}\) ?
✅ answer: Only if \(\vec{B}=0\).
Q26
Can magnitude of a vector be zero if one of its components is not zero? Explain.
✅ answer: No, if any component ≠0, magnitude =√(sum squares) >0.
Q27
Find work done by force \(\vec{F}=20\hat{i}-10\hat{j}\) N when displacement \(\vec{s}=5\hat{i}+2\hat{j}\) m.
✅ answer: W = 20·5 + (-10)·2 = 100 -20 = 80 J.
Q28
If dot product of two vectors is zero, what can you conclude?
✅ answer: They are perpendicular (orthogonal) or one of them is zero.
Q29
Two vectors have magnitudes 5 and 8. Can their dot product be -50? Explain.
✅ answer: No, max |dot| = 5·8=40. So -50 is impossible.
Q30
A force \(\vec{F}=15\hat{i}+20\hat{j}\) N moves an object from (2,0) to (8,5) m. Find work done.
✅ answer: displacement = (6,5) m. Work = 15·6 + 20·5 = 90+100 = 190 J.
Q31
Find \(\alpha\) so that \(\vec{A}=3\hat{i}+\alpha\hat{j}\) and \(\vec{B}=4\hat{i}-2\hat{j}\) are perpendicular.
✅ answer: Dot = 3·4 + α·(-2) = 12 - 2α = 0 → α = 6.
