Covalent Bonding

Learning objective: Covalent Bonding

Explanation

Covalent bonding occurs when atoms share pairs of electrons. It is common between non-metal atoms and produces molecules or extended covalent structures.

Key points

  • Hydrogen forms H2 by sharing one pair of electrons.
  • Water has the formula H2O.
  • Carbon dioxide has the formula CO2.
  • A chemical formula indicates the types and relative numbers of atoms.

Practical activity

Draw simple dot-and-cross diagrams for H2, H2O and CO2.
