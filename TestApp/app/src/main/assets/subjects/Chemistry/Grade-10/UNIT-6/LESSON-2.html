Neutralization and Salt Formation

Learning objective: Neutralization and Salt Formation

Explanation

Neutralization commonly occurs when an acid reacts with a base. Acid-base reactions can form a salt and water, although the products depend on the reactants and conditions.

Key points

  • Acid + base → salt + water for a typical neutralization.
  • Hydrochloric acid can form chloride salts.
  • Sulfuric acid can form sulfate salts.
  • Use balanced equations to represent reactions.

Practical activity
