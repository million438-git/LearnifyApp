Acids, Bases and Salts - Unit Exam

Answer each question before revealing the answer. This is supplementary Learnify practice material, not an official examination.

Question 1

At about 25°C, what does a pH of 3 indicate?

Reveal answer

An acidic solution.

Question 2

What other product forms when hydrochloric acid reacts with sodium hydroxide?

Reveal answer

Water.

Question 3

Why should household cleaning products never be mixed unless the label explicitly permits it?

Reveal answer

Mixing can cause dangerous chemical reactions or release harmful gases.
