Stoichiometry and Reaction Ratios

Learning objective: Stoichiometry and Reaction Ratios

Explanation

A balanced equation gives mole ratios between reactants and products. Calculations use these ratios together with the amount of a known substance.

Key points

  • Balance the equation first.
  • Convert a given mass to moles.
  • Use the coefficient ratio.
  • Convert the required amount to mass if asked.

Practical activity

For 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O, determine how many moles of water can form from 3 moles of oxygen if hydrogen is in excess.

Check your understanding

Question: According to 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O, how many moles of water form from 1 mole of O2 with enough hydrogen?

Show answer

2 moles of H2O.
