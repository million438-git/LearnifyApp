Grade 10 Chemistry Final Practice Exam

Try each question before revealing its answer. This is supplementary practice, not an official examination.

Question 1

How many neutrons are in an atom with mass number 35 and atomic number 17?

Reveal answer

35 - 17 = 18 neutrons.

Question 2

What is the shell arrangement of sodium, atomic number 11?

Reveal answer

2,8,1.

Question 3

What type of bond involves shared electron pairs?

Reveal answer

Covalent bonding.

Question 4

Balance: N2 + H2 → NH3.

Reveal answer

N2 + 3H2 → 2NH3.

Question 5

Calculate the amount in moles in 18 g of water with molar mass 18 g/mol.

Reveal answer

n = 18/18 = 1 mol.

Question 6

Calculate concentration for 0.40 mol in 0.20 L.

Reveal answer

c = 0.40/0.20 = 2.0 mol/L.

Question 7

What products commonly form in acid-base neutralization?

Reveal answer

A salt and water.

Question 8

What does a pH of 11 indicate at about 25°C?

Reveal answer

An alkaline solution.

Question 9

Give one way to reduce chemical pollution.

Reveal answer

Treat wastewater or prevent hazardous chemicals from entering waterways.

Question 10

Why must chemical equations be balanced?

Reveal answer

To represent conservation of atoms during the reaction.
