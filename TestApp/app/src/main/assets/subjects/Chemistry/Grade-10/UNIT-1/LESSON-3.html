Periodic Table and Periodic Trends

Learning objective: Periodic Table and Periodic Trends

Explanation

The periodic table arranges elements by increasing atomic number. Elements in a group often have similar chemical properties because they have related outer-electron arrangements.

Key points

  • Rows are called periods.
  • Columns are called groups.
  • Metals are mostly on the left and centre.
  • Group 18 elements are generally unreactive noble gases.

Practical activity

Use a periodic table to identify the group and period of sodium, carbon, chlorine and neon.

Check your understanding

Question: Why do elements in the same main group often have similar chemical properties?

Show answer

They have similar outer-shell electron arrangements.
