Indicators, Safety and Everyday Applications

Learning objective: Indicators, Safety and Everyday Applications

Explanation

Indicators change colour over a pH range and can help distinguish acidic and alkaline solutions. Acids and bases have household, agricultural and industrial applications, but some are corrosive.

Key points

  • Litmus can distinguish acids and bases.
  • Universal indicator estimates pH using a colour scale.
  • Wear suitable eye protection in supervised experiments.
  • Follow product labels and never mix household cleaning products.

Practical activity

Create a table showing two safe everyday applications of acids or bases and one safety precaution for each.

Check your understanding

Question: Why should household cleaning products never be mixed unless the label explicitly permits it?

Show answer

Mixing can cause dangerous chemical reactions or release harmful gases.
