The Mole and Molar Mass

Learning objective: The Mole and Molar Mass

Explanation

The mole is a unit for amount of substance. The relationship between amount, mass and molar mass is n = m/M, where n is amount in moles, m is mass in grams, and M is molar mass in grams per mole.

Key points

  • One mole contains approximately 6.022 × 10^23 specified entities.
  • Molar mass has units g/mol.
  • Rearrange the equation to find mass: m = nM.
  • Check that units cancel correctly.

Practical activity

Calculate the amount in moles in 36 g of water if its molar mass is 18 g/mol.

Check your understanding
