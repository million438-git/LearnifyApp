Atoms, Protons, Neutrons and Electrons

Learning objective: Atoms, Protons, Neutrons and Electrons

Explanation

An atom has a small nucleus containing protons and usually neutrons, with electrons occupying regions around the nucleus. A proton has positive charge, an electron negative charge, and a neutron no electric charge.

Key points

  • Atomic number equals the number of protons.
  • Mass number equals protons plus neutrons.
  • A neutral atom has equal numbers of protons and electrons.
  • Isotopes have the same proton number but different neutron numbers.

Practical activity

For an atom with atomic number 11 and mass number 23, calculate the number of protons, neutrons and electrons in a neutral atom.

Check your understanding

Question: How many neutrons are in sodium-23, atomic number 11?

Show answer

23 - 11 = 12 neutrons.
