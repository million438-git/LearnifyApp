Environmental and Industrial Chemistry - Unit Exam

Answer each question before revealing the answer. This is supplementary Learnify practice material, not an official examination.

Question 1

Give one way to reduce water pollution.

Reveal answer

Treat wastewater before discharge, prevent chemical dumping, or reduce harmful runoff.

Question 2

Why should fertilizers be applied according to recommended rates?

Reveal answer

Excess application can waste resources and contribute to water pollution or plant damage.

Question 3

What is one goal of sustainable chemistry?

Reveal answer

To reduce waste, hazards and environmental impact while providing useful products.
