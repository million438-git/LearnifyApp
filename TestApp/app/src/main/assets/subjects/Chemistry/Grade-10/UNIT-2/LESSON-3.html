Metallic Bonding and Material Properties

Learning objective: Metallic Bonding and Material Properties

Explanation

Metallic bonding is the attraction between positive metal ions and delocalized electrons. These mobile electrons help explain why metals conduct electricity and heat.

Key points

  • Metals are often malleable and ductile.
  • Many metals conduct electricity as solids.
  • Ionic solids generally have high melting points.
  • Simple molecular substances often have relatively low melting points.

Practical activity

Compare the expected electrical conductivity of copper, solid sodium chloride and sodium chloride dissolved in water. Do not handle reactive metals.

Check your understanding

Question: Why can solid copper conduct electricity?

Show answer

It contains mobile delocalized electrons.
