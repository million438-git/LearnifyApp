Solutes, Solvents and Solutions

Learning objective: Solutes, Solvents and Solutions

Explanation

A solution is a homogeneous mixture. The solute is the substance dissolved, and the solvent is the medium that dissolves it. Water is a common solvent.

Key points

  • A dilute solution contains relatively little solute.
  • A concentrated solution contains relatively more solute for a given amount of solution.
  • Solubility depends on the substances and conditions.
  • Dissolving does not always mean a chemical reaction has occurred.

Practical activity
