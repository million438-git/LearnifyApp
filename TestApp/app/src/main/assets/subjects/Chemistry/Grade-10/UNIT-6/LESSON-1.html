Properties of Acids and Bases

Learning objective: Properties of Acids and Bases

Explanation

Acids and bases have different chemical properties. In aqueous solution, acids increase hydrogen ion concentration, while bases can accept hydrogen ions or produce hydroxide ions depending on the definition used.

Key points

  • Acidic solutions have pH below 7 at about 25°C.
  • Neutral water has pH about 7 at about 25°C.
  • Alkaline solutions have pH above 7 at about 25°C.
  • Never identify chemicals by tasting them.

Practical activity

Classify lemon juice, pure water and a typical soap solution as acidic, approximately neutral or alkaline, using appropriate indicator information.

Check your understanding

Question: At about 25°C, what does a pH of 3 indicate?

Show answer

An acidic solution.
