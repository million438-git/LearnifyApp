Preparing and Diluting Solutions

Learning objective: Preparing and Diluting Solutions

Explanation

Solutions should be prepared with suitable equipment and safe handling. During dilution, adding solvent lowers concentration while the amount of solute remains unchanged, assuming no loss or reaction.

Key points

  • Use labelled containers.
  • Wear appropriate eye protection in a laboratory.
  • For dilution, C1V1 = C2V2 when units are consistent.
  • Never taste laboratory chemicals.

Practical activity

A 2.0 mol/L stock solution is diluted to 0.50 mol/L. Calculate the final volume if 25 mL of stock is used.

Check your understanding

Question: Using C1V1=C2V2, what final volume results from diluting 25 mL of 2.0 mol/L solution to 0.50 mol/L?

Show answer

V2 = (2.0 × 25)/0.50 = 100 mL.
