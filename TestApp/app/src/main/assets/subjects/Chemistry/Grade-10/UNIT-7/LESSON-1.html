Air, Water and Chemical Pollution

Learning objective: Air, Water and Chemical Pollution

Explanation

Chemical pollution occurs when harmful substances enter air, water or soil. Pollution can affect human health, ecosystems and the availability of clean resources.

Key points

  • Combustion can produce carbon dioxide and other pollutants.
  • Untreated wastewater may contaminate water sources.
  • Some pollutants persist in the environment.
  • Prevention and proper waste treatment reduce harm.

Practical activity

Identify one potential source of water pollution in a community and suggest a practical prevention measure.

Check your understanding

Question: Give one way to reduce water pollution.

Show answer

Treat wastewater before discharge, prevent chemical dumping, or reduce harmful runoff.
