Relative Atomic Mass and Formula Mass

Learning objective: Relative Atomic Mass and Formula Mass

Explanation

Relative atomic mass values are listed on the periodic table. Relative formula mass is calculated by adding the relative atomic masses of all atoms represented in a formula.

Key points

  • Use the periodic table values supplied in a question.
  • For H2O, Mr = 2(1) + 16 = 18.
  • For CO2, Mr = 12 + 2(16) = 44.
  • Always show the arithmetic and units where appropriate.

Practical activity

Calculate the relative formula masses of NaCl and CaCO3 using Na=23, Cl=35.5, Ca=40, C=12 and O=16.
