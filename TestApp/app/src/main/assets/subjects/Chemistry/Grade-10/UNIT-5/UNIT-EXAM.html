Solutions and Concentration - Unit Exam

Answer each question before revealing the answer. This is supplementary Learnify practice material, not an official examination.

Question 1

In salt water, what is the solvent?

Reveal answer

Water.

Question 2

What is the concentration of 0.30 mol in 0.15 L?

Reveal answer

c = 0.30/0.15 = 2.0 mol/L.

Question 3

Using C1V1=C2V2, what final volume results from diluting 25 mL of 2.0 mol/L solution to 0.50 mol/L?

Reveal answer

V2 = (2.0 × 25)/0.50 = 100 mL.
