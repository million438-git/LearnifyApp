The Mole and Chemical Calculations - Unit Exam

Answer each question before revealing the answer. This is supplementary Learnify practice material, not an official examination.

Question 1

Calculate Mr for CO2 using C=12 and O=16.

Reveal answer

12 + 2(16) = 44.

Question 2

How many moles are in 10 g of a substance with molar mass 20 g/mol?

Reveal answer

n = 10/20 = 0.5 mol.

Question 3

According to 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O, how many moles of water form from 1 mole of O2 with enough hydrogen?

Reveal answer

2 moles of H2O.
