Molar Concentration

Learning objective: Molar Concentration

Explanation

Molar concentration is amount of dissolved solute divided by solution volume in litres. The relationship is c = n/V, with concentration in mol/L when n is in mol and V is in litres.

Key points

  • Convert millilitres to litres before calculating.
  • Rearrange to n = cV.
  • A solution volume of 250 mL is 0.250 L.
  • Use the final unit mol/L or mol dm-3.

Practical activity

Calculate concentration when 0.20 mol of solute is present in 0.50 L of solution.

Check your understanding

Question: What is the concentration of 0.30 mol in 0.15 L?

Show answer

c = 0.30/0.15 = 2.0 mol/L.
