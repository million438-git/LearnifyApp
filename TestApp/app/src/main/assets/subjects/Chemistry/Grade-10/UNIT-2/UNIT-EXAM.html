Chemical Bonding and Structure - Unit Exam

Answer each question before revealing the answer. This is supplementary Learnify practice material, not an official examination.

Question 1

What is the formula of a compound made from Mg2+ and Cl- ions?

Reveal answer

MgCl2, because two chloride ions balance one magnesium ion.

Question 2

What type of bond forms when two non-metal atoms share electrons?

Reveal answer

A covalent bond.

Question 3

Why can solid copper conduct electricity?

Reveal answer

It contains mobile delocalized electrons.
