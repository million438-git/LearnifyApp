Chemistry in Agriculture and Industry

Learning objective: Chemistry in Agriculture and Industry

Explanation

Chemistry supports fertilizer production, water treatment, food processing, materials manufacturing and medicine. Benefits must be balanced against safety and environmental impacts.

Key points

  • Fertilizers supply nutrients needed by plants.
  • Water treatment can remove contaminants and pathogens.
  • Industrial processes require quality control.
  • Correct chemical storage and disposal are important.

Practical activity

Choose one local product and describe how chemistry may be involved in its production or quality testing.

Check your understanding

Question: Why should fertilizers be applied according to recommended rates?

Show answer

Excess application can waste resources and contribute to water pollution or plant damage.
