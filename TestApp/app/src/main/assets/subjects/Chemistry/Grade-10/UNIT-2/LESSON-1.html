Ionic Bonding

Learning objective: Ionic Bonding

Explanation

Ionic bonding involves electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions. Ions form when atoms transfer electrons. Many ionic compounds form crystal lattices.

Key points

  • Metals commonly form positive ions by losing electrons.
  • Non-metals commonly form negative ions by gaining electrons.
  • Magnesium forms Mg2+; oxygen forms O2-.
  • Ionic compounds are overall electrically neutral.

Practical activity

Use electron transfer diagrams to explain how magnesium and oxygen form magnesium oxide.

Check your understanding

Question: What is the formula of a compound made from Mg2+ and Cl- ions?

Show answer

MgCl2, because two chloride ions balance one magnesium ion.
