Reactants, Products and Chemical Equations

Learning objective: Reactants, Products and Chemical Equations

Explanation

A chemical reaction changes starting substances called reactants into products. A chemical equation represents the reaction using formulas and coefficients.

Key points

  • Reactants are written on the left.
  • Products are written on the right.
  • Coefficients indicate relative amounts of particles or moles.
  • Subscripts are part of a formula and should not be changed when balancing.

Practical activity

Identify reactants and products in 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O.

Check your understanding

Question: In 2Mg + O2 → 2MgO, which substance is a product?
