Reaction Types and Energy Changes

Learning objective: Reaction Types and Energy Changes

Explanation

Reactions can be classified in different ways, including combination, decomposition, displacement and combustion. Reactions also involve energy transfer.

Key points

  • Combination forms a product from two or more reactants.
  • Decomposition breaks a compound into simpler substances.
  • Combustion commonly releases energy.
  • Exothermic processes release energy to the surroundings; endothermic processes absorb it.
