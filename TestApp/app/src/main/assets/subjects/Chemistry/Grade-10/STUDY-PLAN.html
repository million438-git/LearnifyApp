Grade 10 Chemistry Study Plan

Four-week study plan

  1. Week 1: Atomic structure, periodic table and bonding.
  2. Week 2: Chemical reactions, equations and the mole.
  3. Week 3: Solutions, concentration, acids, bases and salts.
  4. Week 4: Environmental chemistry, unit exams and final revision.

For each session, review the explanation, solve examples without looking at the answers, and record errors to revisit.
