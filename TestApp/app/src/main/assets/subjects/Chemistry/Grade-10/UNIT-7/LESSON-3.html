Sustainable Chemistry and Waste Management

Learning objective: Sustainable Chemistry and Waste Management

Explanation

Sustainable chemistry aims to reduce hazardous substances, energy use and waste while maintaining useful products and processes. Reuse, recycling and appropriate disposal help reduce environmental impacts.

Key points

  • Reduce unnecessary material use.
  • Separate waste according to local guidance.
  • Do not pour unknown chemicals into drains.
  • Choose safer materials and efficient processes where possible.

Practical activity

Design a simple school plan to reduce laboratory or household chemical waste safely.

Check your understanding

Question: What is one goal of sustainable chemistry?

Show answer

To reduce waste, hazards and environmental impact while providing useful products.
