Electron Arrangement

Learning objective: Electron Arrangement

Explanation

Electrons occupy energy levels around the nucleus. For the first 20 elements, a simplified school model commonly fills shells 2, 8, 8, then the next shell as needed.

Key points

  • Sodium has the shell arrangement 2,8,1.
  • Chlorine has the shell arrangement 2,8,7.
  • Outer-shell electrons influence chemical behaviour.
  • Atoms may gain, lose or share electrons during bonding.

Practical activity

Write simplified shell arrangements for carbon, oxygen, magnesium and calcium using a periodic table.

Check your understanding

Question: What is the simplified shell arrangement of magnesium, atomic number 12?
