Atomic Structure and the Periodic Table - Unit Exam

Answer each question before revealing the answer. This is supplementary Learnify practice material, not an official examination.

Question 1

How many neutrons are in sodium-23, atomic number 11?

Reveal answer

23 - 11 = 12 neutrons.

Question 2

What is the simplified shell arrangement of magnesium, atomic number 12?

Reveal answer

2,8,2.

Question 3

Why do elements in the same main group often have similar chemical properties?

Reveal answer

They have similar outer-shell electron arrangements.
