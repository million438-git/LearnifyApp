Grade 10 Chemistry Formula Reference

Core formulas and reminders

  • Neutrons = mass number − atomic number.
  • Relative formula mass: add the relative atomic masses of all atoms in the formula.
  • Amount in moles: n = m/M.
  • Molar concentration: c = n/V, with V in litres for mol/L.
  • Dilution: C1V1 = C2V2 when units are consistent and solute amount is conserved.
  • Balance equations by changing coefficients, never formula subscripts.
  • Typical neutralization: acid + base → salt + water.
  • At about 25°C: acidic pH below 7, neutral pH about 7, alkaline pH above 7.

Always use the atomic masses and definitions supplied by your textbook or examination.
