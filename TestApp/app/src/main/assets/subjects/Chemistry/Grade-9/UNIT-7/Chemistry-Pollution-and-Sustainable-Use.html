Lesson notes

Air pollution can include particulate matter, carbon monoxide, sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides. Water pollution may involve sewage, excess nutrients, industrial chemicals or plastic waste. The effects depend on the pollutant, amount, exposure and ecosystem.

Chemistry helps analyze water quality, develop treatment methods, improve materials and design cleaner industrial processes. Prevention, reuse, recycling and careful waste handling can reduce environmental harm.

Chemical safety applies at home as well as in laboratories. Never mix household cleaners, especially bleach with acids or ammonia-containing products, because dangerous gases may form. Keep chemicals in labeled original containers and follow local disposal guidance.
