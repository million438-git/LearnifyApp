Groups, Periods and Periodic Trends

Lesson notes

Elements in the same group often form ions with related charges. Group 1 metals commonly form +1 ions, Group 2 metals commonly form +2 ions, and Group 17 halogens commonly form −1 ions in simple ionic compounds.

Across a period, atomic number increases by one for each successive element. For main-group elements, the number of occupied electron shells generally stays the same across that period.

Down a group, atoms have additional occupied electron shells. Reactivity trends differ by group: Group 1 metals generally become more reactive down the group, while the halogens generally become less reactive down the group. These are patterns, not substitutes for safe experimental evidence.
