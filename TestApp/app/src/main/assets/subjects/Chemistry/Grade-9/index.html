Welcome to Grade 9 Chemistry

This offline course introduces matter, atoms, periodic organization, bonding, reactions, acids and bases, laboratory safety and environmental responsibility.

Course units

  1. Unit 1: Chemistry and Its Importance — What chemistry studies, how it helps society, and how to work safely.
  2. Unit 2: Matter and Its Properties — Matter, states, physical and chemical changes, mixtures and separation.
  3. Unit 3: Atomic Structure — Atoms, subatomic particles, ions, isotopes and atomic notation.
  4. Unit 4: The Periodic Table — Periodic organization, groups, periods and broad element properties.
  5. Unit 5: Chemical Bonding — Why atoms bond and how ionic and covalent substances form.
  6. Unit 6: Chemical Reactions and Equations — Reaction evidence, word and symbol equations, balancing and conservation.
  7. Unit 7: Acids, Bases, Salts and the Environment — Acidic and basic solutions, indicators, neutralization and responsible chemistry.
