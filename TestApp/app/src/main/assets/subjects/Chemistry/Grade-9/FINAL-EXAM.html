Grade 9 Chemistry Final Practice Exam

20 questions · Answer-check guide included · Learnify Grade 9 Chemistry

Final practice assessment

Answer all questions. Suggested time: 45–60 minutes. Suggested total: 40 marks, with 2 marks per question.

  1. Define chemistry and give one example of its use in agriculture.
  2. State three laboratory safety rules.
  3. Explain the particle arrangement in a gas.
  4. Classify rusting iron as a physical or chemical change and explain why.
  5. Name a suitable method to separate an insoluble solid from a liquid.
  6. An atom has atomic number 13 and mass number 27. Find its protons, neutrons and electrons if neutral.
  7. Define isotopes.
  8. How does a positive ion form?
  9. What are groups and periods in the periodic table?
  10. Give two typical properties of metals.
  11. Explain ionic bonding.
  12. Write the formula for a compound containing Ca²⁺ and Cl⁻.
  13. Explain covalent bonding and give one example.
  14. Balance: Fe + O₂ → Fe₂O₃.
  15. Balance: Na + Cl₂ → NaCl.
  16. State the law of conservation of mass.
  17. Give two possible signs of a chemical reaction.
  18. Classify a solution with pH 4.
  19. What is neutralization?
  20. Explain one way chemistry can help reduce pollution.

Answer-check guide

Show short answer guide after attempting the exam
  1. Study of matter, its properties and changes; for example, soil nutrients or fertilizers.
  2. Any three valid safety rules.
  3. Particles are far apart and move freely.
  4. Chemical change: iron compounds such as rust form.
  5. Filtration.
  6. 13 protons, 14 neutrons and 13 electrons.
  7. Atoms with the same proton number but different neutron numbers.
  8. It loses one or more electrons.
  9. Rows are periods; columns are groups.
  10. For example, good electrical conductivity and malleability; trends have exceptions.
  11. Attraction between oppositely charged ions.
  12. CaCl₂.
  13. Atoms share electron pairs; examples include H₂ or H₂O.
  14. 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃.
  15. 2Na + Cl₂ → 2NaCl.
  16. Total mass is conserved in a closed system.
  17. Any two valid observations, interpreted appropriately.
  18. Acidic.
  19. A typical acid–base reaction that produces salt and water.
  20. Examples include water treatment, pollution monitoring or waste reduction.
