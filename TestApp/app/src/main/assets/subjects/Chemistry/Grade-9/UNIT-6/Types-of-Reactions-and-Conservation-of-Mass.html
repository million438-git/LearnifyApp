Lesson notes

A synthesis reaction combines substances to form a product, as in 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO. A decomposition reaction breaks a compound into simpler substances. Other useful introductory patterns include displacement and acid–base neutralization.

In a displacement reaction, a more reactive element may replace a less reactive element in a compound. In neutralization, an acid reacts with a base to form a salt and water in a typical acid–base reaction.

The law of conservation of mass states that total mass remains constant in a closed system during a chemical reaction. If gas escapes from an open container, the measured mass of the container and contents can decrease even though matter has not ceased to exist.

Try it: Explain why a sealed reaction container is useful when testing conservation of mass. Balance the equation 2H₂ + O₂ → H₂O before using it as a conservation example.
