Lesson notes

Chemical bonds are attractions that hold atoms or ions together. Atoms form bonds because the resulting arrangement can be more stable and have lower energy than separated atoms under the same conditions.

Valence electrons are electrons in the outermost occupied shell in the introductory shell model. Their arrangement helps explain how an atom may lose, gain or share electrons.

A chemical formula shows the types of atoms or ions in a substance and their ratios. The formula H₂O represents two hydrogen atoms for every oxygen atom in a water molecule; MgCl₂ represents magnesium and chloride ions in a 1:2 ratio.
