Unit 6 Exam: Chemical Reactions and Equations

Practice assessment · Suggested total 20 marks · Learnify Grade 9 Chemistry

Instructions

Answer all five questions. Show your working where calculations are required. Suggested time: 25 minutes. Suggested total: 20 marks.

  1. Define reactants and products.
  2. Balance: Mg + O₂ → MgO.
  3. Balance: H₂ + O₂ → H₂O.
  4. State the law of conservation of mass.
  5. Explain why changing a formula's subscripts is not a valid way to balance an equation.

Marking guidance

Suggested allocation: 4 marks per question. Award credit for scientifically accurate explanations, correct terminology, appropriate working and relevant examples. For open-ended questions, accept other valid answers supported by scientific reasoning.
