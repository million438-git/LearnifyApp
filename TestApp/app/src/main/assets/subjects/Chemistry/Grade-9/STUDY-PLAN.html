Four-week study plan

Study for about 30–60 minutes per session. Adjust the pace to your school timetable.

Week 1 — Foundations

  • Day 1: Unit 1, lesson 1
  • Day 2: Unit 1, lessons 2–3
  • Day 3: Unit 1 exam and corrections
  • Day 4: Unit 2, lesson 1
  • Day 5: Unit 2, lesson 2
  • Day 6: Unit 2, lesson 3
  • Day 7: Review and rest

Week 2 — Atoms and the periodic table

  • Day 1–3: Unit 3 lessons and practice
  • Day 4–6: Unit 4 lessons and periodic-table exercises
  • Day 7: Review units 3–4

Week 3 — Bonding and reactions

  • Day 1–3: Unit 5, including formula writing
  • Day 4–6: Unit 6, including equation balancing
  • Day 7: Correct mistakes and review

Week 4 — Acids, environment and revision

  • Day 1–3: Unit 7 lessons and exam
  • Day 4: Review the formula sheet
  • Day 5: Take the final practice exam
  • Day 6: Correct answers and revisit weak topics
  • Day 7: Light revision
Effective study routine: Spend 5 minutes recalling yesterday's lesson, 20–30 minutes studying, 10–15 minutes answering questions, and 5 minutes checking mistakes.
