Lesson notes

The atomic number, Z, is the number of protons and identifies the element. The mass number, A, is the total number of protons and neutrons in a particular atom.

Number of neutrons = mass number − atomic number. For sodium-23, Z = 11, so the atom has 11 protons, 12 neutrons and, if neutral, 11 electrons.

Isotopes are atoms of the same element with equal proton numbers but different neutron numbers. They have similar chemical behaviour because they have the same atomic number and electron arrangement in neutral atoms, but their masses differ.

Try it: An atom has atomic number 17 and mass number 35. Find the numbers of protons, neutrons and electrons in its neutral form.
