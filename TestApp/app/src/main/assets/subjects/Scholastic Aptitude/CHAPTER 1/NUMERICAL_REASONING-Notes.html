🧠 Numerical Reasoning – Chapter 1

Grade 11–12 | Ethiopian Entrance Exam

📘 FULL NOTES

Numerical Reasoning tests your ability to understand number patterns, relationships, and logic. ⚡
It is NOT about memorization, but about thinking fast and smart.

1️⃣ Number Series

A number series is a sequence of numbers arranged in a pattern.
Example: 2, 4, 6, 8, ? → Pattern = +2 → Answer = 10

2️⃣ Arithmetic Series

Difference between numbers is constant.
5, 10, 15, 20 → Difference = +5

3️⃣ Geometric Series

Each number is multiplied or divided by a fixed number.
3, 6, 12, 24 → ×2

4️⃣ Mixed Series

Uses more than one operation (×, +, −).
2, 6, 7, 21, 22 → ×3, +1

5️⃣ BODMAS Rule

B = Brackets → O = Orders → D = Division → M = Multiplication → A = Addition → S = Subtraction

6️⃣ Basic Number Properties

- Even & odd numbers
- Prime & composite numbers
- Squares & square roots
- Cubes & cube roots

📝 PRACTICE QUESTIONS (HARD + INTERMEDIATE)

Q1️⃣ Find the next number:
4, 8, 16, 32, ?
A) 48
B) 56
C) 64
D) 60
✅ Correct Answer: C) 64
Explanation: Multiply each number by 2 → 32 × 2 = 64 🔥
Q2️⃣ Find the missing number:
3, 9, 27, ?, 243
A) 54
B) 81
C) 72
D) 90
✅ Correct Answer: B) 81
Explanation: Multiply each number by 3 → 27 × 3 = 81 ✨
Q3️⃣ Odd one out:
16, 25, 36, 49, 64
A) 16
B) 25
C) 36
D) 49
✅ Correct Answer: B) 25
Explanation: 25 is the only square of an odd prime (5²). Others are even squares 🔥
Q4️⃣ Simplify using BODMAS:
6 + 12 ÷ 3 × 2
A) 14
B) 16
C) 18
D) 20
✅ Correct Answer: B) 16
Explanation: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 → 4 × 2 = 8 → 6 + 8 = 16 🎯
Q5️⃣ Prime number:
A) 21
B) 27
C) 29
D) 33
✅ Correct Answer: C) 29
Explanation: 29 has only two factors: 1 and itself ✔️
