🔍 UNDERSTANDING LITERATURE

📜 Short Stories

  • Plot: sequence of events (exposition, rising action, climax, falling action, resolution).
  • Setting: time and place.
  • Conflict: internal or external struggle.
  • Point of view: first-person, third-person limited/omniscient.

🎭 Poems

  • Speaker: the voice of the poem.
  • Imagery: sensory details.
  • Figurative language: metaphor, simile, personification.
  • Rhyme & meter: pattern of sounds.

🎪 Drama

  • Acts & scenes: divisions of a play.
  • Dialogue & monologue.
  • Stage directions.
  • Tragedy / comedy.

🎯 Theme

  • Central message or insight about life.
  • Not a one-word topic (e.g., "love" is a topic; theme: "love can overcome hatred").

⚖️ Moral Lesson

  • Didactic purpose; often explicitly stated in fables.
  • Example: "Slow and steady wins the race."

🧑‍🎤 Character Analysis

  • Protagonist / antagonist.
  • Round vs flat, dynamic vs static.
  • Motivations, traits, development.

📄 Sample: "The Tortoise and the Hare"

Theme: Overconfidence leads to failure; perseverance wins.

Character analysis: Hare – arrogant, static; Tortoise – determined, dynamic (reveals inner strength).

Moral: "Slow and steady wins the race."
