📘 DEEP DIVE: ACTIVE ↔ PASSIVE VOICE
📌 PRESENT PASSIVE
structure
am / is / are + past participle (V3)
usage & examples
• General truths, habits, current facts
• Active: They make coffee in Brazil.
➜ Passive: Coffee is made in Brazil.
• Active: The chef prepares the meal.
➜ Passive: The meal is prepared by the chef.
⚡ advanced: “The documents are checked every morning.”
⚡ negative: “English is not spoken here.”
📌 PAST PASSIVE
structure
was / were + past participle (V3)
usage & examples
• Completed actions in the past
• Active: They built this bridge in 1990.
➜ Passive: This bridge was built in 1990.
• Active: The committee approved the proposal.
➜ Passive: The proposal was approved (by the committee).
⚡ advanced: “The manuscripts were discovered in a monastery.”
⚡ negative: “The letter was not sent yesterday.”
📌 FUTURE PASSIVE
structure
will be + past participle (V3)   |   am/is/are going to be + V3
usage & examples
• Predictions, plans, scheduled events
• Active: They will announce the results tomorrow.
➜ Passive: The results will be announced tomorrow.
• Active: The company is going to launch a new product.
➜ Passive: A new product is going to be launched.
⚡ advanced: “The policy will be implemented next year.”
⚡ negative: “The meeting will not be held on Monday.”
📌 MODAL PASSIVE
structure
modal (can, must, should, might, etc.) + be + past participle
usage & examples
• Obligation, possibility, necessity, advice
• Active: You must finish the report.
➜ Passive: The report must be finished (by you).
• Active: They can’t repair this phone.
➜ Passive: This phone can’t be repaired.
⚡ advanced: “The application should be submitted before Friday.”
⚡ perfect modals: “It might have been stolen.” (modal + have been + V3)

🎯 ADVANCED PASSIVE STRUCTURES (entrance level)

  • • Present continuous passive: am/is/are + being + V3 (The room is being cleaned)
  • • Past continuous passive: was/were + being + V3 (The road was being repaired)
  • • Present perfect passive: has/have + been + V3 (The letters have been sent)
  • • Past perfect passive: had + been + V3 (The work had been finished)
  • • Future perfect passive: will have been + V3 (The project will have been completed)
  • • Modal + perfect passive: must have been + V3 (He must have been invited)
  • • Verbs with two objects (give, send, offer): two passive forms (I was given a book / A book was given to me)
  • • Impersonal passive (It is said that… / He is said to…) – high level
