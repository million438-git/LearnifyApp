📘 DEEP DIVE: DIRECT → INDIRECT (REPORTED) SPEECH
📌 REPORTED STATEMENTS
structure
reporting verb (said, told) + that + clause (that optional)
tense changes (backshift)
• present simple → past simple
• present continuous → past continuous
• present perfect → past perfect
• past simple → past perfect
• will → would · can → could · may → might
direct: “I work in a bank.”
➜ indirect: He said (that) he worked in a bank.
direct: “I am reading.”
➜ indirect: She said she was reading.
📌 REPORTED QUESTIONS
wh- questions
wh-word remains, statement word order, no auxiliary do/does/did.
yes/no questions
if / whether + statement order.
direct: “Where do you live?”
➜ He asked me where I lived.
direct: “Are you tired?”
➜ She asked if I was tired.
📌 COMMANDS / REQUESTS
structure
ask/tell/order/command + person + to-infinitive
negative commands
not + to-infinitive
direct: “Sit down.”
➜ He told me to sit down.
direct: “Don’t touch that!”
➜ She ordered me not to touch that.
📌 TENSE CHANGE EXCEPTIONS
no backshift when:
• universal truth: “The earth is round.” ➜ He said the earth is round.
• reporting verb present/future: “I am happy.” ➜ She says she is happy.
• still true / recent: “I live in Addis.” ➜ He said he lives in Addis (if still true).
📌 TIME & PLACE ADJUSTMENTS
• now → then / at that time
• today → that day
• yesterday → the day before / the previous day
• tomorrow → the next day / the following day
• here → there
• this → that · these → those
• ago → before
direct: “I will meet you here tomorrow.”
➜ He said he would meet me there the next day.
📌 ADDITIONAL POINTS
• say / tell – tell + indirect object, say + to + person (optional)
• promise, offer, refuse, advise + to-infinitive
• suggest + gerund / that clause
• exclamations – often reported with ‘exclaimed with joy / sorrow’

🎯 ADVANCED PATTERNS (entrance level)

  • • Modal verbs in indirect: would, could, might, should (no change), must (usually remains or had to)
  • • Indirect questions embedded: “Do you know where the station is?” (not *where is the station)
  • • Mixed time: if the introductory verb is past, backshift is standard but optional if still true.
  • • Reported speech with infinitives: “You should rest.” ➜ He advised me to rest.
  • • Reported speech of ‘let’s’ : suggest + -ing / that clause
