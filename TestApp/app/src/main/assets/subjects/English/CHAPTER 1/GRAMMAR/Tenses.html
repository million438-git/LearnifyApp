📚 TENSE MASTER NOTES · usage, form, signal words & error correction
Present Simple
Usage: habits, facts, timetables, permanent situations.
Form: base verb (+s/es for he/she/it) • negative: do/does + not
Signal: always every day often usually never on Mondays
⚡ error: using -s after modals or in questions: "She doesn't works" ✘ → She doesn't work ✔
Present Continuous
Usage: now, around now, temporary actions, future arrangements.
Form: am/is/are + verb-ing
Signal: now at the moment today this week Look! currently
⚡ stative verbs (know, like) rarely used in continuous.
Present Perfect
Usage: past with present relevance, life experience, recent changes.
Form: have/has + past participle
Signal: ever never already yet just since for
⚡ "I have visited last year" ✘ (specific past needs past simple).
Present Perfect Continuous
Usage: actions started in past still continuing or recently stopped with result.
Form: have/has + been + verb-ing
Signal: for hours since morning lately recently all day
⚡ focus on duration / visible effect: "I'm tired, I have been working."
Past Simple
Usage: completed past actions, past habits, sequences.
Form: verb+ed (or irregular) • did + not + infinitive
Signal: yesterday last week in 2010 ago then
⚡ irregular forms: go → went, see → saw. Common error: "I have seen it yesterday" ✘
Past Continuous
Usage: interrupted past action, simultaneous actions, background.
Form: was/were + verb-ing
Signal: while when (with past simple) at 8pm last night as
⚡ "When I arrived, she cooked" ✘ → she was cooking (interrupted).
Past Perfect
Usage: action before another past action / past of past.
Form: had + past participle
Signal: already by the time before after never... until
⚡ "After I ate, I went out." both past simple OK (sequence clear). But "I had eaten before he arrived."
Past Perfect Continuous
Usage: duration before something in the past.
Form: had + been + verb-ing
Signal: for since before (with duration) all day/week
⚡ "They had been walking for hours when they found the village."
Future (will)
Usage: predictions, spontaneous decisions, promises.
Form: will + infinitive
Signal: tomorrow probably I think I promise
⚡ "I will help you!" (instant decision).
Future (going to)
Usage: plans, intentions, evidence-based predictions.
Form: am/is/are + going to + infinitive
Signal: next year tomorrow (planned) look at those clouds
⚡ "I'm going to study medicine." (intention).
Present cont. for future
Usage: fixed arrangements, often with time.
Form: am/is/are + verb-ing (+future time)
Signal: tonight tomorrow this weekend at 5pm
⚡ "I'm meeting the director at 3." (arrangement).

⚠️ COMMON ERROR CORRECTIONS (Ethiopian entrance top mistakes)

  • • ✘ She don't like → ✔ She doesn't like
  • • ✘ I am agree → ✔ I agree
  • • ✘ They have gone yesterday → ✔ They went yesterday
  • • ✘ When she arrived we have dinner → ✔ we were having
  • • ✘ By next week I finish → ✔ I will have finished
  • • ✘ He has been knowing → ✔ He has known
  • • ✘ I will going to → ✔ I am going to / I will go
  • • ✘ While he studied, she called → ✔ While he was studying
