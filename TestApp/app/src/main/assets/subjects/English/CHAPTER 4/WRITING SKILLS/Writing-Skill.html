📌 SENTENCE WRITING

🔹 Simple Sentences

Definition

A simple sentence has one independent clause (one subject + one predicate). It expresses a complete thought.

Not simple: The dog barked and the cat ran. (compound)
Simple: The dog barked loudly.
Advanced insight: Simple sentences can have compound subjects or verbs: "The dog and the cat ran." still one clause.

🔹 Compound Sentences

Structure

Two or more independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction (FANBOYS: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so) or a semicolon.

Comma splice: I love coffee, I drink it daily.
Compound: I love coffee, so I drink it daily.

🔹 Complex Sentences

One independent + one or more dependent clauses

Dependent clause begins with subordinating conjunction (because, although, when, etc.) or relative pronoun.

Missing comma: When she arrived I was sleeping.
Complex: When she arrived, I was sleeping.

🔹 Sentence Correction

Common errors: fragments, run-ons, comma splices, lack of parallelism.

Fragment: Because she was late.
Complete: She missed the bus because she was late.

📌 PARAGRAPH WRITING

🔹 Topic Sentence

States the main idea; usually first sentence. Must be clear and specific.

"I will talk about dogs." (weak)
"Owning a dog requires daily commitment and patience."

🔹 Supporting Details

Facts, examples, reasons that develop the topic sentence.

🔹 Concluding Sentence

Restates the main idea or summarizes; gives closure.

📌 ESSAY WRITING

🔹 Narrative Essays

Tells a story; includes characters, plot, setting, chronological order.

🔹 Descriptive Essays

Creates a vivid picture using sensory details.

🔹 Argumentative Essays

Presents a claim and supports with evidence; includes counterargument.

🔹 Expository Essays

Explains or informs; uses facts, examples, no opinion.

📌 FUNCTIONAL WRITING

🔹 Letters (Formal & Informal)

Formal: sender's address, date, recipient's address, subject, salutation (Dear Sir/Madam), body, complimentary close (Yours faithfully).
Informal: casual tone, friendly greeting, close with "Best wishes".

🔹 Emails

Subject line, concise, professional or friendly depending on context.

🔹 Reports

Title, introduction, findings, conclusion, recommendations.

🔹 Notices & Messages

Clear, to the point, include date, time, venue, contact if needed.

🔹 Applications

Formal; state position, qualifications, reason for applying.

📌 GUIDED & FREE WRITING

🔹 Picture-based writing

Observe details, describe, infer story, use imagination.

🔹 Story completion

Maintain consistent tone, develop plot logically.

🔹 Dialogue writing

Natural conversation, punctuation inside quotes, new line for each speaker.

🔹 Summary writing

Condense main ideas, use own words, omit examples.
