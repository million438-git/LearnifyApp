🔍 INTRODUCTION TO ERROR IDENTIFICATION

Every entrance exam tests your ability to spot and correct common mistakes. This chapter covers tense errors, grammar mistakes, preposition errors, article mistakes, word order errors, and spelling mistakes — with professional explanations.

⏳ 1. TENSE ERRORS

🔸 inconsistent verb tense

Shift in tense within a sentence or paragraph must be logical. Common errors: using present after past, or past after present.

✗ Incorrect: She finished her homework and goes to bed.
(finished – past, goes – present)
✓ Correct: She finished her homework and went to bed.
why: Both actions happened in the past; must use past tense (went).

🔸 present perfect vs past simple

✗ I have seen that movie yesterday.
✓ I saw that movie yesterday. (past simple because yesterday is finished time)
explanation: "yesterday" requires simple past; present perfect is for unspecified time or ongoing.

🔸 conditional / reported speech

If he would have asked me, I would help.
If he had asked me, I would have helped. (third conditional)

📐 2. GRAMMAR MISTAKES

🔸 subject–verb agreement

The team are winning. (if collective noun treated as unit → is)
The team is winning. (American English; BrE "are" possible but exam prefers consistency)
"The team" as a single entity → singular verb. For individuals: "The team are wearing their uniforms."

🔸 pronoun agreement

Everyone must bring their own book. (traditional grammar: everyone singular → his/her)
Everyone must bring his or her own book. (or "their" increasingly accepted, but exam may test formal)

🔸 double negatives

I don't have no money.
I don't have any money. / I have no money.

📍 3. PREPOSITION ERRORS

🔸 wrong preposition after verb

She is married with a doctor.
She is married to a doctor.
He entered into the room.
He entered the room. (enter directly, no preposition)
common patterns: depend on, insist on, proud of, interested in, etc.

🔸 preposition at/in/on for time

I will see you on Monday morning.
I will see you on Monday morning. (actually correct; but often mistake: "in Monday" ✗)
✗ in Friday → on Friday; ✗ at the morning → in the morning

📌 4. ARTICLE MISTAKES

🔸 a/an confusion

She is a honest person.
She is an honest person. (honest starts with vowel sound)

🔸 missing / unnecessary the

I went to the school to study. (if meaning general schooling, no the)
I went to school. (as a student) / I went to the school to pick up my brother. (specific building)

🔄 5. WORD ORDER ERRORS

🔸 adjective order

a red large ball
a large red ball (size before color)

🔸 adverb placement

I drive every day carefully.
I drive carefully every day. (adverb of manner before time)

✏️ 6. SPELLING MISTAKES

🔸 commonly confused

  • accommodation (not accomodation) – double c, double m
  • embarrass (double r, double s)
  • separate (not seperate)
  • definitely (not definately)
  • receive (i before e except after c)
