🔍 MASTERING READING COMPREHENSION

Stories Passages Articles Letters Dialogues Reports

🎯 Main Idea

  • Ask: "What is the text mostly about?"
  • Check first and last sentences; avoid minor details.
  • Incorrect choices are often too broad or too narrow.

🔎 Supporting Details

  • Facts, examples, statistics that back the main idea.
  • Usually found in middle paragraphs.

💡 Inference

  • "Read between the lines" — not directly stated.
  • Combine text clues with your logic.

📖 Vocabulary in Context

  • Use surrounding words to guess meaning.
  • Look for synonyms, antonyms, or explanations nearby.

🔗 Reference Words

  • "it", "they", "this", "such" — find what they refer to.
  • Usually the nearest preceding noun.

✅ True / False Questions

  • Scan for exact wording; beware of 100% absolutes (always, never).

📄 Sample Passage

"The Great Barrier Reef is the world's largest coral reef system. It supports a vast diversity of marine life. However, climate change poses a serious threat, causing coral bleaching. Scientists warn that without immediate action, much of the reef could be lost by 2050."

Main idea: The Great Barrier Reef is under threat from climate change.
Inference: Scientists are concerned about the future of the reef.
"it" refers to The Great Barrier Reef.
