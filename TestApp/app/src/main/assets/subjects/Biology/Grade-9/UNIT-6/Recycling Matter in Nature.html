Recycling Matter in Nature

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Describe the water cycle
  • Explain the carbon cycle
  • Relate decomposers to nutrient recycling
  • Recognize human impacts

Study notes

Matter cycles through living organisms and the environment. In the water cycle, evaporation and transpiration move water vapour into the atmosphere, condensation forms clouds, and precipitation returns water to Earth's surface. In the carbon cycle, photosynthesis removes carbon dioxide from air or water, while respiration, decomposition, and combustion release it. Decomposers return nutrients from dead organisms to soil and water. Human activities can alter these cycles through pollution, deforestation, and fossil-fuel combustion.

Practical or thinking activity

Draw and label a water-cycle diagram showing evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection.

Chapter review

1. What process releases carbon dioxide during the use of food by living cells?
Show answer

Cellular respiration.

2. What role does decomposition play in nutrient cycling?
Show answer

It breaks down organic material and releases nutrients that can be reused by organisms.
