Unit 6 Exam — Ecology

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Instructions

Attempt each question without opening its answer. Then reveal the answer and explain why it is correct. This is a revision assessment, not an official examination paper.

Questions: 10 short-answer questions.

Unit revision questions

1. What is an ecosystem?
Show answer

A system in which organisms interact with one another and with nonliving environmental factors.

2. Give two abiotic factors.
Show answer

Examples include water, light, temperature, air, and soil minerals.

3. What do arrows in a food chain show?
Show answer

The direction of energy transfer from the food source to the organism that consumes it.

4. Why are decomposers important?
Show answer

They break down dead material and wastes, returning nutrients to the environment.

5. What process releases carbon dioxide during the use of food by living cells?
Show answer

Cellular respiration.

6. What role does decomposition play in nutrient cycling?
Show answer

It breaks down organic material and releases nutrients that can be reused by organisms.

7. What is an adaptation?
Show answer

An inherited feature that improves survival or reproduction in a particular environment.

8. Do individual organisms deliberately evolve an inherited adaptation during their lifetime?
Show answer

No. Adaptation in populations develops across generations through evolutionary processes.

9. Why should tree species be selected for local conditions?
Show answer

Suitable species are more likely to survive and support local ecosystems without causing avoidable ecological problems.

10. Give two ways to protect soil and biodiversity.
Show answer

Examples include planting suitable native vegetation, preventing erosion, protecting habitats, and reducing pollution.
