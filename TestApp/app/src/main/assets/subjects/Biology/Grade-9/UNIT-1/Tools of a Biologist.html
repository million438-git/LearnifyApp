Tools of a Biologist

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Match tools to their purposes
  • Record measurements with units
  • Use field notebooks
  • Handle specimens responsibly

Study notes

Biologists use laboratory and field equipment to observe, measure, collect, and study living things. Examples include hand lenses, microscopes, thermometers, balances, measuring cylinders, forceps, specimen containers, quadrats, and field notebooks. Each tool has a suitable purpose and must be handled correctly. Observations should be recorded with units, dates, labels, and enough detail to be understood later.

Practical or thinking activity

Prepare a list of tools needed to investigate the diversity of plants in a school garden without damaging them.

Chapter review

1. Which instrument is used to measure temperature?
Show answer

A thermometer.

2. Why should biological measurements include units?
Show answer

Units identify the scale of a measurement and make results interpretable and comparable.
