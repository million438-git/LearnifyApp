Food Chains, Food Webs, and Energy Flow

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Identify producers and consumers
  • Explain decomposers
  • Draw food chains with correct arrows
  • Compare chains and webs

Study notes

Producers such as green plants capture energy, usually from sunlight, and store it in organic molecules. Consumers obtain energy by eating organisms; decomposers break down dead material and wastes. A food chain shows one pathway of feeding relationships, while a food web connects many chains. Arrows point from the food source to the organism receiving energy. Energy flows through ecosystems and some is dissipated as heat at each transfer, while matter can be recycled.

Practical or thinking activity

Build a food chain using organisms found in a local grassland or farm and explain each arrow.

Chapter review

1. What do arrows in a food chain show?
Show answer

The direction of energy transfer from the food source to the organism that consumes it.

2. Why are decomposers important?
Show answer

They break down dead material and wastes, returning nutrients to the environment.
