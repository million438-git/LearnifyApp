Respiration and Cellular Respiration

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Trace airflow
  • Explain gas exchange
  • Distinguish breathing from cellular respiration
  • State the role of oxygen

Study notes

The respiratory system brings oxygen into the body and removes carbon dioxide. Air passes through the nose or mouth, trachea, bronchi, and bronchioles to the alveoli in the lungs. Gas exchange occurs across thin alveolar walls. Cellular respiration releases usable energy from nutrients; aerobic respiration uses oxygen and releases carbon dioxide and water. Breathing is the movement of air, whereas cellular respiration is a chemical process inside cells. Physical activity increases the body's demand for oxygen and energy.

Practical or thinking activity

Compare breathing rate before and after gentle activity, following teacher instructions and stopping if uncomfortable.

Chapter review

1. Where does gas exchange occur in the lungs?
Show answer

Across the walls of the alveoli.

2. How is breathing different from cellular respiration?
Show answer

Breathing moves air in and out of the lungs; cellular respiration is the cellular process that releases energy from nutrients.
