Grade 9 Biology Final Exam

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Final revision assessment

30 short-answer questions covering all six units. Try to answer from memory first. Use the expandable answer keys to check your understanding.

Questions and answer key

1. What is biology?
Show answer

Biology is the scientific study of living organisms.

2. Give two practical applications of biology.
Show answer

Examples include medicine, agriculture, food production, disease prevention, and conservation.

3. What is a hypothesis?
Show answer

A testable proposed explanation for an observation or question.

4. Identify the independent and dependent variables in a plant-light experiment.
Show answer

Independent variable: light exposure; dependent variable: measured plant growth.

5. Which instrument is used to measure temperature?
Show answer

A thermometer.

6. What is metabolism?
Show answer

The chemical reactions occurring in an organism, including reactions that release or use energy.

7. How can a plant demonstrate a response to a stimulus?
Show answer

A shoot may grow toward light or roots may grow in response to gravity.

8. What is taxonomy?
Show answer

The science of identifying, naming, and classifying organisms.

9. Why do biologists classify organisms?
Show answer

To organize biodiversity, support identification, and communicate about organisms consistently.

10. What two parts make up a binomial scientific name?
Show answer

The genus name and the specific epithet.

11. What is the basic structural and functional unit of life?
Show answer

The cell.

12. Give two structures commonly found in plant cells but not typical animal cells.
Show answer

A cellulose cell wall and chloroplasts in photosynthetic cells; plant cells also commonly have a large central vacuole.

13. What is the main distinction between prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells?
Show answer

Eukaryotic cells have a membrane-bound nucleus; prokaryotic cells do not.

14. Put cell, tissue, organ, and organ system in order.
Show answer

Cell → tissue → organ → organ system.

15. Why is a thin specimen useful for a light microscope?
Show answer

It allows sufficient light to pass through for viewing.

16. What is one major difference between sexual and asexual reproduction?
Show answer

Sexual reproduction involves gamete fusion and usually creates more genetic variation; asexual reproduction generally does not.

17. Why is reproduction important to a species?
Show answer

It produces offspring and helps maintain the species across generations.

18. What is budding?
Show answer

A form of asexual reproduction in which a new individual grows as an outgrowth from the parent.

19. Give one example of vegetative propagation.
Show answer

New plants developing from potato tubers, runners, bulbs, stems, or suitable cuttings.

20. Where are sperm produced?
Show answer

In the testes.

21. Which nutrient is especially important for growth and tissue repair?
Show answer

Protein.

22. Why is water essential to the body?
Show answer

It supports transport, chemical reactions, temperature regulation, and other body functions.

23. Where are most digested nutrients absorbed?
Show answer

In the small intestine.

24. What is the difference between mechanical and chemical digestion?
Show answer

Mechanical digestion physically breaks food into smaller pieces; chemical digestion breaks molecules down through chemical reactions, often using enzymes.

25. Where does gas exchange occur in the lungs?
Show answer

Across the walls of the alveoli.

26. What is an ecosystem?
Show answer

A system in which organisms interact with one another and with nonliving environmental factors.

27. Give two abiotic factors.
Show answer

Examples include water, light, temperature, air, and soil minerals.

28. What do arrows in a food chain show?
Show answer

The direction of energy transfer from the food source to the organism that consumes it.

29. Why are decomposers important?
Show answer

They break down dead material and wastes, returning nutrients to the environment.

30. What process releases carbon dioxide during the use of food by living cells?
Show answer

Cellular respiration.
