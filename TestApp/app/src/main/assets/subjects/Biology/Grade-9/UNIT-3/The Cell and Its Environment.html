Study notes

Particles move in and out of cells through the selectively permeable membrane. Diffusion is the net movement of particles from higher to lower concentration. Osmosis is the net movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane from a region of higher water potential toward lower water potential. Active transport uses energy to move substances against a concentration gradient. Water movement can change cell volume; plant cell walls help resist excessive expansion. Transport is essential for nutrient uptake, gas exchange, and waste removal.

Practical or thinking activity

Chapter review

1. How does diffusion differ from active transport?
Show answer

Diffusion is passive net movement down a concentration gradient; active transport uses energy to move substances against a gradient.

2. What is osmosis?
Show answer

The net movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane due to a water-potential difference.
