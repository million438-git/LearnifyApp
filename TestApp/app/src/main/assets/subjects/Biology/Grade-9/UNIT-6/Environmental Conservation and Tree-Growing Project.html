Environmental Conservation and Tree-Growing Project

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Explain conservation
  • Identify local environmental problems
  • Plan a safe tree-growing project
  • Monitor results

Study notes

Conservation protects biodiversity, soil, water, and ecosystem services. Practical actions include reducing pollution, restoring habitats, preventing erosion, planting appropriate native species, managing waste, and using natural resources sustainably. Tree-growing projects work best when species suit local conditions, seedlings are planted at appropriate times, soil and water needs are considered, and young trees are protected and monitored. Conservation planning should consider community needs, local ecological knowledge, and evidence about likely outcomes.

Practical or thinking activity

Design a school conservation project with a clear objective, responsible participants, resources, schedule, and monitoring method.

Chapter review

1. Why should tree species be selected for local conditions?
Show answer

Suitable species are more likely to survive and support local ecosystems without causing avoidable ecological problems.

2. Give two ways to protect soil and biodiversity.
Show answer

Examples include planting suitable native vegetation, preventing erosion, protecting habitats, and reducing pollution.
