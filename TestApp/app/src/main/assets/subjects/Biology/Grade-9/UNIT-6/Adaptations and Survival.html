Adaptations and Survival

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Define adaptation
  • Compare structural, behavioural, and physiological adaptations
  • Relate features to environments
  • Explain adaptation over generations

Study notes

An adaptation is an inherited feature that improves survival or reproduction in a particular environment. Structural adaptations include body shape or protective coverings; behavioural adaptations include migration or activity patterns; physiological adaptations include internal processes such as water conservation. Adaptations arise over generations through evolutionary processes, not because an individual deliberately develops a needed inherited trait. A feature that is helpful in one environment may be less useful in another.

Practical or thinking activity

Select a local organism and connect one observable feature to a possible survival function, distinguishing evidence from a guess.
