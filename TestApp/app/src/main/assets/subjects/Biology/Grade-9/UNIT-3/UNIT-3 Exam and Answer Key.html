Unit 3 Exam — Cells

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Instructions

Attempt each question without opening its answer. Then reveal the answer and explain why it is correct. This is a revision assessment, not an official examination paper.

Questions: 8 short-answer questions.

Unit revision questions

1. What is the basic structural and functional unit of life?
Show answer

The cell.

2. Give two structures commonly found in plant cells but not typical animal cells.
Show answer

A cellulose cell wall and chloroplasts in photosynthetic cells; plant cells also commonly have a large central vacuole.

3. What is the main distinction between prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells?
Show answer

Eukaryotic cells have a membrane-bound nucleus; prokaryotic cells do not.

4. Put cell, tissue, organ, and organ system in order.
Show answer

Cell → tissue → organ → organ system.

5. Why is a thin specimen useful for a light microscope?
Show answer

It allows sufficient light to pass through for viewing.

6. What is resolution?
Show answer

The ability to distinguish two close points as separate details.

7. How does diffusion differ from active transport?
Show answer

Diffusion is passive net movement down a concentration gradient; active transport uses energy to move substances against a gradient.

8. What is osmosis?
Show answer

The net movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane due to a water-potential difference.
