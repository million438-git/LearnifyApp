Cell Theory and Cell Structure

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • State the main principles of cell theory
  • Identify cell structures
  • Link structures to functions
  • Compare plant and animal cells

Study notes

Cell theory states that living organisms are made of one or more cells, the cell is the basic unit of life, and new cells arise from existing cells. The cell membrane regulates movement into and out of the cell. Cytoplasm contains cellular structures and is the site of many reactions. The nucleus of typical eukaryotic cells contains genetic material. Mitochondria are involved in aerobic respiration; ribosomes build proteins; plant cells commonly have a cellulose cell wall, chloroplasts in photosynthetic tissues, and a large central vacuole.

Practical or thinking activity

Draw a labelled plant or animal cell and connect each labelled structure to its function.

Chapter review

1. What is the basic structural and functional unit of life?
Show answer

The cell.
