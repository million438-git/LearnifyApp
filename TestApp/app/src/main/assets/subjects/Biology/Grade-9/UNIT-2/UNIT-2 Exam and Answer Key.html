Unit 2 Exam — Characteristics and Classification of Organisms

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Instructions

Attempt each question without opening its answer. Then reveal the answer and explain why it is correct. This is a revision assessment, not an official examination paper.

Questions: 10 short-answer questions.

Unit revision questions

1. What is metabolism?
Show answer

The chemical reactions occurring in an organism, including reactions that release or use energy.

2. How can a plant demonstrate a response to a stimulus?
Show answer

A shoot may grow toward light or roots may grow in response to gravity.

3. What is taxonomy?
Show answer

The science of identifying, naming, and classifying organisms.

4. Why do biologists classify organisms?
Show answer

To organize biodiversity, support identification, and communicate about organisms consistently.

5. What two parts make up a binomial scientific name?
Show answer

The genus name and the specific epithet.

6. Which comes first: family or genus?
Show answer

Genus comes before family in the hierarchy from broad to specific.

7. How do fungi usually obtain nutrients?
Show answer

They absorb nutrients from organic material or other sources.

8. Which kingdom traditionally includes organisms that photosynthesize and have cellulose cell walls?
Show answer

Plantae.

9. What is biodiversity?
Show answer

The variety of life within species, between species, and across ecosystems.

10. Name two threats to biodiversity.
Show answer

Examples include habitat loss, pollution, overharvesting, and invasive species.
