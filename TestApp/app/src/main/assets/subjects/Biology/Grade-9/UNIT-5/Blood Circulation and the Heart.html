Blood Circulation and the Heart

Learning objectives

  • Describe the heart's pumping role
  • Compare arteries, veins, and capillaries
  • Identify blood components
  • Explain circulation

Study notes

The circulatory system transports oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and wastes. The heart pumps blood through pulmonary circulation between the heart and lungs and systemic circulation between the heart and the rest of the body. Arteries carry blood away from the heart, veins carry blood toward it, and capillaries allow exchange with tissues. Blood includes plasma, red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets. Exercise, balanced nutrition, and avoiding tobacco exposure support cardiovascular health.

Practical or thinking activity

Draw a simplified diagram showing blood travelling from the heart to the lungs and back, then to the body and back.

Chapter review

1. What is the main function of capillaries?
Show answer

They allow exchange of gases, nutrients, and wastes between blood and tissues.

2. What is the difference between arteries and veins?
Show answer

Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry blood toward the heart.
