Definition and Importance of Biology

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Define biology
  • Identify major branches
  • Explain applications of biology

Study notes

Biology is the scientific study of living organisms and their interactions with one another and the environment. Its branches include botany (plants), zoology (animals), microbiology (microorganisms), ecology (organisms and environments), and genetics (heredity). Biology supports agriculture, medicine, conservation, biotechnology, and public health. In Ethiopia, biological knowledge is important for improving crop production, protecting biodiversity, managing livestock, and understanding local health and environmental challenges.

Practical or thinking activity

Choose a local plant or animal and explain how biological knowledge could help farmers or communities.
