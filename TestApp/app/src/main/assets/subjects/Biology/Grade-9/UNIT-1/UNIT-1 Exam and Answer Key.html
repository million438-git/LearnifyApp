Unit 1 Exam — Introduction to Biology

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Instructions

Attempt each question without opening its answer. Then reveal the answer and explain why it is correct. This is a revision assessment, not an official examination paper.

Questions: 8 short-answer questions.

Unit revision questions

1. What is biology?
Show answer

Biology is the scientific study of living organisms.

2. Give two practical applications of biology.
Show answer

Examples include medicine, agriculture, food production, disease prevention, and conservation.

3. What is a hypothesis?
Show answer

A testable proposed explanation for an observation or question.

4. Identify the independent and dependent variables in a plant-light experiment.
Show answer

Independent variable: light exposure; dependent variable: measured plant growth.

5. Which instrument is used to measure temperature?
Show answer

A thermometer.

6. Why should biological measurements include units?
Show answer

Units identify the scale of a measurement and make results interpretable and comparable.

7. How is total microscope magnification calculated?
Show answer

Multiply the eyepiece magnification by the objective magnification.

8. Name two laboratory safety rules.
Show answer

For example, follow instructions and never taste laboratory materials.
