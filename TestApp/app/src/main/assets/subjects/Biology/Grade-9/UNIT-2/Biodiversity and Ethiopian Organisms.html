Biodiversity and Ethiopian Organisms

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Define biodiversity
  • Relate organisms to habitats
  • Explain threats
  • Describe conservation actions

Study notes

Ethiopia contains varied habitats, including highlands, forests, grasslands, wetlands, and drylands. Biodiversity includes variation within species, between species, and among ecosystems. Local plants, crops, livestock, and wildlife can be studied using careful observation and reliable identification guides. Habitat loss, pollution, overharvesting, invasive species, and climate pressures can threaten biodiversity. Conservation combines scientific evidence, responsible land use, and community participation.

Practical or thinking activity

Choose one locally familiar organism and identify its habitat, ecological role, and a possible conservation concern.
