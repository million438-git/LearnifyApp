Menstrual Cycle, Fertilization, and Pregnancy

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Define menstruation and ovulation
  • Describe fertilization
  • Outline early development
  • Understand individual variation

Study notes

The menstrual cycle involves coordinated changes in the ovaries and uterine lining under hormonal control. Ovulation is the release of an egg from an ovary. Fertilization usually occurs in a fallopian tube when a sperm cell fuses with an egg, forming a zygote. The developing embryo implants in the uterine lining and later develops as a fetus. Cycle length and timing vary, especially during adolescence. Pregnancy and reproductive concerns should be discussed with a qualified health professional.

Practical or thinking activity

Put these events in order: ovulation, fertilization, implantation, embryo development.
