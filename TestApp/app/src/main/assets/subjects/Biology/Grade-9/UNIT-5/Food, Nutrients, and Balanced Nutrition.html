Food, Nutrients, and Balanced Nutrition

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Identify major nutrient groups
  • Explain their functions
  • Describe a balanced diet
  • Recognize the importance of water and fibre

Study notes

Food supplies energy and materials for growth, repair, and body functions. Carbohydrates and fats provide energy; proteins support growth and tissue repair; vitamins and minerals support normal body processes; water is essential for transport and temperature regulation; and dietary fibre supports bowel function. A balanced diet varies with age, activity, health, and food availability. A varied diet using locally available foods can help provide essential nutrients. Food safety and clean water are also important for health.

Practical or thinking activity

Plan a balanced meal using locally available foods and explain how its components contribute to nutrition.
