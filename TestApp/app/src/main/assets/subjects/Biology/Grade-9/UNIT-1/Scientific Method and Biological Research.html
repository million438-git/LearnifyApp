Study notes

Scientific investigation often begins with an observation or question. A researcher gathers background information, proposes a testable hypothesis, designs a fair investigation, records observations or measurements, analyses evidence, and draws a conclusion. Results should be communicated so others can evaluate or repeat the work. An independent variable is deliberately changed; a dependent variable is measured; controlled variables are kept as constant as practical. Evidence may support a hypothesis without proving it permanently.

Practical or thinking activity

Compare the growth of two similar plants while changing only the amount of light; keep water, soil, and container size as similar as possible.
