Unit 5 Exam — Human Health, Nutrition, and Disease

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Instructions

Attempt each question without opening its answer. Then reveal the answer and explain why it is correct. This is a revision assessment, not an official examination paper.

Questions: 12 short-answer questions.

Unit revision questions

1. Which nutrient is especially important for growth and tissue repair?
Show answer

Protein.

2. Why is water essential to the body?
Show answer

It supports transport, chemical reactions, temperature regulation, and other body functions.

3. Where are most digested nutrients absorbed?
Show answer

In the small intestine.

4. What is the difference between mechanical and chemical digestion?
Show answer

Mechanical digestion physically breaks food into smaller pieces; chemical digestion breaks molecules down through chemical reactions, often using enzymes.

5. Where does gas exchange occur in the lungs?
Show answer

Across the walls of the alveoli.

6. How is breathing different from cellular respiration?
Show answer

Breathing moves air in and out of the lungs; cellular respiration is the cellular process that releases energy from nutrients.

7. What is the main function of capillaries?
Show answer

They allow exchange of gases, nutrients, and wastes between blood and tissues.

8. What is the difference between arteries and veins?
Show answer

Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry blood toward the heart.

9. Do antibiotics treat viral infections such as influenza?
Show answer

No. Antibiotics target bacteria and do not treat viruses.

10. Name three ways infectious diseases can be prevented.
Show answer

Examples include handwashing, safe water, sanitation, vaccination where available, ventilation, and vector control.

11. What does vaccination help the immune system do?
Show answer

Recognize a pathogen or its components and respond more effectively to future exposure.

12. Can mosquitoes transmit HIV?
Show answer

No. HIV is not transmitted by mosquito bites.
