The Digestive System

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Trace the path of food
  • Compare mechanical and chemical digestion
  • Explain absorption
  • Describe digestive health practices

Study notes

Digestion breaks large food molecules into smaller substances that can be absorbed. Mechanical digestion includes chewing and stomach mixing; chemical digestion uses enzymes and digestive fluids. The mouth begins digestion, the oesophagus transports food, the stomach mixes food and begins substantial protein digestion, and the small intestine completes much digestion and absorbs most nutrients. The large intestine absorbs water and helps form faeces. A healthy diet, clean food, and handwashing help protect digestive health.

Practical or thinking activity

Draw the digestive tract and label the organs in the order food passes through them.

Chapter review

1. Where are most digested nutrients absorbed?
Show answer

In the small intestine.

2. What is the difference between mechanical and chemical digestion?
Show answer

Mechanical digestion physically breaks food into smaller pieces; chemical digestion breaks molecules down through chemical reactions, often using enzymes.
