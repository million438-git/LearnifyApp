Reproductive Health and STI Prevention

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Explain reproductive health
  • Recognize STI transmission routes
  • Describe prevention and testing
  • Correct HIV transmission myths

Study notes

Reproductive health includes physical, emotional, and social well-being related to the reproductive system. Sexually transmitted infections can spread through sexual contact and, for some infections, through blood or from parent to child. Prevention includes accurate education, reducing exposure to infection, correct condom use where relevant, testing, vaccination for preventable infections such as HPV and hepatitis B where recommended, and timely clinical care. HIV is not spread by ordinary social contact such as hugging or sharing classroom space. Medical decisions should rely on qualified health services, not stigma or rumours.

Practical or thinking activity

Make a factual, non-stigmatizing poster explaining how infections can be prevented and where reliable health advice can be obtained.

Chapter review

1. Can HIV spread through ordinary hugging or sharing a classroom?
Show answer

No. Ordinary social contact such as hugging and sharing classroom space does not transmit HIV.

2. Name two ways to reduce STI risk.
Show answer

Examples include accurate education, appropriate barrier protection, vaccination where recommended, testing, and prompt medical care.
