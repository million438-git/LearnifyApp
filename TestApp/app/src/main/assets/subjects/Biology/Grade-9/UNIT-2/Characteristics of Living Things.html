Characteristics of Living Things

Grade 9 Biology • Ethiopian curriculum-oriented learning resource

Learning objectives

  • Cellular organization
  • Metabolism
  • Growth and response
  • Homeostasis and reproduction

Study notes

Living organisms generally show cellular organization, metabolism, growth and development, response to stimuli, homeostasis, reproduction, and the ability to adapt across generations. Movement does not always mean an organism changes location: a plant shoot turning toward light is movement or response. No single visible feature should be used alone to decide whether something is alive; scientists consider a collection of characteristics and evidence.

Practical or thinking activity

Observe a plant and list evidence of growth, response, and maintenance of life.

Chapter review

1. What is metabolism?
Show answer

The chemical reactions occurring in an organism, including reactions that release or use energy.

2. How can a plant demonstrate a response to a stimulus?
Show answer

A shoot may grow toward light or roots may grow in response to gravity.
