Biodiversity and Conservation

Learning objective: Understand biodiversity and conservation.

Explanation

Biodiversity includes variation among genes, species, and ecosystems. Habitat loss, pollution, invasive species, and overexploitation can threaten biodiversity. Conservation aims to protect species and ecological processes.

Key points

  • Protected areas conserve habitats and wildlife.
  • Restoration can help damaged ecosystems recover.
  • Sustainable resource use reduces long-term pressure.
  • Local community participation supports conservation.

Practical activity

Choose a local environmental issue and propose two realistic actions that students or communities could take.

Check your understanding

Question: Give one cause of biodiversity loss and one conservation response.

Show answer

Example: habitat loss; protect or restore the affected habitat.
