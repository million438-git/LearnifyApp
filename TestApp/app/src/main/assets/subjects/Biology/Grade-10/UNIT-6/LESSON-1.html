Ecosystems and Energy Flow

Learning objective: Understand ecosystems and energy flow.

Explanation

An ecosystem includes organisms and the physical environment with which they interact. Producers capture energy, consumers obtain energy by eating organisms, and decomposers break down dead material.

Key points

  • A food chain shows one pathway of energy transfer.
  • Food webs connect many food chains.
  • Energy is lost between trophic levels, including as heat.
  • Decomposers recycle nutrients into the environment.

Practical activity

Create a local food chain using organisms you know. Mark the producer and each consumer.

Check your understanding

Question: What is the role of a producer in a food chain?

Show answer

It makes organic food, usually using sunlight through photosynthesis.
