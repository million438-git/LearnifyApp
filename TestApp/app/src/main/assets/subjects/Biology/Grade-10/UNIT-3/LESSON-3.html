Water, Minerals and Food Tests

Learning objective: Understand water, minerals and food tests.

Explanation

Water is an important solvent and participates in transport and chemical reactions. Mineral ions are required in small quantities for normal growth and metabolism. Food tests can provide evidence for particular nutrients.

Key points

  • Iodine solution can test for starch.
  • Benedict's reagent with appropriate heating can test for reducing sugars.
  • Biuret reagent can indicate proteins.
  • Food tests require safe procedures and suitable controls.

Practical activity

Make a table predicting which food samples may contain starch, reducing sugars, or protein. Only perform laboratory tests with teacher supervision.

Check your understanding

Question: What colour change indicates a positive iodine test for starch?

Show answer

Iodine changes from brown-orange toward blue-black.
