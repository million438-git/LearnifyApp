Relationships and Population Change

Learning objective: Understand relationships and population change.

Explanation

Organisms interact through competition, predation, parasitism, mutualism, and other relationships. Population size can change with births, deaths, immigration, emigration, and resource availability.

Key points

  • Competition occurs when organisms need the same limited resource.
  • Predators feed on prey.
  • Mutualism benefits both interacting species.
  • Food, water, habitat, disease, and climate can influence populations.

Practical activity

Identify one possible competition or mutualism example in a garden, farm, or nearby habitat.

Check your understanding

Question: What is mutualism?

Show answer

An interaction in which both species benefit.
