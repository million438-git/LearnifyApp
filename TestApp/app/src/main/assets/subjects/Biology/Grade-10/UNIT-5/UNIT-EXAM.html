Human Biology - Unit Exam

Answer each question before opening its answer. This is Learnify practice material, not an official examination.

Question 1

Where does most nutrient absorption occur?

Reveal answer

In the small intestine.

Question 2

What is the main function of alveoli?

Reveal answer

They provide a large surface for gas exchange in the lungs.

Question 3

What is homeostasis?

Reveal answer

The regulation of internal conditions within limits suitable for normal body function.
