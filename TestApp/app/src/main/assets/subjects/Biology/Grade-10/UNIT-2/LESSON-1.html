Plant Structure and Functions

Learning objective: Understand plant structure and functions.

Explanation

Plants have structures specialized for survival. Roots commonly anchor plants and absorb water and minerals. Stems support leaves and transport materials. Leaves are major sites of photosynthesis.

Key points

  • Root hairs increase the surface area for absorption.
  • Xylem transports water and mineral ions.
  • Phloem transports sugars and other organic substances.
  • Stomata allow gas exchange and contribute to water loss.

Practical activity

Observe a non-protected plant. Identify its roots, stem, and leaves, and describe the function of each.

Check your understanding
