Biology Reference Sheet

Quick reference

  • Photosynthesis: carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (light and chlorophyll required).
  • Plant transport: xylem carries water and mineral ions; phloem carries sugars and other organic substances.
  • Cell organization: cell → tissue → organ → organ system → organism.
  • Mitosis: supports growth and repair; typically produces two genetically similar daughter cells.
  • Meiosis: reduces chromosome number and contributes to genetic variation.
  • Ecology: producers capture energy; consumers feed on organisms; decomposers break down dead material.
  • Homeostasis: regulation of internal conditions within suitable limits.
