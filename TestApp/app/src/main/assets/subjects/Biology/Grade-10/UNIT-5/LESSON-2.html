Circulation, Respiration and Excretion

Learning objective: Understand circulation, respiration and excretion.

Explanation

The circulatory system transports oxygen, nutrients, and wastes. The respiratory system exchanges oxygen and carbon dioxide. The kidneys help remove wastes and regulate water and ion balance.

Key points

  • The heart pumps blood through vessels.
  • Gas exchange occurs in the alveoli of the lungs.
  • Red blood cells contain haemoglobin that carries oxygen.
  • Kidneys filter blood and help maintain internal balance.

Practical activity

Measure your resting pulse safely, then compare it with your pulse after gentle walking. Stop if you feel unwell.

Check your understanding

Question: What is the main function of alveoli?

Show answer

They provide a large surface for gas exchange in the lungs.
