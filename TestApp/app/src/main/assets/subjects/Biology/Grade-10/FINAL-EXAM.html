Grade 10 Biology Final Practice Exam

Try these revision questions first. Open each answer only after you have attempted the question.

Question 1

Which branch of biology studies plants?

Reveal answer

Botany.

Question 2

Name the tissue that transports water in plants.

Reveal answer

Xylem.

Question 3

What are the raw materials of photosynthesis?

Reveal answer

Carbon dioxide and water.

Question 4

What is the role of enzymes?

Reveal answer

They speed up biological reactions.

Question 5

What happens during anaphase of mitosis?

Reveal answer

Sister chromatids separate.

Question 6

How many chromosomes are in a haploid cell if the diploid number is 16?

Reveal answer

8 chromosomes.

Question 7

Where does most nutrient absorption occur in humans?

Reveal answer

The small intestine.

Question 8

Define homeostasis.

Reveal answer

Regulation of internal conditions within suitable limits.

Question 9

What is mutualism?

Reveal answer

An interaction in which both species benefit.

Question 10

Give one way to conserve biodiversity.

Reveal answer

Protect or restore habitats, among other approaches.
