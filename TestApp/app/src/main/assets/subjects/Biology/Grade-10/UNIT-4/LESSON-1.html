Cell Cycle and Chromosomes

Learning objective: Understand cell cycle and chromosomes.

Explanation

The cell cycle includes growth, DNA replication, and cell division. Chromosomes contain DNA and associated proteins. DNA must be copied before a cell divides so genetic information can be passed on.

Key points

  • Interphase includes growth and DNA replication.
  • Chromosomes become more visible during cell division.
  • DNA carries genetic instructions.
  • Cell-cycle regulation helps coordinate normal growth and division.

Practical activity

Draw a simple cycle showing cell growth, DNA replication, and division. Explain why DNA replication happens before division.

Check your understanding

Question: Why must DNA be replicated before a typical cell divides?

Show answer

So each daughter cell can receive a copy of the genetic information.
