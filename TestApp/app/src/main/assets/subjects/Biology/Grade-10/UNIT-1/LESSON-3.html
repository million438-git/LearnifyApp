Scientific Investigation in Biology

Learning objective: Understand scientific investigation in biology.

Explanation

Biologists investigate questions using observations and testable hypotheses. A fair experiment changes one independent variable, measures a dependent variable, and controls other relevant factors.

Key points

  • Record observations accurately.
  • Use a suitable comparison or control when possible.
  • Repeat measurements to improve reliability.
  • Draw conclusions from evidence rather than expectation.

Practical activity

Plan a safe investigation comparing seed germination under two light conditions while keeping seed type, water, and container similar.

Check your understanding

Question: Why should important variables be controlled in a fair experiment?

Show answer

To help determine whether the variable being tested caused the observed difference.
