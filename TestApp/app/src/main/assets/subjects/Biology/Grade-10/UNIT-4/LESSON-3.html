Meiosis and Genetic Variation

Learning objective: Understand meiosis and genetic variation.

Explanation

Meiosis is a specialized type of cell division that reduces chromosome number and produces haploid cells. In sexually reproducing organisms, it helps form gametes or spores, depending on the life cycle.

Key points

  • Meiosis involves two successive divisions.
  • The chromosome number is reduced by half.
  • Crossing over can exchange DNA between homologous chromosomes.
  • Independent assortment and fertilization contribute to variation.

Practical activity
