Carbohydrates and Lipids

Learning objective: Understand carbohydrates and lipids.

Explanation

Living organisms contain organic molecules needed for energy, structure, and other functions. Carbohydrates include sugars and starch. Lipids include fats and oils.

Key points

  • Glucose is a simple sugar used in cellular respiration.
  • Starch is a storage carbohydrate in many plants.
  • Lipids provide concentrated energy storage.
  • Some lipids are important components of cell membranes.

Practical activity

Read food labels on packaged foods, if available. Compare the carbohydrate and fat amounts per serving.
