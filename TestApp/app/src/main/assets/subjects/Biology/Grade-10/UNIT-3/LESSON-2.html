Proteins, Enzymes and Nucleic Acids

Learning objective: Understand proteins, enzymes and nucleic acids.

Explanation

Proteins are made from amino acids and have structural and functional roles. Enzymes are biological catalysts that speed up reactions. DNA and RNA are nucleic acids involved in genetic information.

Key points

  • Protein shape affects its function.
  • Enzymes act on particular substrates.
  • Temperature and pH can affect enzyme activity.
  • DNA stores hereditary information; RNA helps use that information.

Practical activity

Explain why extreme heat can change the shape of a protein and interfere with its function.

Check your understanding

Question: What is the main role of an enzyme in a biological reaction?
