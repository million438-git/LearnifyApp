Coordination, Hormones and Homeostasis

Learning objective: Understand coordination, hormones and homeostasis.

Explanation

The nervous system uses electrical signals and chemical messengers to coordinate rapid responses. Endocrine glands release hormones into the blood. Homeostasis keeps internal conditions within suitable limits.

Key points

  • Receptors detect stimuli.
  • The central nervous system includes the brain and spinal cord.
  • Hormones can regulate growth, metabolism, and reproduction.
  • Negative feedback helps counter changes away from a normal range.

Practical activity

Describe how the body responds when it becomes hot, including sweating and changes in heat loss.

Check your understanding

Question: What is homeostasis?

Show answer

The regulation of internal conditions within limits suitable for normal body function.
