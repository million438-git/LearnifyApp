What Biology Studies

Learning objective: Understand what biology studies.

Explanation

Biology is the scientific study of living organisms and their interactions with the environment. It uses observation, evidence, experiments, and classification to explain life.

Key points

  • Botany studies plants.
  • Zoology studies animals.
  • Microbiology studies microorganisms.
  • Ecology studies interactions between organisms and their environment.

Practical activity

List five living organisms around your home or school. Record where each lives and one feature that helps it survive.
