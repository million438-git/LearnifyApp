Photosynthesis and Plant Nutrition

Learning objective: Understand photosynthesis and plant nutrition.

Explanation

Photosynthesis uses light energy to make sugars from carbon dioxide and water. Chlorophyll absorbs light, and oxygen is released during the process.

Key points

  • Word equation: carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen.
  • Light and chlorophyll are required for the usual photosynthesis process.
  • Light intensity, carbon dioxide availability, and temperature can affect the rate.
  • Plants use sugars for respiration, growth, and storage.

Practical activity

Draw a leaf and label the inputs and outputs of photosynthesis. Do not taste or consume any plant material.

Check your understanding

Question: Name two raw materials needed for photosynthesis.

Show answer

Carbon dioxide and water.
