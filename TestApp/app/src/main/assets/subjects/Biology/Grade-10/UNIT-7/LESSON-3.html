Exam Preparation and Study Strategies

Learning objective: Understand exam preparation and study strategies.

Explanation

Effective revision combines retrieval practice, spaced review, diagrams, explanations in your own words, and checking errors. Practise explaining why an answer is correct.

Key points

  • Review difficult topics more frequently.
  • Attempt questions before checking answers.
  • Keep an error log and revisit misunderstandings.
  • Use diagrams when they clarify a process or structure.

Practical activity

Complete the unit exams, mark your answers, and make a list of three topics that need further review.

Check your understanding

Question: Why is it useful to review incorrect answers after a practice test?

Show answer

It helps identify misunderstandings and guide focused revision.
