Human Body Organization and Nutrition

Learning objective: Understand human body organization and nutrition.

Explanation

Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs work together in organ systems. The digestive system breaks food into absorbable substances, while the circulatory system transports nutrients.

Key points

  • The small intestine absorbs most digested nutrients.
  • The blood transports many nutrients around the body.
  • A balanced diet includes a variety of nutrients and water.
  • Digestion involves mechanical and chemical processes.

Practical activity

Draw the pathway of food through the digestive tract and note where digestion or absorption occurs.
