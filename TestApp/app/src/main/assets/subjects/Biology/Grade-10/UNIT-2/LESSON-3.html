Plant Transport, Reproduction and Responses

Learning objective: Understand plant transport, reproduction and responses.

Explanation

Plants transport water, minerals, and sugars through specialized tissues. Many flowering plants reproduce sexually through pollination and fertilization. Plants also respond to environmental stimuli.

Key points

  • Pollination transfers pollen to a receptive stigma.
  • Fertilization involves fusion of male and female gametes.
  • Tropisms are directional growth responses to stimuli.
  • Shoots often grow toward light; roots commonly respond to gravity.

Practical activity

Observe a potted plant near a window over several days. Record the direction of shoot growth without damaging the plant.

Check your understanding

Question: What is phototropism?

Show answer

A growth response to light.
