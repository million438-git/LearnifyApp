Plants - Unit Exam

Answer each question before opening its answer. This is Learnify practice material, not an official examination.

Question 1

Which plant tissue transports water from roots toward leaves?

Reveal answer

Xylem.

Question 2

Name two raw materials needed for photosynthesis.

Reveal answer

Carbon dioxide and water.

Question 3

What is phototropism?

Reveal answer

A growth response to light.
