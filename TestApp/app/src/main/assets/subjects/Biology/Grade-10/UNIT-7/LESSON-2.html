Scientific Skills and Data Interpretation

Learning objective: Understand scientific skills and data interpretation.

Explanation

Biology questions may ask you to interpret tables, graphs, experimental designs, and observations. Identify variables, units, patterns, and evidence before drawing conclusions.

Key points

  • Read graph axes and units first.
  • Describe trends using data where possible.
  • Distinguish correlation from demonstrated causation.
  • State limitations when evidence is incomplete.

Practical activity

Sketch a graph of a hypothetical plant's height over four weeks and write two conclusions supported by your graph.

Check your understanding

Question: What should you check first when interpreting a scientific graph?

Show answer

The title, axes, units, and what each variable represents.
