Ecological Interaction - Unit Exam

Answer each question before opening its answer. This is Learnify practice material, not an official examination.

Question 1

What is the role of a producer in a food chain?

Reveal answer

It makes organic food, usually using sunlight through photosynthesis.

Question 2

What is mutualism?

Reveal answer

An interaction in which both species benefit.

Question 3

Give one cause of biodiversity loss and one conservation response.

Reveal answer

Example: habitat loss; protect or restore the affected habitat.
