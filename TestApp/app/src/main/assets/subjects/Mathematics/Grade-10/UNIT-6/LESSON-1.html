Collecting and Representing Data

Data may be numerical or categorical. Tables, bar charts, line graphs, and histograms help organize and communicate information.

Label graph axes, include units where appropriate, and choose a scale that represents the data accurately. A bar chart often compares categories; a line graph often shows change over time.
