Grade 10 Mathematics Study Plan

  1. Day 1: Real numbers and sets.
  2. Day 2: Relations and functions.
  3. Day 3: Polynomials and factorization.
  4. Day 4: Equations and inequalities.
  5. Day 5: Geometry and measurement.
  6. Day 6: Statistics and probability.
  7. Day 7: Sequences, revision, and final exam.

One-hour session: 10 minutes review, 30 minutes learning and examples, 15 minutes independent practice, and 5 minutes recording mistakes.
