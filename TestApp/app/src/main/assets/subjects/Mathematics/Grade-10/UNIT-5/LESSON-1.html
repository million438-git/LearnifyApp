Angles and Triangles

Angles on a straight line total 180 degrees. Angles around a point total 360 degrees. The interior angles of a triangle total 180 degrees.

If a triangle has angles 50 and 60 degrees, its third angle is 180 - 50 - 60 = 70 degrees.
