Number Patterns and Sequences

A sequence is an ordered list of numbers. An arithmetic sequence has a constant difference between consecutive terms.

Example: 4, 7, 10, 13 has common difference 3. Its nth term is first term + (n - 1) times the common difference.

Practice

  1. Find the next term: 2, 5, 8, 11.
  2. Find the common difference: 12, 8, 4, 0.
  3. Find the fifth term of 3, 6, 9, ...
