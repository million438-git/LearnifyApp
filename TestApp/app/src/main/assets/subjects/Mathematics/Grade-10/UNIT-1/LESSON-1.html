Real Number Systems

Real numbers include rational and irrational numbers. Rational numbers can be written as a fraction of integers with a nonzero denominator. Irrational numbers cannot be written as such a fraction.

Examples

  • Rational: -3, 0.5, 2/7, 0.333...
  • Irrational: square root of 2 and pi.

Integers include negative whole numbers, zero, and positive whole numbers. Natural numbers are counting numbers, although conventions about including zero vary.

Practice

  2. Is square root of 9 rational?
  3. Give one irrational number.
