Basic Probability

For equally likely outcomes, probability = favorable outcomes divided by total possible outcomes. Probability ranges from 0 to 1.

A fair six-sided die has probability 1/6 of showing a 4 and 3/6 = 1/2 of showing an even number.

Practice

  1. What is the probability of heads on a fair coin?
  2. What is the probability of rolling a 7 on a standard die?
  3. What is the probability of an even result on a fair six-sided die?
