Exponents and Scientific Notation

For nonzero a, a^m times a^n = a^(m+n), and a^m divided by a^n = a^(m-n). Scientific notation expresses a number as a times 10^n, where 1 <= absolute value of a < 10.

Examples: 45000 = 4.5 times 10^4; 0.0032 = 3.2 times 10^-3.
