Perimeter, Area and Volume

Rectangle area = length times width. Triangle area = one half times base times height. Rectangular prism volume = length times width times height.

Examples

  • Rectangle 5 cm by 3 cm: area = 15 square cm.
  • Triangle with base 8 cm and height 4 cm: area = 16 square cm.
  • Box 2 cm by 3 cm by 4 cm: volume = 24 cubic cm.

Practice

  1. Find the area of a 7 cm by 4 cm rectangle.
  2. Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm.
  3. Find the volume of a 5 by 2 by 3 cm box.
Check answers

1. 28 square cm. 2. 30 square cm. 3. 30 cubic cm.
