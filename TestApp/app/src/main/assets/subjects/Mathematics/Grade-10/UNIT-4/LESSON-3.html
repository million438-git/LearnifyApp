Linear Inequalities

Inequalities use symbols such as <, >, <=, and >=. Solve them similarly to equations, but reverse the inequality sign when multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number.

Example: -2x > 6 gives x < -3.

Practice

  1. Solve x + 2 > 5.
  2. Solve 3x <= 12.
  3. Solve -x > 4.
Check answers

1. x > 3. 2. x <= 4. 3. x < -4.
