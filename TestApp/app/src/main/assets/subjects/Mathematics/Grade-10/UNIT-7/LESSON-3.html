Mathematics Revision Strategies

Good revision involves understanding methods, practising without looking at solutions, checking mistakes, and returning to difficult questions later.

  • Write each step clearly.
  • Check signs and arithmetic.
  • Include units in measurement answers.
  • Test a solution by substituting it into the original equation.

Practice

  1. Solve 5x = 35.
  2. Factor x^2 - 25.
  3. Find the mean of 5, 7, 9.
Check answers

1. x = 7. 2. (x - 5)(x + 5). 3. 7.
