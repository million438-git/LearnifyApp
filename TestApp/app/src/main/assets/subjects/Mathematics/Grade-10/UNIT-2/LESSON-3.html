Linear Functions and Graphs

A linear function often has the form y = mx + c. The number m is the slope and c is the vertical intercept.

For y = 2x + 1, points (0,1), (1,3), and (2,5) lie on the line. Plot points and connect them with a straight line.

  2. Find y when x = 2 in y = 3x + 2.
