Quadratic Equations

A quadratic equation has the form ax^2 + bx + c = 0, where a is not zero. Methods include factorization and the quadratic formula.

Example: x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 becomes (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0, so x = 2 or x = 3.

Practice

  1. Solve x^2 - 9 = 0.
  2. Factor x^2 + 5x + 6.
  3. How many distinct real solutions does x^2 = 0 have?
Check answers

1. x = -3 or x = 3. 2. (x + 2)(x + 3). 3. One distinct real solution, x = 0.
