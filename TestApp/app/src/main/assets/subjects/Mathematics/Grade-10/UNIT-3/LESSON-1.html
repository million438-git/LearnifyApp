Algebraic Expressions and Polynomials

An algebraic expression combines numbers, variables, and operations. A polynomial has terms with nonnegative integer powers of its variables.

In 3x^2 + 2x - 5, the terms are 3x^2, 2x, and -5. The coefficient of x^2 is 3, and the constant term is -5.

Practice

  2. Evaluate 2x + 3 when x = 4.
  3. What is the constant term in x^2 + 5x - 8?
