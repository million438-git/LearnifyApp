Similarity and Congruence

Congruent shapes have the same size and shape. Similar shapes have the same shape, with corresponding lengths in a constant ratio.

If corresponding sides of similar triangles are 3 cm and 6 cm, the scale factor from the smaller to the larger is 2. Corresponding angles in similar shapes are equal.
