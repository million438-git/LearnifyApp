Linear Equations

A linear equation has a variable with power one. Perform the same operation on both sides to preserve equality.

Example: 3x + 4 = 19. Subtract 4 to get 3x = 15, then divide by 3 to get x = 5.

Practice

  2. Solve 4x = 28.
  3. Solve 2x - 3 = 11.
