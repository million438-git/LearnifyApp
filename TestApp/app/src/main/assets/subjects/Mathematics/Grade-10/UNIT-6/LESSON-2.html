Mean, Median, Mode and Range

Mean = sum of values divided by the number of values. Median is the middle value after sorting. Mode is the most frequent value. Range = maximum minus minimum.

For 2, 3, 3, 6, 11: mean = 5, median = 3, mode = 3, and range = 9.

Practice

  1. Find the mean of 4, 6, 8.
  2. Find the median of 1, 5, 7, 9, 10.
  3. Find the range of 3, 12, 8.
