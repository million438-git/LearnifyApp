Grade 10 Mathematics Final Practice Exam

  1. Solve 3x - 4 = 17.
  2. Factor x^2 - 7x + 12.
  3. Find the mean of 4, 6, 8, 10.
  4. A triangle has angles 45 and 65 degrees. Find the third angle.
  5. Find the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 5 cm.
  6. Find the probability of rolling an odd number on a fair die.
  7. Find the next two terms: 5, 9, 13, 17.
  8. Write 0.00072 in scientific notation.
  9. If f(x) = 3*x + 2, find f(4).
  10. Solve x^2 - 4 = 0.
Suggested answers

1. x = 7. 2. (x - 3)(x - 4). 3. 7. 4. 70 degrees. 5. 30 square cm. 6. 1/2. 7. 21, 25. 8. 7.2 times 10^-4. 9. 14. 10. x = -2 or x = 2.
