Factorization

Factorization expresses an expression as a product. Look first for a common factor. Special identities can also help.

Examples: 6x + 12 = 6(x + 2); x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3). The second example uses the difference of two squares.

Practice

  1. Factor 5x + 15.
  2. Factor x^2 - 16.
  3. Expand (x + 2)(x + 3).
Check answers

1. 5(x + 3). 2. (x - 4)(x + 4). 3. x^2 + 5x + 6.
