Key Ideas

Natural numbers count; whole numbers include zero; integers include negative whole numbers; rational numbers can be written as a/b where a and b are integers and b is not zero.

Worked Example

Simplify 2^3 × 2^2. Since the bases are equal, add the exponents: 2^(3+2) = 2^5 = 32.

Study method: write down the known information, choose the relevant rule, show each calculation, and verify the result.
