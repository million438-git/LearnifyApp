Unit 5 - Geometry and Measurement - Unit Exam

Practice assessment

Learnify | Grade 9 Mathematics

Instructions

Suggested time: 40 minutes. Answer every question. Show your working and include units when needed.

Questions

  1. Define or explain Lines, Angles and Angle Relationships and give a suitable example.
  2. Solve a problem related to Lines, Angles and Angle Relationships. Show all working and check your result.
  3. Define or explain Triangles and Their Properties and give a suitable example.
  4. Solve a problem related to Triangles and Their Properties. Show all working and check your result.
  5. Define or explain Quadrilaterals and Polygons and give a suitable example.
  6. Solve a problem related to Quadrilaterals and Polygons. Show all working and check your result.
  7. Define or explain Perimeter, Area and Volume and give a suitable example.
  8. Solve a problem related to Perimeter, Area and Volume. Show all working and check your result.
  9. Write down two important rules or formulas from this unit and explain when to use them.
  10. Describe one real-life situation where a skill from this unit is useful.

Answer Review Guide

For calculation questions, compare your method with the worked examples in the lesson pages. For explanation questions, include a correct definition, the relevant rule and a suitable example. This guide does not replace a full worked answer key.
