Unit 3 - Equations and Inequalities

Grade 9 Mathematics - Unit overview

Learnify | Grade 9 Mathematics

Unit Overview

Solving linear equations, translating word problems, working with inequalities and checking solutions.

Worked Example

Solve 3x + 5 = 20. Subtract 5 to get 3x = 15. Divide by 3: x = 5. Check: 3(5)+5 = 20.
