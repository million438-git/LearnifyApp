Key Ideas

A frequency table records how often each value or category occurs. Check that the frequencies add up to the total number of observations.

Worked Example

For data 2, 4, 4, 6, 9, the mean is 25/5 = 5, the median is 4, and the mode is 4.

Study method: write down the known information, choose the relevant rule, show each calculation, and verify the result.
