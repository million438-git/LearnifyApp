Unit 1 - Number Systems

Grade 9 Mathematics - Unit overview

Learnify | Grade 9 Mathematics

Unit Overview

Classifying real numbers, comparing values, working with powers and roots, and using scientific notation.

Worked Example

Simplify 2^3 × 2^2. Since the bases are equal, add the exponents: 2^(3+2) = 2^5 = 32.

Lessons in This Unit

  • Natural, Whole, Integer and Rational Numbers
  • Irrational and Real Numbers
  • Comparing and Ordering Real Numbers
  • Powers, Roots and Scientific Notation
