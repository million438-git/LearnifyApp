Grade 9 Mathematics - Final Exam

Cumulative practice assessment

Learnify | Grade 9 Mathematics

Instructions

Suggested time: 90 minutes. Answer all questions, show your working, and review your answers before finishing.

Questions

  1. Number Systems: Classify -4, 0, 7 and 3/5. Explain why 0.25 is rational.
  2. Explain an important concept from Number Systems and show a worked example.
  3. Relations and Functions: Plot (2,3), (-1,2) and (0,-2). State the coordinates of a point four units right and one unit down from the origin.
  4. Explain an important concept from Relations and Functions and show a worked example.
  5. Equations and Inequalities: Simplify 4x + 3x - 2. Simplify 2a + 5b - a + b.
  6. Explain an important concept from Equations and Inequalities and show a worked example.
  7. Polynomials and Factorization: Identify the number of terms in 5x, x+3 and x²+2x+1. State the degree of 4x³+2x.
  8. Explain an important concept from Polynomials and Factorization and show a worked example.
  9. Geometry and Measurement: Find the supplement of 65°. Find the angle around a point if the other angles are 90°, 120° and 80°.
  10. Explain an important concept from Geometry and Measurement and show a worked example.
  11. Coordinate Geometry and Graphs: Plot (-2,1), (3,0), and (1,-3). State which point lies on the x-axis.
  12. Explain an important concept from Coordinate Geometry and Graphs and show a worked example.
  13. Statistics and Data Handling: Design a question to collect data about students' travel-to-school methods. Identify whether the answers are numerical or categorical.
  14. Explain an important concept from Statistics and Data Handling and show a worked example.
  15. Probability and Problem Solving: List the sample space for tossing one coin. List the possible outcomes when rolling a standard die.
  16. Explain an important concept from Probability and Problem Solving and show a worked example.
  17. A student gets an incorrect answer. Describe how you would check the working and find the error.
  18. Choose a real-life problem and explain how mathematics can help solve it.

Marking Guidance

Give clear steps, correct formulas, accurate calculations and explanations. For questions requiring examples, include both the example and its solution. Use the lesson pages to review any topic you find difficult.
