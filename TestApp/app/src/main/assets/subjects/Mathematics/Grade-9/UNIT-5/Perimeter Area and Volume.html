Perimeter, Area and Volume

Unit 5 - Geometry and Measurement - Lesson

Learnify | Grade 9 Mathematics

Learning Objectives

  • Explain the main idea of Perimeter, Area and Volume.
  • Use the relevant mathematical rules accurately.
  • Show working and check your answer.

Key Ideas

Rectangle area = length × width; triangle area = 1/2 × base × height; rectangular-prism volume = length × width × height. Include units in answers.

Worked Example

A triangle has angles 50° and 60°. Its third angle is 180° - 50° - 60° = 70°.

Study method: write down the known information, choose the relevant rule, show each calculation, and verify the result.

Practice Questions

  2. Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm
