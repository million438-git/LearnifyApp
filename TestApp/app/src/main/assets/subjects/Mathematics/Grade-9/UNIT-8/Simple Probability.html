Key Ideas

For equally likely outcomes, probability = number of favorable outcomes divided by total possible outcomes. Probability lies between 0 and 1.

Worked Example

For a fair six-sided die, P(rolling a 4) = 1/6 because one of the six equally likely outcomes is 4.

Study method: write down the known information, choose the relevant rule, show each calculation, and verify the result.
