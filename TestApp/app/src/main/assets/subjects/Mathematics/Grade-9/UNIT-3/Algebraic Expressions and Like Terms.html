Key Ideas

A term combines a coefficient and variables. Like terms have the same variable part and exponents. Only like terms can be combined by addition or subtraction.

Worked Example

Solve 3x + 5 = 20. Subtract 5 to get 3x = 15. Divide by 3: x = 5. Check: 3(5)+5 = 20.

Study method: write down the known information, choose the relevant rule, show each calculation, and verify the result.
