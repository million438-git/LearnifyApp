Core Formulas — Prism Dispersion

  • Snell's Law (first face): n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂
  • Snell's Law (second face): n sin θ₃ = 1 · sin θ₄
  • Inside angle: θ₃ = A − θ₂ (where A is the apex angle)
  • Total deviation: δ = θ₁ + θ₄ − A
  • Minimum deviation: n = sin[(A + δ_min)/2] / sin(A/2)
  • Dispersion: Δδ = δ_violet − δ_red
  • Cauchy's equation: n(λ) = A + B/λ² (empirical)
  • Critical angle: sin θ_c = 1/n (internal → air)

💡 Quick tips: A prism spreads white light into a rainbow because n depends on wavelength — blue bends more than red. Violet light has a higher n (~1.52) than red (~1.51) in glass, so violet deviates more. Minimum deviation occurs when θ₁ = θ₄ (symmetric path). The apex angle A together with the deviation δ determines n via the prism formula. Diamonds sparkle because their very high n (2.42) gives a small critical angle (~24°), trapping light in total internal reflection.
