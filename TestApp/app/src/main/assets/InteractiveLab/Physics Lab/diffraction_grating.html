Core Formulas — Diffraction Grating

  • Grating equation: d·sin θ = m·λ
  • Slit spacing: d = 1 / (lines per meter)
  • Order angle: θ_m = arcsin(m·λ / d)
  • Screen position: y_m = L · tan θ_m
  • Max order: m_max = floor(d / λ)
  • Angular dispersion: dθ/dλ = m / (d·cos θ)
  • Resolving power: R = m·N

💡 Key insights: A diffraction grating has thousands of narrow parallel slits. Light passing through adjacent slits interferes constructively only at specific angles: d·sin θ = m·λ, where d is the slit spacing and m is the order (0, 1, 2, ...). The zero-order (m=0) is always straight through. Higher orders spread wider. Different wavelengths (colors) diffract at different angles — this is how a grating splits white light into a spectrum (and how a spectrometer works).
