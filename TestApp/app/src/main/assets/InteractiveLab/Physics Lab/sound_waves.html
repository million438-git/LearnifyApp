Core Formulas — Sound Waves

  • Wave speed: v = f · λ
  • Period: T = 1/f
  • Angular frequency: ω = 2πf
  • Wave number: k = 2π/λ
  • Wave equation: y(x,t) = A·sin(kx − ωt)
  • Doppler (moving source): f_obs = f · v / (v ∓ v_s)
  • Beat frequency: f_beat = |f₁ − f₂|
  • Air speed vs temperature: v ≈ 331 + 0.6·T(°C) m/s

💡 Quick tips: Sound travels ~343 m/s in room-temperature air, 4× faster in water, 17× faster in steel. Frequency is set by the source and does not change when sound enters a new medium — but wavelength and speed change (λ = v/f). Pitch = frequency, loudness ∝ log(intensity).
