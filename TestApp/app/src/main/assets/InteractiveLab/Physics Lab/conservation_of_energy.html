Core Formulas — Conservation of Energy

  • Kinetic energy: KE = ½mv²
  • Gravitational PE: PE = mgh
  • Spring PE: PE_s = ½kx²
  • Conservation: KE + PE + PE_s + Thermal = constant
  • Without friction: KE_initial + PE_initial = KE_final + PE_final
  • Velocity from height drop: v = √(2gh)
  • Height from velocity: h = v²/(2g)
  • Thermal energy: E_thermal = f · d (friction × distance)
  • Power: P = F·v = dE/dt

💡 Key insights: Energy is never created or destroyed — it only changes form. In a frictionless system, KE + PE = constant. When the object falls, PE converts to KE. When it rises, KE converts back to PE. Friction converts mechanical energy to thermal (heat) energy, which is why real systems eventually stop. The total energy (including thermal) is always conserved.
