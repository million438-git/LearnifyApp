Core Formulas — AC Circuits

  • Inductive reactance: XL = 2πfL
  • Capacitive reactance: XC = 1 / (2πfC)
  • Impedance: |Z| = √(R² + (XL − XC)²)
  • RMS current: I = V / |Z|
  • Phase angle: φ = arctan((XL − XC) / R)
  • Real power: P = V·I·cos φ
  • Reactive power: Q = V·I·sin φ
  • Series resonance: f₀ = 1 / (2π√(LC))
  • RMS from peak: Vrms = Vpeak / √2

💡 Quick tips: At resonance XL = XC, Z is minimum (= R), and current is maximum. Increasing f raises XL and lowers XC. In a purely inductive circuit, current lags voltage by 90°; in a purely capacitive circuit, current leads by 90°.
