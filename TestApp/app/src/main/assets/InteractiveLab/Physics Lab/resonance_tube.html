Core Formulas — Resonance in Air Columns

  • Speed of sound in air: v = 331 + 0.6·T(°C) m/s
  • Wavelength: λ = v / f
  • Closed tube (one end closed): L = (2n−1)·λ/4 for n = 1, 2, 3, ...
  • Closed tube frequencies: f_n = (2n−1)·v/(4L) — only ODD harmonics
  • Open tube (both ends open): L = n·λ/2 for n = 1, 2, 3, ...
  • Open tube frequencies: f_n = n·v/(2L) — ALL harmonics
  • End correction: e ≈ 0.6·r (r = tube radius)
  • Successive resonance spacing: λ/2 (closed tube)

💡 Quick tips: A closed tube (one end capped) resonates at odd multiples of its fundamental — f₁, 3f₁, 5f₁, ... An open tube resonates at all integer multiples — f₁, 2f₁, 3f₁, ... Standing waves have nodes (zero displacement) and antinodes (maximum displacement). A closed end is always a displacement node; an open end is an antinode.
