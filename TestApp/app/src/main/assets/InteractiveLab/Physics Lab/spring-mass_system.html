FULL FORMULAS — Spring–Mass System

  1. Hooke’s Law (Restoring Force): F = -k x
    Force always pulls back to equilibrium.
  2. Equation of Motion: m d²x/dt² + k x = 0
    Governs simple harmonic oscillation.
  3. Angular frequency: ω = √(k / m)
  4. Time period: T = 2π √(m / k)
  5. Frequency: f = 1 / T
  6. Displacement: x(t) = A cos(ω t + φ)
  7. Velocity: v(t) = −A ω sin(ω t + φ)
  8. Acceleration: a(t) = −ω² x
  9. Maximum velocity: v_max = A ω
  10. Maximum acceleration: a_max = A ω²
  11. Elastic potential energy: PE = 1/2 k x²
  12. Kinetic energy: KE = 1/2 m v²
  13. Total energy (ideal): E = 1/2 k A²
  14. Damped motion (advanced): x(t) = A e^{-b t} cos(ω t)
    Motion amplitude decays over time when damping present.
  15. Newton: F = m a
