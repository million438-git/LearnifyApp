Core Formulas — Thin Lens

  • Thin lens equation: 1/f = 1/u + 1/v
  • Magnification: m = -v/u = hᵢ/h₀
  • Image distance: v = uf/(u − f)
  • Magnification: m = f/(f − u)
  • Power of lens: P = 1/f (in diopters, f in metres)
  • Diverging lens: f is negative; image always virtual, upright, reduced

Sign convention: u is positive (object on the left). For converging lenses f > 0. Real images have v > 0 (on the right) and m < 0 (inverted). Virtual images have v < 0 and m > 0 (upright).
