Core Formulas — Special Relativity

  • Lorentz factor: γ = 1 / √(1 − v²/c²)
  • Time dilation: Δt = γ · Δt₀
  • Length contraction: L = L₀ / γ
  • Relativistic mass: m = γ · m₀
  • Rest energy: E₀ = m₀ · c²
  • Total energy: E = γ · m₀ · c²
  • Kinetic energy: K = (γ − 1) · m₀ · c²
  • Momentum: p = γ · m₀ · v
  • Energy-momentum relation: E² = (pc)² + (m₀c²)²
  • Velocity addition: u = (u' + v) / (1 + u'v/c²)

💡 Quick tips: As v → c, γ → ∞ — nothing with mass can reach c. Time dilation is symmetric: each observer sees the other's clock running slow. At v = 0.866c, γ = 2 (time runs half speed). At v = 0.995c, γ ≈ 10. Length contracts only in the direction of motion. c = 299,792,458 m/s exactly.
