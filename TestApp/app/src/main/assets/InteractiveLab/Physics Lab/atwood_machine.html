Core Formulas — Atwood Machine

  • Acceleration: a = g(m₂ − m₁)/(m₁ + m₂)
  • Tension: T = 2m₁m₂g/(m₁ + m₂)
  • Force on m₁: T − m₁g = m₁a (upward)
  • Force on m₂: m₂g − T = m₂a (downward)
  • Speed after distance d: v² = 2ad
  • Kinetic energy: KE = ½(m₁ + m₂)v²
  • Net work: W = (m₂ − m₁)g·d

💡 Key insights: The Atwood machine demonstrates Newton's second law with two connected masses over a pulley. The heavier mass accelerates downward, the lighter mass accelerates upward, and both move with the same magnitude of acceleration. The tension is the same throughout the string (ideal pulley). When masses are equal, a = 0 and T = mg — the system balances.
