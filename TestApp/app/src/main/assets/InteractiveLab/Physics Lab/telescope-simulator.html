Core Formulas — Telescopes

  • Angular magnification: M = f_obj / f_eye
  • Tube length (refracting): L = f_obj + f_eye
  • Tube length (reflecting): L ≈ f_obj (with flat secondary)
  • Focal ratio: f/# = f_obj / D
  • Resolving power (Rayleigh): θ_min = 1.22 λ / D
  • Light-gathering area: A = π (D/2)²
  • Limiting magnitude: m_lim ≈ 2 + 5·log₁₀(D in mm)
  • Angular size viewed: θ' = M · θ_original

💡 Quick tips: Longer f_obj and shorter f_eye give higher magnification. Larger D collects more light AND gives finer resolution — which is why bigger telescopes see fainter, sharper objects. Optical telescopes record intensity, wavelength, and position — not physical detail beyond the diffraction limit.
