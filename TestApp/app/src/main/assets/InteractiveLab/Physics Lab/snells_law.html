Core Formulas — Refraction

  • Snell's Law: n₁ · sin(θ₁) = n₂ · sin(θ₂)
  • Refraction angle: θ₂ = arcsin[(n₁/n₂) · sin θ₁]
  • Refractive index: n = c / v (c = speed of light in vacuum)
  • Critical angle: sin θ_c = n₂ / n₁ (when n₁ > n₂)
  • Speed of light in medium: v = c / n
  • Wavelength in medium: λ_n = λ_vacuum / n
  • Total internal reflection: θ₁ > θ_c → no transmitted ray

💡 Quick tips: Light bends toward the normal when entering a denser medium (higher n). Light bends away from the normal when entering a less dense medium. When going from a denser to a less-dense medium, above the critical angle the light reflects back entirely (total internal reflection) — this is how fiber optics work.
