Core Formulas — Projectile Motion

  • Trajectory: y = x·tan(θ) − (g·x²) / (2·v₀²·cos²θ)
  • Time of flight: T = 2·v₀·sin(θ) / g
  • Maximum height: H = v₀²·sin²(θ) / (2·g)
  • Range: R = v₀²·sin(2θ) / g
  • Horizontal: x = v₀·cos(θ)·t
  • Vertical: y = v₀·sin(θ)·t − ½·g·t²
  • Velocity components: vₓ = v₀·cos(θ), v_y = v₀·sin(θ) − g·t

Quick tips: 45° gives max range (no drag). Doubling v₀ → range ×4.
