Core Formulas — Pendulum

  • Time period: T = 2π√(L/g)
  • Frequency: f = 1 / T
  • Angular frequency: ω = √(g / L)
  • Displacement (SHM): θ(t) = θ₀ cos(ω t)
  • Max velocity: vmax = ω A
  • Energy: PE = m g h, KE = ½ m v²

Notes: small-angle approximation (θ < ~15°) gives ideal SHM and the simple T expression.
