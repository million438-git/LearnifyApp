Core Formulas — Thermal Expansion

  • Linear expansion: ΔL = α · L₀ · ΔT
  • New length: L = L₀ (1 + α · ΔT)
  • Area expansion: ΔA = 2α · A₀ · ΔT
  • Volumetric expansion: ΔV = 3α · V₀ · ΔT (isotropic solids)
  • Liquid expansion: ΔV = β_liquid · V₀ · ΔT
  • Bimetallic bending: curvature ∝ (α₂ − α₁) · ΔT

💡 Quick tips: Most metals expand about 0.001–0.002% per °C. Invar (α = 1.2×10⁻⁶) barely changes — used in precision instruments.
