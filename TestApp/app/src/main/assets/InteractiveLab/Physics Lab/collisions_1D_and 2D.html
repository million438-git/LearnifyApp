Core formulas
Momentum: p = m·v (vector) · Kinetic energy: KE = ½mv²
Conservation: m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁′ + m₂v₂′ (each of x, y, z)
Restitution: e = (v₂′ − v₁′)/(u₁ − u₂) — e = 1 elastic, e = 0 stick together
1D result: v₁′ = [m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ + m₂e(u₂ − u₁)]/(m₁+m₂), v₂′ = [m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ + m₁e(u₁ − u₂)]/(m₁+m₂)
Momentum is always conserved with no external force. KE is lost to heat, sound and deformation unless e = 1. Model limits: point masses, no spin, no friction.
