Core Formulas — RC Circuits

  • Time constant: τ = R · C
  • Charging — voltage: V_C(t) = V₀ · (1 − e−t/τ)
  • Charging — current: I(t) = (V₀/R) · e−t/τ
  • Discharging — voltage: V_C(t) = V₀ · e−t/τ
  • Discharging — current: I(t) = (V₀/R) · e−t/τ
  • Charge: Q = C · V_C
  • Energy stored: E = ½ · C · V²
  • Resistor voltage: V_R = I · R (charges linear with current)

💡 Quick tips: At t = τ, the capacitor reaches 63% of V₀. At t = 5τ, it's considered fully charged (99.3%). The current starts maximum and decays exponentially. Doubling R or C doubles τ — the capacitor charges twice as slowly. Discharging is the mirror image: V_C drops to 37% at t = τ.
