Core Formulas — Faraday's Law

  • Faraday's law: ε = −N · dΦ/dt
  • Magnetic flux: Φ = B · A · cos θ
  • Rotating coil: ε = N·B·A·ω · sin(ωt)
  • Peak EMF: ε₀ = N·B·A·ω
  • Motional EMF: ε = B · L · v
  • Current: I = ε / R
  • Lenz's law: Induced current opposes the change in flux
  • Force on rod: F = B·I·L
  • Mechanical power: P_m = F·v = I²R = P_electrical

💡 Key insights: Faraday's law states that a changing magnetic flux through a loop induces an EMF. This is the principle behind electric generators, transformers, and induction cooktops. Lenz's law says the induced current flows in the direction that OPPOSES the change in flux — a manifestation of conservation of energy. When the coil rotates in a magnetic field, the flux changes sinusoidally, creating AC current. When a rod slides on rails, motional EMF is generated.
