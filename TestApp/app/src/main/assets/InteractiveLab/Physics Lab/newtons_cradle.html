Core Formulas — Newton's Cradle

  • Pendulum period: T = 2π√(L/g)
  • Potential energy: PE = mgh = mgL(1 − cosθ)
  • Kinetic energy: KE = ½mv²
  • Speed at bottom: v = √(2gh)
  • Max speed from angle: v = √(2gL(1 − cosθ))
  • Momentum: p = mv
  • Elastic collision (equal mass): velocities swap
  • Momentum conservation: m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = m₁v₁' + m₂v₂'
  • Energy conservation: KE + PE = constant (if e = 1)

💡 Key insights: Newton's cradle demonstrates conservation of momentum and conservation of kinetic energy through a chain of equal-mass elastic collisions. When you lift one ball and release it, the collision cascades through the middle balls and one ball on the other end shoots out at almost the same speed. Lift two and two fly out. This works because in a perfectly elastic collision between equal masses, the velocities simply exchange. Real cradles have restitution e < 1, so the motion slowly dies down.
