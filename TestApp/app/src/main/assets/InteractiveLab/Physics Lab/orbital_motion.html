Core Formulas — Orbital Mechanics

  • Newton's gravity: F = G·M·m / r²
  • Circular orbital speed: v_circ = √(GM/r)
  • Escape velocity: v_esc = √(2GM/r) = √2 · v_circ
  • Orbital period: T = 2π·√(r³/GM) (Kepler's 3rd law)
  • Specific orbital energy: ε = v²/2 − GM/r
  • Specific angular momentum: h = r × v (perpendicular component)
  • Eccentricity: e = √(1 + 2εh²/(GM)²)
  • Periapsis / Apoapsis: r_p = h²/(GM(1+e)); r_a = h²/(GM(1−e))

💡 Quick tips: An orbit is a continuous free-fall — the satellite is always being pulled toward the central body, but its tangential speed keeps it from falling in. If v = v_circ, you get a circular orbit. If v_circ < v < v_esc, you get an ellipse. If v ≥ v_esc, the orbit becomes a parabola (or hyperbola) and the satellite escapes. If v < v_circ, the satellite falls into a lower orbit or crashes.
