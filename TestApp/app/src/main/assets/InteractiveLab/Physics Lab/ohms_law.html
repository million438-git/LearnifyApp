Core Formulas — Ohm's Law & Kirchhoff

  • Ohm's law: V = I·R
  • Current: I = V/R
  • Resistance: R = V/I
  • Power (electrical): P = V·I = I²·R = V²/R
  • Series resistance: R_total = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + …
  • Parallel resistance: 1/R_total = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ + …
  • Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL): ΣI_in = ΣI_out at any node
  • Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL): ΣV = 0 around any closed loop

💡 Quick tips: In a series circuit, current is the same through every component and voltage divides. In parallel, voltage is the same across every branch and current divides. Kirchhoff's laws are the two rules that let you solve any complex circuit — KCL enforces conservation of charge at each node, KVL enforces conservation of energy around each loop.
