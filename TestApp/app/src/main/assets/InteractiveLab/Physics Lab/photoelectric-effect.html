Einstein's Photoelectric Equations

  • Photon energy: E = hf = hc/λ
  • Photoelectric equation: KE_max = hf − φ
  • Stopping potential: eV₀ = KE_max = hf − φ
  • Threshold frequency: f₀ = φ/h
  • Threshold wavelength: λ₀ = hc/φ
  • Photoelectric condition: f > f₀ (or λ < λ₀) for emission
  • Electron speed: v = √(2·KE/m)

💡 Key insight: Increasing light intensity increases the number of electrons emitted (current), but NOT their max kinetic energy — that depends only on frequency. Increasing frequency increases KE_max. If f < f₀, no emission occurs regardless of intensity.
