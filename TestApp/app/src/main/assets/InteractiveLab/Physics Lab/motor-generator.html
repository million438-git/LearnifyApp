Core Formulas — Motor & Generator

  • Force on wire (Lorentz): F = B·I·L
  • Torque on coil: τ = N·B·I·A·sin(θ)
  • Motor EMF (back-EMF): ε_b = N·B·A·ω·sin(ωt)
  • Generator EMF: ε = N·B·A·ω·sin(ωt)
  • Peak EMF: ε₀ = N·B·A·ω
  • Motor equation: V = ε_b + I·R
  • Flux: Φ = N·B·A·cos(ωt)
  • Mechanical power: P_m = τ·ω
  • Electrical power: P_e = ε·I

💡 Key insights: A motor and a generator are the same device — just viewed differently. In motor mode, current through the coil in a magnetic field creates torque, spinning the armature. As it spins, it acts like a generator, producing a back-EMF that opposes the applied voltage. In generator mode, external rotation spins the coil, changing flux and inducing EMF. Real motors have a commutator that keeps torque unidirectional.
